## Commentary
The Levites were set apart to serve around the tabernacle. Their work included assisting Aaron and his sons, caring for the tabernacle, and guarding what was holy.

Nearness to God is never treated casually in Scripture. The Levites were brought close, but that nearness came with responsibility. The holy things of God had to be handled according to God’s command. This same theme appears throughout the law: the Levites were appointed to guard the tabernacle so wrath would not fall on the congregation (==Numbers 1:50–53==), and later God reminds Aaron and the Levites that priestly service was a gift, but also a holy responsibility (==Numbers 18:1–7==).

This teaches us that the things of God should never be handled carelessly. Worship is not performance. Ministry is not a platform. Service before the Lord requires reverence, obedience, and holy fear (==Hebrews 12:28–29==).
