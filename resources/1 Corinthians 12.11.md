## Commentary
“But one and the same Spirit works all these things, distributing to each one individually as He wills.”

Two truths in one verse. First, the distribution is personal: “to each one individually.” There are no ungifted believers. If the Spirit lives in you, He has handed you something. Second, the distribution is sovereign: “as He wills.” You didn’t choose your gift, you didn’t apply for it, and you didn’t earn it. He decided. That frees you from two diseases at once—envy, because someone else’s gift was never on the table for you, and pride, because yours was never a reward for something you've done. A gift is not an achievement or an identity. It’s an assignment.

And notice *where* He distributes. Every gift lands inside a body, “for the profit of all” (==1 Corinthians 12:7==). The Spirit gives His gifts into a family and for a family. Which means the local church isn’t just where gifts get used—it’s where they’re meant to be birthed, tested, and matured, surrounded by the love, relationship, and accountability that something as precious as a gift of God requires.

Imagine what it looks like when gifts stay rooted in a local family. Anointed worshipers who reject the magnetic pull of the stage. Evangelists who refuse to leave their community when revival breaks out somewhere else. Intercessors who weep night after night over promises God has made to their church family that haven’t yet come to pass. Entrepreneurs who could make more money in a larger market but know there are still jobless and homeless people in their own town. That’s not wasted gifting—that’s gifting operating exactly as He wills.

## Application
Plug into the church family. Don’t graze on the fringes when it’s convenient to attend—commit to being part of the body, and expect the Holy Spirit to give gifts to His church. Then steward what He hands you where He handed it to you: ask the people who know you what they see the Spirit doing through you, and put the gift to work serving them. The Giver decides what you carry. The family is where you carry it.
