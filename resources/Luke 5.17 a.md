## Commentary
When we think of healing, we imagine someone being prayed for. But this passage shows us that healing can flow from teaching, as well. "...As He was teaching...the power of the Lord was present to heal them." As long as He is present, He is present to heal. And as long as we are with Him in faith, the power of the Lord will be present to heal.
