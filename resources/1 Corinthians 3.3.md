## Commentary
This is a scathing indictment against a church that excelled in the spiritual gifts (see 1 Corinthians 1:7). Their abilities to function as a spiritual people were, in some ways, a source of identity and pride. Yet, Paul tells them here that because of their divisions and jealousies they are actually not spiritual at all. They are mere "humans" (anthrōpos). If they truly were people of the Spirit, there would be fruit of the Spirit growing in their midst. The fact that "deeds of the flesh" (as Paul writes in Galatians 5:19ff) are being produced is evidence of their carnality. It is the goodness of the Lord to reveal the true state of the church, not to shame us, but to correct us for the goal of maturing us.
