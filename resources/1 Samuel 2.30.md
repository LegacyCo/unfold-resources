## Commentary
“Therefore the LORD God of Israel says: ‘I said indeed that your house and the house of your father would walk before Me forever.’ But now the LORD says: ‘Far be it from Me; for those who honor Me I will honor, and those who despise Me shall be lightly esteemed.’”

A man of God delivers this word to Eli after years of corruption in his house. The previous promise to Eli's family line was real: “I said indeed that your house… would walk before Me forever.” But the promise assumed a priesthood that treated God as God, and the condition was clear: those who honor Me I will honor, and those who despise Me shall be lightly esteemed. 

This chapter illustrates both halves of that promise. Hannah, who honored God with her vow and gave back her only son, is honored—her song opens the chapter, and the LORD visits her with five more children (==1 Samuel 2:21==). Hophni and Phinehas, who treated the offering of the LORD as a light thing (==1 Samuel 2:17==), die on the same day (==1 Samuel 2:34==; ==1 Samuel 4:11==), and the family that mocked God with their behavior ends with a grandson named Ichabod—“the glory has departed” (==1 Samuel 4:21==).

God gives people what they chose. A life that treats the God of glory as insignificant becomes insignificant; a life that gives Him His true glory receives glory it could never generate on its own (==John 12:26==; ==Matthew 10:32==). 

## Word Study
**Lightly esteemed** is the Hebrew *qalal* (קָלַל)—to consider insignificant, to be light, of little weight; the root also underlies the vocabulary of cursing, because to curse someone is to declare them weightless. Its opposite is *kabed*, to be heavy or weighty—the root of *kabod*, glory. So the verse is built on a scale: honor Me (give Me weight) and I will give you weight; treat Me as light and you will become light. The same wordplay closes Eli's story—*Ichabod*, no *kabod*, no glory, no weight left in the house (==1 Samuel 4:21==). Summary: glory in Scripture is weight, and every life takes on the weight of whatever it treats as weighty.
