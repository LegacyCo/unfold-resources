## Commentary
“But now” is one of the great turns in the book of Romans. After Paul has shown the guilt of Gentile and Jew alike, he announces the good news: the righteousness we could not produce has now been revealed in Jesus Christ. 

But Paul is careful to say this righteousness is not disconnected from the Old Testament. It is “witnessed to by the Law and the Prophets.” In other words, the gospel is not God’s backup plan. It is the fulfillment of everything Scripture had been pointing toward. Abraham believed God and it was counted to him as righteousness. David spoke of the blessedness of the one whose sins are forgiven. Isaiah spoke of the Servant who would bear the iniquities of many. The Old Covenant was always point to the day when righteousness would come to all who believe.

The New Covenant doesn't lower the standard of attaining righteousness. It locates righteousness in the Man, Christ Jesus. The sinner is now justified because Jesus has become the place where mercy and justice meet.
