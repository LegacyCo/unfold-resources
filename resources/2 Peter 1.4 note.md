## Commentary
God desires to make us like Him. We are image bearers (imago dei). He is so humble and gracious to bestow on us His own nature. The Greek word that we translate "nature" is physis. Physis means the attributes that pertain to the origin of something, the source.

In the ESV, there are two cross references mentioned for this verse:
	- Ephesians 4:24 - "put on the new man which was created after the likeness of God in true righteousness and holiness."
		- The "new man", the new life of the born again believer, is now the imago dei. We are image bearers of God. We walk in true righteousness and holiness. Any idea that we are less than the righteousness and holiness of God is illegal, and an insult to the total victory Jesus accomplished on the cross.
	- Hebrews 12:10 - "For they disciplined us for a short time as seemed best to them, but He for our good, that we may be partakers of His holiness."
		- God is committed to us being holy
