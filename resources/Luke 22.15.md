## Commentary
This is from "Come to the Table" by John Mark Hicks: Instead of the Last Supper standing aloof from the other meals mentioned by Luke, it gives fuller meaning to them. The Last Supper is one meal among many, but it is also the paradigmatic meal for understanding the rest of the meals. The Last Supper interprets and gives substance to the other meals as they are understood in light of the Last Supper.
