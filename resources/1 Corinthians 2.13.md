## Commentary
A better translation of the end of this verse is, "...joining together Spirit revealed truths with Spirit revealed words." The Holy Spirit teaches us about God: His ways, what His kingdom is like, His character, His will, what it sounds like when He is speaking to us, the kind of words He uses, etc. Jesus said in John 10:27, "My sheep know My voice, and they follow Me...and no one will snatch them out of My hand." As we learn what it sounds like when God is speaking to us, we simultaneously learn what other voices sound like; our own voice, the enemy, the world, etc. The Holy Spirit helps us discern the many voices vying for our attention.

## Cross Reference
