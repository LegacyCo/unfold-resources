## Commentary
Jesus entered the glory of the mountain through prayer. The transfiguration happened in the place of communion with the Father.

Jesus knew how to enter significant moments with prayer. Before public ministry, He prayed (==Luke 3:21-22==). Before choosing the twelve, He prayed (==Luke 6:12-13==). Before the cross, He prayed (==Luke 22:39-46==). And here, before the glory of His identity was revealed on the mountain, He prayed.

Special assignments require special communion. Special temptations require special dependence. Special suffering requires special wrestling. And special glory requires special surrender.

Jesus shows us that prayer is not preparation for the real work. Prayer is the place where the real work begins.

Only in prayer do we learn how to face the enemy.  
Only in prayer do we receive strength for our vocation.  
Only in prayer do we find grace to drink the cup.  
Only in prayer do we become able to carry glory without being destroyed by it.

The mountain was a place of revelation, but it was first a place of prayer.

## Application
If Jesus Himself entered the great moments of His life through communion with the Father, we should stop pretending we can enter ours any other way.
