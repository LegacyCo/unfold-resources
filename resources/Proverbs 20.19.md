## Commentary
 If you have animosity towards an individual, you will attract misinformation about them, because you need it to justify what you feel towards them. 

## Application
When your heart is already turned against someone, be slow to believe what you hear about them. Offense makes gossip feel believable, because it feeds the case you've already built. Before you receive a negative report, bring it before the Lord. Ask yourself, "Do I want truth here, or ammunition?"
