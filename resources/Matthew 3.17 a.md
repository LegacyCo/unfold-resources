## Commentary
The Father's declaration over the Son draws together the *royal* vocation of the Messiah in Psalm 2:7, "...The LORD has said to Me, ‘You are My Son, today I have begotten You,'" and His *servant* vocation of Isaiah 42:1, "Behold! My Servant whom I uphold, My Elect One in whom My soul delights! I have put My Spirit upon Him; He will bring forth justice to the Gentiles."

Jesus comes to show us what royalty in His kingdom looks like. In His Kingdom, kings serve: they wash feet, they embody grace and mercy, and ultimately, they lay their lives down for others.

------

Another way to translate "well pleased" is to take delight in.
