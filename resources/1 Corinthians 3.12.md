## Commentary
Bob Gladstone writes this in "A Time To Build": We rarely link human traditions to defilement, but God does. He wants His house built with eternal materials...people who have become family. When we add our own customs and methods to His wisdom, we pollute His house. We assume that God accepts as standard what we have defined as standard. But He doesn't. He is jealous over His house and cares deeply what materials we use to build it.
