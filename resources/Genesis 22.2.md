## Commentary
The test for Abraham took place on a very specific piece of real estate: a mountain in the land of Moriah. Some notable occurrences on this mountain:

-Mount Moriah is where the angel of the Lord relented when Israel was being judged for David's sin (1 Chronicles 21:15). David built an alter on that spot on Mount Moriah and the Lord answered by fire. 
-Years later, Solomon built the temple on that exact spot, "Now Solomon began to build the house of the LORD at Jerusalem on Mount Moriah where the LORD had appeared to his father David, at the place that David had prepared on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite" )2 Chronicles 3:1).
-Mount Moriah was, and is, the the center point of God's dealings with His people...it became the city of David, Zion, Jerusalem, the epicenter around which the gravitational pull of our faith orbits. 

Before it became the city where the Lamb of God was slain, it first had to be the place where a lamb of a different sort was slain.
