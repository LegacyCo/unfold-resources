## Commentary
David declares "this is the house of the Lord God." What does he mean by "this?" There was not yet a building, there was nothing there but a threshing floor that David had bought from Ornan (==1 Chronicles 21:24==). What was there was the fire of God. David had built an altar on the spot where the Lord's hand relented from His people (==1 Chronicles 21:28==). God answered him from heaven by fire (v26). There was something in David that knew it was the presence and the holy fire of God touching earth that gave a piece of dirt the privilege of being called the "house of God."
