## Commentary
After the tabernacle was fully set up, anointed, and consecrated, the leaders of Israel brought offerings before the Lord. These were the heads of the ancestral houses, the men responsible for numbering and representing the tribes. Their first recorded act after the tabernacle was established worship.

Leadership among the people of God begins with offering. The leaders in Numbers personally brought something costly to the Lord. They gave six covered carts and twelve oxen to assist the Levites in carrying the tabernacle. Their worship became practical support for the work of God’s house.

There is something important here. True worship is not only expressed in songs. It also takes the form of making the work of God possible. These leaders gave what helped the Levites do what God had entrusted them to do. Their offering strengthened the burden-bearers.

That's a picture of faithful leadership we don't often get to see. Godly leaders bring what they have to the Lord so the burden of ministry can be shared.
