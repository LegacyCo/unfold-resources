## Commentary
“Now we have received, not the spirit of the world, but the Spirit who is from God, that we might know the things that have been freely given to us by God.”

The eye has not seen and the ear has not heard what God has prepared for those who love Him—but the sentence does not stop there: “God has revealed them to us through His Spirit. For the Spirit searches all things, yes, the deep things of God” (==1 Corinthians 2:9-10==). Only the Spirit of God knows the things of God (==1 Corinthians 2:11==)—and this verse says that same Spirit has been given to us, for the stated purpose that we might *know* what God has freely given. The Holy Spirit is the believer's guide into the depths of the heart of God, and there is no bottom to those depths. He is endless and inexhaustible. As Jesus said, “it has been given to you to know the mysteries of the kingdom” (==Matthew 13:11==).

Consider what is still unexplored. What does He have for a generation that will seek Him like no other generation has? What ideas and inventions have yet to be discovered because no one has opened that door? What paintings have yet to be painted, what stories written, what problems in the world remain unsolved because the solution is still tucked away in the heart of God, waiting for someone the Spirit can lead there? You know as much of God as you want to know. The Spirit who searches the deep things has taken up residence in you, and His whole assignment in this verse is disclosure—to take what is Christ's and declare it to you (==John 16:14-15==). The depths are not the private preserve of mystics and scholars. They are the freely given inheritance of everyone who has received the Spirit who is from God.
