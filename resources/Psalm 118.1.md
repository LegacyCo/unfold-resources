## Commentary
The word translated here as "mercy" is the beautiful Hebrew word hesed. Hesed means unfailing love, kindness, and goodness. It speaks to God's faithful, covenantal love. 

The Lord has covenanted with us, and that covenant makes certain demands on the Lord. Hesed love is one of those demands. He is obligated by covenant to faithfully love those He is in covenant with. This hesed love of the covenant is a love that endures forever. Not only that, it is a love that is new every morning (Lamentations 3:22-23).

## Application
God’s love for you is not fragile, moody, or dependent on you having your best day. His hesed is enduring and faithful. That means when you are weak, ashamed, tired, or aware of your need, you are not meeting a God whose love has grown impatient. You are meeting the same faithful God, with mercies new again every morning. So build your life on this: His love is not emotional, but a covenant reality He has bound Himself to.
