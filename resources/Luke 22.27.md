## Commentary
Matthew and Mark point to the death of Christ as the supreme expression of how Jesus served humanity (cf Matthew 20:28, Mark 10:45). Luke, however, points to something else; he points to Christ's **table service** (Luke 22:27). Luke recontextualizes both the dispute about greatness and the supreme expression of this greatness as servanthood in the setting of the table rather than the altar. "Who is greater, he who sits at the table or he who serves?...Yet I am among you as One who serves."
