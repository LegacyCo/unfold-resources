## Commentary
I like the way the NASB and ESV translate this verse, "For who among people knows the *thoughts* of a person except the spirit of the person that is in him? So also the *thoughts* of God no one knows, except the Spirit of God." Some translations say "the *things* of a man...the *things* of God." There is no word in the original manuscripts for either *thought* or *thing* in this verse, which is one reason translations vary here.

## Application
It's easy to consider that God has words, that God has actions, that God even has a will. But have you ever stopped to consider that God has thoughts? 

Thoughts are private. The minute a thought becomes public it's no longer a thought, it's a word or an action...something communicated. God loves His people so much He even gives us access to His thought life. This is one of the ministries of the Holy Spirit; searching the deep things of God and revealing what He discovers to those who come to Him seeking things only found in the mind of God. Verse 12 tells us one of the reasons we have received the Holy Spirit is so we would know these things that have been "freely given to us by God." Verse 16 goes even further, saying that we "have the mind of Christ."

Take some time to sit with God and ask, "God, what are You thinking about right now?"

## Cross Reference
See Jesus' words in John 15:15, "No longer do I call you slaves, for the slave does not know what his master is doing; but I have called you friends, because all things that I have heard from My Father I have made known to you."
