## Commentary
“For the hearts of this people have grown dull. Their ears are hard of hearing, and their eyes they have closed, lest they should see with their eyes and hear with their ears, lest they should understand with their hearts and turn, so that I should heal them.”

Jesus is quoting Isaiah 6:9-10. God spoke these words on the day He called Isaiah. Isaiah had seen the Lord on His throne and said, “Here am I! Send me” (==Isaiah 6:8==). Then God told him what kind of people he'd be preaching to. They would hear and not understand. About seven hundred years later, Jesus says that prophecy “is fulfilled” in the crowd standing in front of Him (==Matthew 13:14==).

The verse names three things: the heart, the ears, and the eyes. Then it names them again in reverse: eyes, ears, heart. The heart comes first and last. That's where the trouble starts. The heart grows dull, and then the ears stop hearing and the eyes close. The way back starts in the heart too. Jesus says they would have to “understand with their hearts and turn.”

“Their eyes they have closed.” Nobody closed their eyes for them. They had plenty to see. In the chapter before this, Jesus healed a man's withered hand in their synagogue (==Matthew 12:13==). He healed a man who was demon-possessed, blind, and mute (==Matthew 12:22==). The Pharisees watched it happen and said He did it by Beelzebub (==Matthew 12:24==). They saw the miracle and chose another explanation for it.

The verse ends with the words “so that I should heal them.” If these people had turned to Him, He would have healed them. He still heals anyone who turns. Paul writes, “When one turns to the Lord, the veil is taken away” (==2 Corinthians 3:16==).

## Word Study
**Grown dull** is the Greek *pachynō*, which means to thicken or to make fat. The New Testament uses it only here and in ==Acts 28:27==, where Paul quotes the same passage. It pictures a heart covered over with a thick layer, so that nothing gets through to it. A heart doesn't get that way in a day. It happens a little at a time, which is why Hebrews tells us to “exhort one another daily, while it is called ‘Today,’ lest any of you be hardened through the deceitfulness of sin” (==Hebrews 3:13==).

## Application
Is there something God has said to you that you've stopped responding to? “Today, if you will hear His voice, do not harden your hearts” (==Hebrews 3:15==).
