## Word Study
The word Jesus uses here for "take heed" is *blepo.* Blepo means to look at, see, observe, pay attention to. Jesus is saying we are to observe closely the things that He says to us. The principle is this: My standard of measure in hearing becomes His standard of measure in speaking. If I give Him my ear, then He will give me His voice, His understanding, and His word (the seed sown). And seeds always contain generations. If I have the seed of His word then I'm already in possession of future revelation, it just needs time to germinate in good soil. How I "hear" becomes essential for the life of that word to take root and grow.
