## Commentary
Jesus was not willing to let inferior disputes become offenses to those who did not understand the kingdom. 

## Application
We must use caution in how we present the church to the world and what our messaging is. Anything that inhibits the lost from hearing the message of the cross, no matter how right, must be secondary.
