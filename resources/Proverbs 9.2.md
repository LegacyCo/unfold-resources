## Commentary
In chapter 7, the adulteress woman points to her bed and its Egyptian linen, colored tapestries, and fine perfume (Proverbs 7:16). That woman is contrasted here against wisdom. She points to her furnished table. The foolish are enticed by the intoxication of the bed, "taking their fill of love until morning" (Proverbs 7:18). But the wise are enticed by the fellowship of the table.
