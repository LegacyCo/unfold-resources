## Commentary
Israel anticipated their king establishing his throne on the hill of Mount Zion by defeating their enemies, Rome being chief among them. In reality, God established His King on the hill of Golgotha. On the cross, Jesus defeated the ultimate enemy of God's people, sin. Israel thought their greatest enemy was something external. It was not. Their rescue was not from an enemy without, their real enemy was within. You and I are no different.
