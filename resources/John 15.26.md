## Word Study
Jesus tells us that one of the many things the Holy Spirit will do is "testify about Me." The word for *testify* is martyreō. It means to bear witness. The image is that of one who has seen something and speaks on the basis of having first-hand knowledge. For example, a witness is called on in a trial to give their testimony to determine what actually happened. Their testimony carries weight because they witnessed the incident in question. The Holy Spirit comes to all believers to testify about Jesus and the reality of His kingdom.

It's interesting that the very next statement from Jesus is, "And you also will bear witness (martyreō)." These words were specific to the disciples ("because you have been with me from the beginning"), but they also apply to every believer who has received the testimony of the Holy Spirit. We now can bear witness to those around us of what *we* have experienced in Christ.
