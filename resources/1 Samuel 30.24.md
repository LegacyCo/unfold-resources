## Commentary
After weeping for their families, 200 of David's men were too exhausted to continue pursuing the Amalekites, so they stayed back. The 400 men who defeated their enemy and reclaimed their families didn't want to share the spoils with the 200 who stayed back. But David communicates a principle of the kingdom of God; the spoils of battle are not just for those on the front lines. Those who "stay by the supplies" also share in the victory.

This is also a principle in the New Covenant church. Those who go out into the mission field are not the only one who receive the reward. Those who "stay by the supplies" and pray, give, support, etc. will also receive the reward of participating in the Great Commission. It is credited to their account as well as those who go. We are one body.
