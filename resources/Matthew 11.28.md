## Commentary
The Greek word that is translated "give you rest" is *anapauo*. Anapauo means to make cease, to bring to an end, bring *into* rest, to calm someone who has become disturbed, to revive.

Jesus's words are an invitation — "Come to Me". He brings those who come to Him into union with Himself, and by way of that union, they inherit rest. The rest is always IN HIM. The moment we are not in Him we become the disciples on the stormy sea crying out "don't You care that we are dying" (Mark 4:35-41).

When our rest is found in Him then it can never be taken away; He gives His peace not as the world gives (John 14:27). The Son, at rest, has the authority to speak to the storm and even bring the storm into a place of rest. That's our inheritance.

## Application
How many do not inherit the promised rest because they are waiting for the rest to come to them — as if the gift is exclusive of the Giver? 

When you are not at rest, it's not that rest has left you, it's an announcement that you have left the kind yoke of the Prince of Peace. It's evidence that you have surrendered authority to circumstances that have untethered you from your place of rest in Him.

Being in His rest doesn't mean you don't feel the effects of difficult circumstances; Jesus was in agony in the garden (Luke 22:44). But it does mean those feelings no longer have permission to control you ("Father, not My will but Yours be done" - Luke 22:42). You can navigate the difficulty with the clarity of someone rooted in rest.
