## Commentary
Paul is writing these words to the church, not unbelievers ("I speak this to *your* shame"). This is powerful.

## Word Study
Awake - *eknepsate*: become sober, return to one's senses, change to a correct state of mind, sleep off a drunken fit, awake.

Righteousness - *dikaios*: the state of being as one ought to be, correct, accurate, right, righteous.

Do not sin - *me hamartanete*: no (me) sinning (hamartanete). Hamartia means to be without your allotted form, to be distorted in identity. Sin is to be out of alignment with the blueprint of original design.

Do not have the knowledge - *agnosia*: failure to understand, ignorance, error, lack of knowledge.

A literal, expanded translation of this verse would be something along these lines: "Wake up from your drunken fit and return to your correct state of mind and the actual state of being that you were designed for. Do not be distorted in your identity, form, or original design any longer...which is actually a natural consequence of failing to understand who God is."
