## Commentary
“By grace you have been saved through faith; and this is not of yourselves, it is the gift of God.”

Every part of salvation arrives as gift.

God supplies the mercy. God sends the Son. God bears the cost. God raises the dead. God gives the Spirit. Faith opens the hand and receives what grace has placed within reach.

Intelligence, discipline, family heritage, theological knowledge, and moral effort cannot produce salvation. The explanation for our rescue rests entirely in the heart of God: He is rich in mercy, and He loved us with a great love (==Ephesians 2:4==).

Faith carries the full weight of the soul toward Jesus. It trusts His work, receives His life, and abandons every claim to self-salvation.

Grace also shapes everything that follows conversion. The life of obedience grows from the same gift that brought us into Christ. God supplies the desire, strength, wisdom, and endurance required to walk with Him.

Paul understood this in his own ministry: “I labored even more than all of them, yet not I, but the grace of God within me” (==1 Corinthians 15:10==). Grace filled his work with divine strength.

Maturity deepens our awareness of dependence. The more clearly we see God, the more freely we recognize that every good thing in us came from Him.

## Application
Pay attention to any place where obedience has begun producing pride.

Every gift, opportunity, insight, victory, and act of faithfulness flows from grace. Receive it gratefully and steward it faithfully.
