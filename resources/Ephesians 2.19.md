## Commentary
Paul doesn’t say you’ve joined an organization. He says you’re “members of the *household* of God.” Family is God’s model for His church—and when we understand how God designed family to work, we can measure the pattern of church we inherited against the heavenly pattern and make the necessary adjustments.

Three things are true of family in God’s design. First, family is built on covenant. All family, as God designed it, begins with covenant—a man and a woman covenant together and God sees them as one (==Genesis 2:24==). Covenant is everything in the family because covenant is everything in the kingdom.

Second, family produces life. In God’s design, covenant family is where new life is meant to be birthed—and then raised inside the safety and benefits of that covenant, surrounded by people committed to it.

Third, covenant family is the crucible where new life matures. Family is the chisel God uses to shape us into the image of Christ. It’s messy and raw, humbling and honest. Family exposes our imperfections precisely so they can be dealt with inside the safety of covenant—“as iron sharpens iron, so a man sharpens the countenance of his friend” (==Proverbs 27:17==). You can’t be sharpened at a distance.

Now apply those three marks to the church, and you see God’s design for His house: a covenant family, that produces new life, and matures that life into the image of Christ. Here’s the problem: when the church functions as an organization instead of a household, it has to rely on programs and self-effort to do what only family life can do. When the church is built on an attendance culture instead of a covenant culture, it may produce converts—but it rarely produces sons and daughters. Converts attend. Sons and daughters belong, serve, stay through the mess, and carry the family name.

God is family—Father, Son, and Spirit—and He is going to build His family (==Matthew 16:18==). The question is whether a generation will become as passionate about being the family of God as it is about anything else. When that happens, the world won’t be looking at a repackaged version of itself. It will be looking at a household—and through that household, at the Father (==1 John 4:12==).
