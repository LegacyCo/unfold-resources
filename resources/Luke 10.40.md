## Word Study
The Greek word for distracted is *perispao*. It means to drag around in circles. Martha is distracted with serving (diakonian - which means *ministry*). Even pure ministry for Jesus can become a weight that we drag around in circles. We become busy, but we are ineffective.

Mary chose the "good part." The Greek word for good part is *agathos*. It means good AND profitable. It is often translated "good works." It's interesting that Jesus uses the word *agathos* here to describe Mary and not *kalos*, which can also be translated "good," but it does not imply any usefulness or benefit — it is good in appearance only. Martha chose the kalos (good, but not useful), Mary chose the agathos (good and profitable). 

## Application
Busyness for the sake of appearance will never be effective. Yet, what may appear ineffective (i.e; sitting at the feet of Jesus) has the potential to have the greatest impact.
