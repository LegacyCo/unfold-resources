## Word Study
**Radiance** - as the sunlight conveys the brightness and intensity of the sun. The word should not be translated or understood as "reflection" — which conveys a separation from the actual substance and source. A reflection is merely an image representation. It has no power or substance to it, whereas the sunlight has within it the heat and brilliance of the sun itself. 

Even this metaphor breaks down when you consider that the sunlight is not the source of the light and heat, but this is dealt with by adding the other 2 descriptors:

1. **Express image** - the Greek word is charakteer. This is the only time it is used in Scripture. This word means:
		- the embossed stamp on a coin
		- the style and character of one's writing
		- the bodily and psychological structure with which one is born which is unique to that person and cannot be changed by education or development even though it may be hidden or effaced (like what happens to the stars at night).
	The image of the Father did not develop in the Son. It has eternally been embossed in Him. There was no time that the Father's nature was not embodied in the Son. Jesus is not just the image of God (reflection), He is the glory and substance of God.

2. **Person** - the Greek word is hypostasis. This word means:
		- the essence, substance, source of something
	- Jesus is not merely *like* the Father, Jesus is the same essence as the Father
	Jesus is the supreme and final revelation of the Father.
