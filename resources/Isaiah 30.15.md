## Commentary
This is from The Table of the Lord by Charles Wallis - The activities and rewards of the time are so engrossing that many high-minded and pure-hearted people find no time for meditation, rest, and communion in the upper room. They listen with such passionate attention to the cries for help that come from the world around them that they no longer hear the still, small voice of the Father.

In the houses of these there is no upper room. It is true, He comes in a thousand forms. But it is also true that men must make ready the room in which they can meet Him face to face. The house ceases to be a place of contagious hope. The upper room is a necessity of the higher nature.
