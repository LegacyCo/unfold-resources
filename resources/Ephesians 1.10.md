## Commentary
The ultimate purpose of God is that all things will be summed up in His Son. Everything He is doing is moving toward this one great end.

That means if we lose sight of Jesus as the center of God’s purpose, even good things can become distractions. We can become consumed with lesser purposes—our calling, our future, our healing, our assignment, our open doors—and forget that none of those things are the point in themselves.

The point is Christ.

Everything God is doing in the earth, and everything He is doing in us, is moving toward the end goal of ultimate union with Jesus. He is gathering what sin scattered. He is reconciling what rebellion fractured. He is bringing all things under the beautiful rule of King Jesus.

## Application
If you want to understand your life, don’t begin with what is happening around you. Begin with Christ.

Your circumstances may be impossible to ignore, but they are not ultimate. Your goals are important, but they are not central. The purpose of your life is not to get through it. God intends you to be gathered more fully into Jesus, until everything about you finds its center in Him.
