## Commentary
“The kingdom of heaven is like leaven, which a woman took and hid in three measures of meal till it was all leavened.” The kingdom that will outlast every other kingdom does not arrive like the 82nd Airborne Division. It comes like leaven worked into dough, like a seed going into the ground (==Matthew 13:31-32==). It advances by persuasion, not brute force—hidden, patient, and unstoppable.

This is precisely why the kingdom disappoints our expectations. John the Baptist announced the Messiah, then watched Him heal in Galilee while Rome still ruled in Jerusalem...and while John himself sat in Herod’s prison. The forerunner ended up sending his disciples to ask, “Are You the Coming One, or do we look for another?” (==Matthew 11:3==). John expected the King to overthrow the empire. The King was leavening the world instead. When the kingdom does not behave the way we expect, our temptation is to impose our own ideas of what it should be doing.

And here is the greatest contrast of all: this kingdom overthrows every other kingdom by offering up its King as the sacrifice. Other kings offer their people as the sacrifice for the good of kingdom. This King offers Himself for the people (==Mark 10:45==). The cross is not a detour from the kingdom’s advance; it is the way this kingdom conquers—and in it, the King shows us what life in His kingdom is like.
