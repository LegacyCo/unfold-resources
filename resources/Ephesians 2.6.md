## Commentary
We are not citizens of this world, we are seated in Christ in the heavenlies. When the king of England travels the world, he still retains his authority even though he isn’t seated on his throne. He is always “seated” on his throne. It’s the same for us; though we travel this world, we are always seated in Christ in heavenly places.

## Application
So don’t live like a captive wandering the earth. Live like a citizen of heaven who has been sent into it. Your feet may be here, but your seat is there. So stop letting earthly conditions tell you who you are or what authority you carry.

You may be walking through pressure, conflict, temptation, or uncertainty, but your true position has not changed. You are still seated in Christ. That means you do not respond to life merely as someone stuck under circumstances. You respond as someone whose life is anchored above them.
