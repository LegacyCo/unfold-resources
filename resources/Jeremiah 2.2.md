## Commentary
A quote by Samuel Whitefield from his book *Will You Choose the Wilderness*: God did not bring Israel into the wilderness for punishment. God designed the wilderness to be a place of tender encounter where Israel entered into deep covenant with Him. In a sense, it was "honeymoon" before He brought Israel into the land and gave her an inheritance. God wanted a kind of communion with Israel that would not be possible once her people spread out and began enjoying their inheritance in the land. The wilderness only became a place of hardship and judgment because of Israel's disobedience.

## Application
Don’t despise the wilderness simply because it's uncomfortable. Ask what God is forming with you there. As Art Katz wrote in *The Prophetic Call*, "There is something about the nature of the wilderness that strips a man from the kind of amenities and seductions that would otherwise compromise him." See also ==Jeremiah 31:2==.
