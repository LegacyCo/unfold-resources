## Commentary
There is a difference between something being cleansed and something being made holy.

The blood consecrated the altar, the vessels, the priests, and the tabernacle. It prepared them for God. But Exodus says the tabernacle would be sanctified by His glory. In other words, blood prepared the place, but God's presence made it holy.

Holiness is not just the absence of something. Holiness is the presence of God. Something becomes holy when God touches it. That's why desert sand can become holy ground when God is there. That's why an ordinary bush can burn with divine fire and not be consumed. The bush became holy because the Holy One was present in it.

And the same is true of us. We are cleansed by the blood of the Lamb of God, and we are made holy by His indwelling Spirit. God doesn't just forgive us and leave us empty. He cleanses the temple so He can fill it with glory.
