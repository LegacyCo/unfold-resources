## Commentary
It takes two verses for Luke to tell us where Paul and his companions traveled, but the areas they were covering were not small. Given the roads they would have been taking, they journeyed at least three hundred miles after leaving Antioch. That's the equivalent of walking from Los Angeles to San Francisco. By the time they reached Mysia, the journey would have taken them several weeks.
