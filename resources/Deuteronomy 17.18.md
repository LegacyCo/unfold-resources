## Commentary
It is impossible for the mind to learn without an external system or process to work out the information, ideas, questions that are being considered. That process can not happen in the mind alone. It must be something external.

There is something about the act of writing something down that helps build the external system to carry the thoughts and writes those things on the mind in a way that just reading them does not.
