## Commentary
Paul is confronting both Jew and Gentile with the impartiality of God's judgment. The Gentile could not plead ignorance. The Jew could not hide behind covenant privilege. Every person stands before God exposed in truth.

This is one of Paul’s major points in Romans 2. Possessing the law is not the same as *obeying* the law. Hearing the word is not the same as *doing* the word. Being exposed to the things of God can become dangerous if it produces confidence without obedience.

James makes the same point when he warns believers not to be hearers only, deceiving themselves (James 1:22). Jesus also says the servant who knew his master’s will and did not do it will receive a greater judgment (Luke 12:47–48). Greater light brings greater responsibility.
