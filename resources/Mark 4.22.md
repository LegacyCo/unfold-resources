## Commentary
Jesus promises that everything hidden will be revealed and any secrets will be brought to the light. He isn't talking about *our* secrets, not in this passage anyway. He's talking about *His*. Jesus is just looking for someone with "ears to hear" to be the light that reveals His mysteries. After all, it has been given for us to know the mysteries of His kingdom (v11).
