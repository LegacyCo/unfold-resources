## Commentary
The opposite of holiness is not sinfulness. It's commonness. Ezekiel says the priests were to teach God’s people “the difference between the holy and the common” and help them discern “between the unclean and the clean.” Holiness means something has been set apart for the Lord. It belongs to Him in a distinct way.

That's why the church matters so deeply. Jesus gave the church the keys of the kingdom, which means the church is meant to be the visible witness and doorway of kingdom life in the earth. But when the church forgets its distinction and becomes common, it can dilute the beauty and holiness of the very kingdom it was entrusted to reveal.
