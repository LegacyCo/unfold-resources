## Commentary
When God separated light and darkness, He made an eternal distinction: darkness has no place in light, and light has no place in darkness. 

Paul writes in 2 Corinthians 6.14, 
	"Do not be unequally yoked together with unbelievers. For what fellowship has 
	righteousness with lawlessness? And what communion has light with darkness?"

If you are in Christ, your place is in the light, and you are to have no fellowship with the darkness…Jesus has called you out and separated you. “Come out from among them and be separate says the Lord” (2 Cor 6:17). The fruit of darkness can't come into the environment of light…God has separated those things. You can’t hide the things of the night in the realm of the day.

We often think of darkness and light as an atmosphere and an environment…and it is. Those atmospheres of darkness and light produce their own fruit according to their likeness. Light produces life, understanding and clarity, revelation, joy, warmth, safety, enemies can't hide in the light...they are exposed. The fruit of darkness is fear, worry, shame, gossip, lies, stealing, selfishness, anxiety. 

The fruit of darkness can not be hidden in the light. Or to say it another way, you can't bring together what God has separated.

You could have a black coat with a hundred spots on it and nobody will see them. But as soon as you put on a white coat, even one speck is clearly seen. If you are a child of light and you try to bring something of the darkness into His world of light, it's His goodness to expose that fruit of darkness. It’s His goodness to not allow light to have communion with darkness.

## Application
Let's take this a step further; in the creation story of Genesis 1, the wisdom of God’s design was to bring order to creation by dividing things that cannot exist together; day from night, the water above from the water below, sea from land. So, anyone who is now in Christ has the renewed mind of the Creator…we have been given the ability to recognize things that the Creator intends to keep separate; spirit from flesh, faith from unbelief, righteousness from wickedness, the free gift of grace from religious striving and works, right from wrong...and on and on. We have been given the light of revelation that shows us what the Creator intends to divide and keep separate. So, when Paul writes to Timothy in  1 Timothy 3:15 that the church is the pillar and support of truth, he is saying that because we have our eyes opened to see what God has done in creation, the Body of Christ is now the gate that holds back the things that God intends to keep separate. That's what light does...it holds back darkness. 

When darkness increases in the world, it’s not the worlds fault. We can’t get upset at darkness for being dark. That’s like getting mad at a fish for being wet. If darkness, and the fruit of darkness, is increasing in the world, that's an announcement that the light of the church has grown dim. The solution is always the same; the Day One light needs to be set up on a hill, set on the lamp stand, so that it can shine.

If light is doing what light does, darkness has no choice but to leave. Just as the darkness has no say in the matter when you turn the light switch on...the only way darkness wins is for light to grow dim.
