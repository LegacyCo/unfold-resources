## Commentary
Jesus’ assessment of John confronts our definition of greatness. Much of his life was hidden in the wilderness, and when he finally appeared, his message was simple: prepare the way of the Lord.

Yet Jesus said there was no greater prophet born of women.

Greatness in the kingdom cannot be measured by the standards of the world. John was great because he understood his assignment, stayed faithful to what God was asking Him to do, prepared a people for the Messiah, and knew how to decrease when the Bridegroom arrived.

John shows us that God’s way into greatness is not self-promotion, but surrender. Not making our name known, but preparing the way for His.
