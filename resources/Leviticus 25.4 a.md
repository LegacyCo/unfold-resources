## Commentary
If Israel did not discern the season they were in, then they would mismanage the blessing of the 6th year. They would see that increased harvest through the wrong lens and the blessing would be squandered.

As long as God's people remained aware of Yahweh's seasonal rhythm then they could respond accordingly. "And if you say, 'What shall we eat in the seventh year, since we shall not sow nor gather in our produce?' Then I will command My blessing on you in the sixth year, and it will bring forth produce enough for three years" (Leviticus 25:20-21).

This also applied to the weekly rhythm. "See! For the Lord has given you the Sabbath; therefore He gives you on the sixth day bread for two days. Let every man remain in his place; let no man go out of his place on the seventh day" (Exodus 16:29).

Awareness of the season was needed for them to enter into the sabbath rest. If they ate twice as much on the sixth day then they were not going to be at rest on the 7th day.
