## Word Study
Scripture uses the word koinonia when it speaks of the Lord's table. Koinonia means fellowship, participation, a sharing in. This fellowship with the Lord at His table, this sharing in His body and blood, this participation with Him in His suffering, is a spiritual reality to all who come to His table by faith. It is a fellowship just as real as the Holy Spirit living in us, for it is the same word that Paul uses to describe the relationship that we have with the Holy Spirit, "Therefore if there is any consolation in Christ, if any comfort of love, if any fellowship (koinonia) of the Spirit, if any affection and mercy..." (Philippians 2:1).
