## Commentary
This is from "A Long Obedience In the Same Direction" by Eugene Peterson: Topographically Jerusalem was the highest city in Palestine, and so all who traveled there spent much of their time ascending. But the ascent was not only literal, it was also a metaphor: the trip to Jerusalem acted out a life lived upward toward God, an existence that advanced from one level to another in developing maturity, what Paul described as "the goal, where God is beckoning us onward-to Jesus."
