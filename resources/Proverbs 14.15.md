## Commentary
The wise are not impulsive. They don't believe every word they hear, react to every emotion they feel, or move simply because an opportunity appears. The prudent person pauses long enough to consider where a path will lead before taking the next step.

Wisdom governs the inner life before it governs the outward one. Those who rule over their spirits study how to answer, are slow to anger, and respond gently when opposed. Their restraint isn't weakness; it's evidence of understanding and strength.

The foolish are easily moved by pressure, flattery, fear, and urgency. They mistake speed for faith and reaction for discernment. But the wise understand that not every open door should be entered, not every accusation needs an answer, and not every desire should be followed.

Even the inheritance God has promised should not be grasped impulsively (==Proverbs 20:21==). Wisdom is willing to wait, consider, pray, and move at the pace of discernment.
