## Commentary
“And David went out to meet them, and answered and said to them, ‘If you have come peaceably to me to help me, my heart will be united with you; but if to betray me to my enemies, since there is no wrong in my hands, may the God of our fathers look and bring judgment.’”

David had good reason to be careful. Doeg had turned him in to Saul (==1 Samuel 22:9==). The people of Keilah were ready to hand him over right after he rescued them (==1 Samuel 23:12==). The Ziphites told Saul where he was hiding—twice (==1 Samuel 23:19==; ==1 Samuel 26:1==). And now here comes a group that includes men from Benjamin, Saul's own tribe. So David walks out to meet them himself and tells them exactly where he stands.

His answer has two sides. To anyone coming in peace, he holds nothing back: “my heart will be united with you.” He doesn't keep them at arm's length or put them on probation. If they're for him, he's all in with them. And to anyone coming to betray him? He doesn't make a single threat. He just says his hands are clean, and that God will see it and judge. It's the same thing he told Saul outside the cave: “Let the LORD judge between you and me, and let the LORD avenge me on you. But my hand shall not be against you” (==1 Samuel 24:12==).

Betrayal hadn't closed David's heart. But it hadn't made him naive either. How do you stay that open after being burned that many times? David could do it because his safety didn't depend on reading every person correctly. It rested with “the God of our fathers.” Peter says Jesus lived the same way: “when He was reviled, did not revile in return; when He suffered, He did not threaten, but committed Himself to Him who judges righteously” (==1 Peter 2:23==).

## Application
When we've been betrayed, we usually go one of two directions. We stop trusting anyone, or we find a way to get even. David did neither. If you've been hurt by people you let in, keep your hands clean. That means no retaliation, no slander, no campaign to settle the score. Hand the judgment to God (==Romans 12:19==). And stay willing to give your heart to people who come in peace. Be discerning, yes. But God isn't asking you to live with a heart that's permanently closed.
