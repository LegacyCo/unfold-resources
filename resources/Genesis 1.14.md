## Commentary
In the creation story we see light mentioned on two different days; day one, when God creates light and separates it from darkness (v3) and day four, when God creates two lights in the heavens (v14-18). There is a very interesting difference between Day One light and Day Four light.

The physical world that God created is designed to mirror the spiritual world. The most important thing in the spiritual world is...God. So it’s fitting that the first thing God creates in the physical world is a representation of Himself...light. 

The first thing that God says to creation about Himself is "I am like this...light." As John writes in 1 John 1:5, 

"This is the message which we have heard from Him and declare to you, that God is light and in Him is no darkness at all." 

James writes in James 1:17 that God is the "Father of all lights". God is the uncreated, eternal light. And the first thing He did in creation was reveal Himself.

There was no source for Day One light other than the Spirit of God speaking it into being. The "source" wasn't created until Day *Four*. 

So we see in the creation story that there is Day *One* light and there is Day *Four* light. Two different lights mentioned in creation. 

One of the differences in Day One light and Day Four light is Day One light is eternal, Day Four light is temporary. Listen to Revelation 21:23-25, 
	"The city (New Jerusalem) had no need of the sun or of the moon to shine in it, for the glory of God has illuminated it. The Lamb is its light. And the nations of those who are saved shall walk in its light, and the kings of the earth bring their glory and honor into it. Its gates shall not be shut at all by day (there shall be no night there)."

But I would suggest that we are actually dealing with *three* kinds of light: 
	Day One light (which testifies of what God is like),
	Day Four light (the light of the sun, moon, and stars),
	The *uncreated* light of God Himself (Ps 36:9 - “In Your light we see light”).
	
This is pretty cool, but what does this have to do with us? 

Consider the words of Jesus in Matthew 5:14, "you are the light of the world." Which light is He saying we are like? 

Is Jesus saying we are like the sun or the moon or the stars…Day Four light? No. 

Is He saying we are the uncreated light of God Himself? No. 

Jesus says we are Day One light…the light shining through the darkness that testifies of the eternal light of God. 

Paul makes this connection in 2 Corinthians 4:6, 
	"For it is the God who commanded light to shine out of darkness, when He shined in our hearts the brilliant dawning light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ."

Before you were saved, you were in darkness...you were blinded by darkness and unable to see the truth of who God is (see Eph 5:8). But just as God caused Day One light to shine into creation to reveal Himself in light, when you were born again in Christ, the first thing He did was cause the light of His glory to shine on your heart and open the eyes of your understanding to see the beauty of the gospel in the face of Jesus. You were suddenly able to see Someone who had always been there, but who was hidden by your darkness. He commanded light to shine on you so that you could see. And now that you can see Him, He says, “Shine, just like I shine.”

## Application
At the end of the age, there will be no need for the sun...the Lamb of God will be our source of light. We will walk in the glory of His light just as we do the glory of the sun in this life. Think of that next time you feel the warmth of the sunlight. Allow your spirit to meditate on that coming day when you will bask in the warmth of eternal light that is bursting from the Lamb of God. Consider the day when there will be no more night…and allow that to drive you to worship.
