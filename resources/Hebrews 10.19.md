## Commentary
The blood of Jesus did not merely cleanse us from where we had been. It opened access into where we were always meant to live.

We often think of His blood only in terms of atonement—and rightly so. His blood is sufficient to wash away sin, silence guilt, cleanse the conscience, and remove every accusation that stood against us. But Hebrews presses the matter further. The blood of Jesus does not only deal with our past. It opens a “new and living way.”

Under the old covenant, the Most Holy Place was closed. Only the high priest could enter, and only once a year, and never without blood. But Hebrews says Jesus entered the Most Holy Place “with His own blood” once and for all. He didn't bring the blood of another. He offered Himself. And by that blood, the way into the presence of God has been opened.

This means the blood of Jesus is not only the answer to sin. It's the invitation into communion with God...forever.

The blood that pardons you also brings you near. The blood that cleanses your conscience also gives you boldness to approach God. The blood that removes guilt also establishes access. We come with full assurance because the High Priest over the house of God has made a way that cannot be closed.

So the Christian life is not meant to be lived at a distance from God, grateful to be forgiven but afraid to come close. The blood of Jesus has opened a new way of life: life in the presence of God, life with a cleansed heart, life with confidence before the Father.

## Application
Don’t stop at forgiveness. The blood of Jesus gives us so much more than that. Draw near to God daily with absolute assurance that are you invited by the blood of His Son. And that access is not based on how you feel, how you've performed, or any other metric religion tries to use to measure value. Your access to God rests solely on the value of the blood of Jesus.
