## Commentary
The Merarites were assigned to carry the structural pieces of the tabernacle: the frames, bars, pillars, bases, pegs, cords, and related equipment.

Their work was not the most visible. They were not carrying the ark. They were not carrying the lampstand. They were not handling the holy vessels. But without their assignment, the tabernacle could not stand.

This is one of the gifts of Numbers 4. It reminds us that God sees service people often overlook. Some assignments are visible. Others are buried behind the scenes. Some people carry what everyone notices. Others carry what makes the visible things possible.

Paul says the members of the body that seem weaker are necessary, and the parts that seem less honorable are given greater honor (==1 Corinthians 12:22–24==). God does not measure service the way we do. He sees the hidden labor, the unseen obedience, the consistent faithfulness, and the gifts carried without applause.

Hebrews says God is not unjust to forget the work and labor of love shown toward His name (==Hebrews 6:10==). That includes the work no one else notices.
