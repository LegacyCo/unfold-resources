## Commentary
We now have Adam speak for the first time. Up to this point in Scripture, God was the only one who has spoken. But here we have man declare that, finally, there is now a companion for him in creation. 

Adam calls the woman “wife” (isha)...and calls himself “husband” (ish) (“she shall be called WIFE, because she was taken out of HUSBAND.”). It's not just that one of these is a masculine form of the word and the other is feminine...the picture created by these words is UNION…she was created *from* him. In the same way that A-dam is made from A-dam-a, isha is mde from ish.
