## Commentary
Paul doesn’t point to Moses, he points to Abraham. Paul is showing that the foundation of God’s relationship with His people was never law, but faith. Moses represents the covenant that came later—the covenant of commandments and external regulation—but Abraham represents the foundational of the faith: a man who believed God, and it was counted to him as righteousness. Paul is making it plain that blessing does not flow to us through Sinai, but through promise. The law came in its time and served its purpose, but it was never the root. Abraham is the root. Faith is the root.

## Application
If you belong to Jesus, then your standing with God is not built on your performance, your discipline, or how well you keep religious rules. It is built the same way Abraham’s was—by trusting the God who speaks promise. That means we stop trying to earn what can only be received. We stop relating to God like slaves under pressure and begin relating to Him like sons who believe their Father is as good as Jesus says He is.
