## Commentary
Jesus is pointing out that while ordinary Israelites were commanded to cease from their labor on the Sabbath, the priests were actually required to work. They offered sacrifices, maintained the temple service, and performed duties that involved considerable physical activity. In fact, ==Numbers 28:9–10== specifically commanded the priests to offer *additional* sacrifices on the Sabbath.

The word translated “profane” is the Greek bebēloō, meaning to treat something sacred as common. Jesus uses the word deliberately to expose the inconsistency in the Pharisees' reasoning. The priests performed activities on the Sabbath that would ordinarily be considered work, yet they were not guilty of violating God's command because their work was itself commanded by God.

Jesus is establishing an important principle: the Sabbath was never intended to obstruct the purposes of the God who instituted it. The priests were blameless because their service was an expression of obedience, not rebellion.
