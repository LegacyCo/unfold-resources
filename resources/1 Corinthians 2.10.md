## Cross Reference
See Jesus' words in Matthew 13:11 when His disciples asked why He speaks to the crowds in parables, "Because it has been given to you to know the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven, but to them it has not been given." This ties in with the previous reference to Psalm 25:24, "The secret counsel of the Lord is for those who fear Him." There is a privilege that those who serve Jesus have to draw near to Him for understanding. The mysteries of the kingdom, the things eye has not seen nor ear heard, these things are the inheritance of sons and daughters of God.
