## Commentary
Recall what Jesus says in John 17:26, "(Father) I have declared/revealed to them Your name, and I will continue to declare/reveal it SO THAT the LOVE that You love Me with will be *IN* them." Whatever God's name is declared over becomes filled with His love. This is why land remembers ("Make an altar of *earth* for Me...In every place where I cause My name to be remembered, I will come to you and bless you").

The Name may not have been declared over a land for generations, but just because a man filled with Abba's love no longer possesses the land doesn't mean the land is no longer filled with love. The land remembers.

The land is crying out for an image-bearer who will come into alignment with the Name that has seeped into the soil — ground that once experienced the glory of the Name being declared over a people who possessed it.

Man's days may be few, and his glory may pass away like the flower fades. One generation lives and dies and, if history is any indication, by the third generation, the man in possession of the land is no longer possessed by the Name. But land only has one generation. Land is the great constant. Land remembers the third day of creation, when God spoke it into being and declared it "good" (Genesis 1:10).

In the same way the widow longs for the spouse of her youth, land groans and cries out for the revealing of the sons of God to take possession of their inheritance (Romans 8:19), to once again build an altar of her soil and offer sacrifices that cause the Name to be declared within her boundaries afresh. This is how cities are restored. This is how desolate places become like the garden of eden (Ezekiel 36:35). This is how the Valley of Baca (weeping) is made into a spring (Psalm 84.:6). This is how broken walls are rebuilt and gates are possessed.
