## Commentary
When Israel withdrew in fear from the voice of God, the Lord said to Moses, “But as for you, stand here by Me.” While the people returned to their tents, Moses was invited to remain in the presence of God.

This is the distinction of the priestly life. Many are willing to hear enough from God to receive instruction, but few are willing to remain with Him when there is nothing else to be gained.

The words “stand here by Me” describe more than physical location. They speak of availability, attention, and nearness. Moses was to remain close enough to hear what God would say and still enough to receive what God would reveal. His authority among the people came from the place he occupied before the Lord (==1 Kings 17:1==).

God still looks for people who will stand with Him. The world is full of movement, noise, and urgency. But God forms His servants among those who are willing to remain by Him (==Ezekiel 44:15==).

Before we can stand before men with anything of eternal weight, we must learn to stand before God (see ==Genesis 17:1==).
