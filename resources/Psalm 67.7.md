## Commentary
This psalm gives us a progression for how the land comes into increase. And it begins with mercy.

“God be merciful to us and bless us, and cause His face to shine upon us” (v1). The first movement is not the land producing. It's the face of God turning toward His people.

This is revival language. It's the cry of a people who know that blessing begins when God’s presence is directed toward them. They are asking for mercy, blessing, and the shining of His face.

But the psalm doesn't stop there. Verse 2 tells us *why* His face shines upon His people: “That Your way may be known on earth, Your salvation among all nations.”

God blesses a people *so that* His ways can be revealed in the earth through them.

The people who receive mercy now carry responsibility. They are not blessed to become consumers of God’s goodness. They are blessed to become witnesses of God’s ways. The mercy that shines on them is meant to make something known in the earth.

Then, as His ways become known, praise erupts from the nations. “Let the peoples praise You, O God; let all the peoples praise You” (v3). The nations rejoice because they see the righteous judgment and government of God. “For You shall judge the people righteously, and govern the nations on earth” (v4).

What a beautiful progression:

God is merciful to His people.  
His face shines upon them.  
His ways become known in the earth.  
The nations see His righteous government.  
Praise erupts among the peoples.  
*Then* the land yields its increase.

“Then the earth shall yield her increase” (v6).

That word “then” is significant. The land yielding increase is not disconnected from what comes before it. The land responds to mercy received, ways revealed, praise restored, and the righteous government of God made known.

A land yielding its increase in this measure is an announcement that the people who possess it understand the ways of the merciful Creator.

This is similar to Psalm 85. “Show us Your mercy, LORD, and grant us Your salvation” (==Psalm 85:7==). Then, “that glory may dwell in our land” (==Psalm 85:9==). Then, “the LORD will give what is good; and our land will yield its increase” (==Psalm 85:12==). Mercy comes to the people, glory dwells in the land, and the land yields increase.

It is also similar to Leviticus 25. That chapter deals with Sabbath rest for the land and the Year of Jubilee. God tells His people to keep His Sabbaths and walk in His ways, and then He promises rain in its season, the land yielding produce, the trees yielding fruit, and His own presence walking among them.

The point is consistent: the land’s increase is tied to the people’s alignment.

We cannot ignore the ways of God and then demand the blessing of God. We cannot violate the order of the Creator and then cry out for creation to produce the fruit of obedience. The land does not yield its increase because people learned how to declare God’s name over their ambitions. The land yields its increase when the people who possess it receive His mercy, walk in His ways, and bear witness to His righteous government.

## Application
Pray for His face to shine on you, but also ask Him to make His ways visible *through* you.
