## Commentary
The word Paul uses here for "judge/examine" is *anakrino*. Anakrino means to investigate in court, examine, question, study throroughly, judge, investigate. The word contains a great deal of legal language and tone to it.

Paul does not do these things, and the fact that anyone else would do them to him means little to him. This is a picture of the freedom we have in Christ.

## Cross Reference
Other passages that use the word *anakrino*:

John 18:31 - "Then Pilate said to them, 'You take Him and judge Him according to your law.'"

Acts 23:3 - "Then Paul said to him, 'God will strike you, you whitewashed wall! For you sit to judge me according to the law, and do you command me to be struck contrary to the law?'”
