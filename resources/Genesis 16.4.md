## Commentary
“So he went in to Hagar, and she conceived. And when she saw that she had conceived, her mistress became despised in her eyes.”

Abraham had received vision from God: he would father a nation that would bless the earth (==Genesis 12:2-3==; ==Genesis 15:5==). But the vision was to be fulfilled inside a covenant—through Sarah, his wife. When the years passed and the barrenness held, the pressure to fulfill the vision drove Abraham and Sarah outside the covenant that gave birth to the vision in the first place. Sarah, concluding something was wrong with her, urged her husband toward the slave woman (==Genesis 16:2==)—and Ishmael was conceived through slavery rather than through promise. The pressure to fulfill the vision produces Ishmael every time. Paul reads the two sons exactly this way—one born according to the flesh, one through promise (==Galatians 4:22-23==).

The secrets God shares in intimacy must be walked out within the boundaries of that intimacy. God gives vision to those who are near to Him—intimacy births vision—and the vision must be carried the same way it was conceived. When His people abandon that, they start believing they can get things done as slaves instead of lovers, and God's house fills with hard-working, slave-conscious employees running ministries instead of those who simply love Him. How was Abraham actually to fulfill a vision of descendants like the stars? Stay with Sarah. Stay in the covenant, no matter how barren things looked—because the same God who gave the vision would quicken the barrenness in His own time (==Genesis 21:1-2==; ==Romans 4:19-21==).

## Application
Check how you are carrying what God has spoken to you. When fruit is slow and the vision looks barren, the temptation is to fulfill it by other means—borrowed methods, forced doors, a Hagar that promises faster results. Whatever is conceived that way will be an Ishmael: genuinely alive, and a lifelong complication (==Genesis 21:9-10==). Honor the covenant that gave birth to the vision. Stay in intimacy with the Lord no matter how much or how little fruit you see, and let Him fulfill what He promised the way He promised it.
