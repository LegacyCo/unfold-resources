## Commentary
David knew that the Lord had established him. What set David apart is he never tried to establish himself...even though he was anointed by Samuel as king. And because he didn't establish himself, he didn't feel the anxiety of preserving himself once he was established (cf ==2 Samuel 15:25-26==).

In addition to the obvious example of Samuel anointing him king, consider also after Samuel's death the men of Judah came and anointed David king (==2 Samuel 2:4==). Also, all the tribe's of Israel came and anointed him king (==2 Samuel 5:1-3==). Even the king of Tyre acknowledged David's kingship and sent him materials and workmen to build his house.

How did David know the Lord had established him as king? Because everything he needed to be king the Lord gave him. And because the Lord gave it to him, it was the Lord's responsibility to preserve it all. David's task was simply to be faithful to God,

"Preserve me, O God, for in You I put all my trust" (==Psalm 16:1==).

"No king is saved by the multitude of an army...the eye of the Lord is on those who fear Him, who hope in His mercy" (==Psalm 33:16==, ==Psalm 33:18==).

"The steps of a good man are established by the Lord" (==Psalm 37:23==).
