## Commentary
There is a difference between being friendly and being trustworthy. What the world calls being friendly is often soulish, or carnal socializing, and the outward appearance of friendship. 
  
The showing forth of the outward appearance of friendship is not the same thing as being a real friend. We should use discernment to see who will be a consistent companion over time. Discernment will see through mere socializing.
