## Commentary
What temptation was Jesus speaking of? Was it the temptation to leave Him when the soldiers came to arrest Him? Was it the temptation to deny Him? Was it the temptation to lose hope and abandon each other when He was crucified?

The spirit is willing to watch and pray, to not enter into temptation, but the flesh is not. He is not talking about the Holy Spirit, He means the spirit of the disciples. The flesh must be overruled in instances like Gethsemane. There is nothing wrong with sleep, Jesus slept. But He knew the right time to sleep (in the boat during the storm even - see Matthew 8:24) and the right time to watch and pray. The disciples had not learned that yet.

## Cross Reference
Jesus uses this same language ("watch and pray") in Luke 21:36 when He is talking about being able to discern the end of the age, "Watch therefore, and pray always that you may have strength to escape all these things that will come to pass, and to stand before the Son of Man.”
