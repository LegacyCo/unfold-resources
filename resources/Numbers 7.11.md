## Commentary
The dedication offerings for the altar were brought over twelve days, one leader from each tribe bringing his offering on his appointed day. God could have wrapped this up quickly. The offerings were nearly identical. Scripture could have said, “Each leader brought the same offering,” and moved on.

But it doesn’t.

Numbers 7 repeats every offering in detail. Every plate, basin, animal, and incense dish is recorded tribe by tribe, leader by leader. What feels repetitive to us is personal to God. He sees each act of worship. He records each gift. He honors each tribe by name.

This is important because faithfulness over time can often feel mundane. Repetition is not meaningless when it is done before the Lord. Consistent obedience may not feel dramatic, but it is still seen by God.

## Application
There are offerings you bring that no one else may notice. Hidden faithfulness. Repeated obedience. The kind of giving and serving that looks ordinary from the outside. Numbers 7 reminds us that God does not overlook what is brought to Him in faith.
