## Commentary
“Even when we were dead in our trespasses, (God) made us alive together with Christ—by grace you have been saved.”

Paul describes the human condition with one word: dead.

Death leaves no resources for recovery. Life must come from another source. Resurrection begins entirely with the One who gives life.

God made us alive with Christ. He raised us with Christ. He seated us with Christ in the heavenly places (==Ephesians 2:5-6==). Paul places God at the center of every action.

Grace gathered us into the death and resurrection of Jesus. His death became the end of our life in Adam. His resurrection became the beginning of our life in God.

His salvation reaches far beyond the repair of a damaged life. We have received participation in the life of Christ. His Spirit lives within us. His relationship with the Father has become the ground of ours. His victory has become our inheritance.

Human effort may be able to shape habits, restrain impulses, and improve outward behavior...for a time, anyway. But true rebirth and resurrection power to live transformed lives is the gift of God.

## Application
Stop measuring the future by the strength you currently possess. Resurrection has never depended upon the resources of the dead. The Spirit who raised Jesus lives in you (==Romans 8:11==).

Expect His life to appear.
