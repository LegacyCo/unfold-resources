## Commentary
“He always lives to make intercession for them.”

Maybe you believe the victory of the cross, you’re living a life of repentance, you’re not harboring habitual sin—and the attacks still come. You believe in the authority of Jesus’ name, but you experience nightmares. You believe the cross disarmed the enemy, but the finances stay strained, the kids still aren’t saved, the healing won’t come, the tension in the marriage won’t lift.

In the thick of that battle, we usually reach for one of two explanations. We turn inward—“I must have done something wrong; this is my failure”—or we hand the devil the credit for our entire present reality. Neither posture leads to freedom. The answer to every storm is the same: Jesus, and the victory He accomplished on the cross. “Be strong in the Lord and in the power of His might” (==Ephesians 6:10==). The focus is never the devil. The focus is Jesus.

And here’s what this verse tells us Jesus is doing right now: interceding. At this moment there’s a Man seated at the right hand of the Father with scars on His body and a hole in His side—and those scars are the evidence of His victory (==Revelation 5:6==). He isn’t kicking back while angels feed Him grapes. He isn’t watching your struggle like entertainment, curious how you’ll figure it out. Jesus is not watching. He is working—actively, presently, continuously praying for you, the way He told Peter, “I have prayed for you, that your faith should not fail” (==Luke 22:32==).

So when it’s hard—when you want to give in to the temptation or quit altogether—remember who is praying for you, and from what position. You’re not enduring the battle alone while heaven observes. You’re being carried through it by a High Priest who is able to save to the uttermost those who come to God through Him.
