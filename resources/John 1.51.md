## Commentary
What Jesus is doing here is absolutely staggering. He's taking Nathanael - and all of us - back to Genesis 28, to Jacob's dream at Bethel. Jacob saw a ladder set up on earth with its top reaching to heaven, and the angels of God were ascending and descending on it (Genesis 28:12).

Jacob woke up and said, "Surely the Lord is in this place, and I did not know it..This is none other than the house of God, and this is the gate of heaven" (Genesis 28:16-17). But Jesus is now saying that He is the ladder. He is the meeting place between heaven and earth. He is the open door, the stairway, the access point where God and man come together. 

For generations, Israel thought the temple in Jerusalem was that place. The priests - men like Ezekiel - ordered their entire lives around it. But when judgment came and the people were exiled to Babylon, Ezekiel saw something that changed everything. He saw the glory of God in motion, not confined to the temple. He saw the throne of God — and on that throne, "a figure with the appearance of a man" (Ezekiel 1:26). Ezekiel was seeing what would one day become flesh and dwell among us (John 1:14). 

The glory that couldn't be contained in a building was always heading toward a body. Jesus is that body. He is the place where heaven touches earth.

This is why Jesus could tell the Samaritan woman, "The hour is coming when neither on this mountain nor in Jerusalem will you worship the Father" (John 4:21). The location doesn't matter anymore because the meeting place is now a Person. Jesus is the new Bethel, the new temple, the new *everything*. 

We are no longer exiles trying to find our way back to a place. We are sons and daughters living in union with the One who is Himself the way, the truth, and the life (John 14:6). In Christ are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge (Colossians 2:3). He is the access point to the Father. He is the open heaven.

So when Jesus tells Nathanael that he will see heaven opened and the angels ascending and descending on the Son of Man, He's saying, "Everything you've been waiting for, everything the temple was supposed to be, everything Jacob saw in that dream - it's all right here."

## Application
In Christ, heaven has already opened to you. Jesus is your access to the Father, and because you are in Him, you are no longer standing outside asking to come near. You have been brought near (Ephesians 2:13).

This changes the way you pray, worship, and move through ordinary life. You can meet with God in the sanctuary, but you can also meet with Him at the kitchen table, in the car, at work, or in the middle of suffering. The meeting place is not somewhere you travel to. The meeting place is Christ, and you are living in union with Him.

So stop searching for what you have already been given. Turn your attention to Jesus. Abide in Him. Speak to the Father with confidence. Live today with the awareness that wherever Christ is—and Christ is in you—heaven and earth have come together.
