## Commentary
While this passage (v1-10) is speaking of the future blessing of Israel returning to Zion from Exile, Jesus goes to this same region and demonstrates the kingdom to a Gentile people (see Matthew 15:21). Jesus brings this Gentile people the blessing of Isaiah 35, which was previously reserved for only Israel. 
## Application
"Zion" is available to all who are in "exile." The freedom that is found only in Christ is not reserved for a select few. Jesus came to set all captives free. The promise is that the "ransomed of the Lord shall come to Zion with singing and everlasting joy on their heads."
