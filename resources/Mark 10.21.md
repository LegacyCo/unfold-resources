## Commentary
Jesus showed love to the rich young ruler. The NASB20 translates this verse,
	"Looking at him, Jesus showed love to him and said to him, 'One thing you lack: go and sell all you possess and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven; and come, follow Me.'”
Jesus showed love towards the young man by pointing out the things that were hindering him from receiving that love: his possessions. His love for the young man was manifest as something that was difficult for the young man to hear, and even do, but it was love none the less. Jesus aimed His judgement at those things which had authority over the young man.

## Application
Where is Jesus wanting to show His love for you, and you are misinterpreting it as something other than love?
