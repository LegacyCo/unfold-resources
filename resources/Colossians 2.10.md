## Word Study
The NASB translates this verse, "you have been made complete." The word that Paul uses in v9 for "fullness" is the same root as the word he uses in v10 for "complete." The same fullness that dwells in Christ now fills *us* and completes *us*. 

Does the fullness of the Godhead dwell in Jesus? Does that same fullness now complete you? You can't answer "yes" for one and not the other. 

This also is the same word that Luke uses to describe how the Old Testament is fulfilled in Christ ("Today this Scripture is fulfilled in your hearing” - Luke 4:21).

Let's ask the question again; is the Old Covenant and it's law of works fulfilled in Christ? In the same way, does that same fullness now complete you? Again, you can't answer "yes" for one and not the other.

## Application
This fullness/completeness is a present reality for believers because it is a present reality for Jesus. We should grow in our understanding of what this means for us in this life, but the reality of it all is settled in heaven.
