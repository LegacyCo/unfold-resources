## Commentary
“Because the carnal mind is enmity against God; for it is not subject to the law of God, nor indeed can be.”

Most of us think the devil and his demons are our biggest enemy. They’re not. According to Paul, enemy #1 is the flesh. The carnal mind isn’t just weak toward God or distracted from God—it is *enmity* against God. Hostile by nature. It doesn’t submit to His rule, and Paul adds the devastating clause: “nor indeed can be.” The flesh can’t be discipled, medicated, or reformed into a friend of God. It only acts like what it is—because everything descended from Adam can only act like Adam.

Understand what the Bible means by “flesh.” It isn’t talking about your skin, and not only about obvious sins. Flesh is shorthand for everything descended from Adam as a result of sin—everything carnal, worldly, self-ruled, belonging to the kingdom of this age; everything Jesus died to take away from you. Spirit is the opposite category: everything descended from God, originating in heaven, belonging to the age to come—everything “in Christ.” And these two are not incompatible the way oil and water are, or cats and dogs. They’re incompatible the way darkness and light are. The way death and life are. Darkness and light can’t occupy the same space; a person can’t be simultaneously dead and alive. You are either in Adam or in Christ (==Romans 8:9==).

Here’s the good news of the new birth: the lost don’t have a choice when it comes to sin—they are entirely enslaved to the flesh. But if you believe that Jesus is who He says He is, His Spirit now lives in you, and for the first time you *can* be ruled and empowered by Someone other than your flesh (==Romans 8:2==). In this age of tension we still choose, daily, which one to walk by—the way a married man chooses daily to live like the husband he actually is, or to betray his own identity and live like someone he’s not (==Galatians 5:16==; ==Romans 6:12-13==). The flesh will keep making its petty demands for comfort and self-preservation. You just don’t answer to it anymore.

## Word Study
- *mind* : **phronēma** (Greek) — not a passing thought but a settled mindset; the whole bent and disposition of a person’s thinking; what the inner life is aimed at. This is the word behind “carnal mind” and “spiritual mind” throughout the passage.

Watch Paul use it three times in one chapter:

- ==Romans 8:6== — “to be carnally *minded* (phronēma) is death, but to be spiritually *minded* (phronēma) is life and peace.” Two mindsets, two destinations.
- ==Romans 8:27== — “He who searches the hearts knows what the *mind* (phronēma) of the Spirit is.” Even the Spirit has a phronēma—a settled aim—and He’s praying it over you.

Summary: the war between flesh and Spirit is fought at the level of the phronēma—the settled direction of your thinking. You don’t drift into the Spirit’s mindset; you set your mind there, and life and peace follow (==Colossians 3:2==).
