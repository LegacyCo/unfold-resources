## Commentary
The gospel begins with God’s kindness. Paul doesn't say that salvation began when we finally became serious about God, but when “the kindness and love of God our Savior appeared” (==Titus 3:4==). His kindness moved toward us before our obedience ever moved toward Him. As Paul writes elsewhere, it is the kindness of God that leads us to repentance (==Romans 2:4==).

His kindness did more than forgive us; it saved us through “the washing of regeneration and renewal of the Holy Spirit.”

The word regeneration is _palingenesia_, meaning a return, restoration, or new beginning. It could describe the end of captivity or the restoration of health after sickness. The word appears only twice in Scripture. Here, it describes the new life given to us by the Spirit. Its other occurrence is in ==Matthew 19:28==, where Jesus uses it to describe the renewal of all creation. The same God who will one day make the whole world new has already begun that work within us.

The word renewal is _anakainosis_, meaning to make new. It is a word found only in Christian literature and appears just twice in Scripture. Its other occurrence is in ==Romans 12:2==, where Paul speaks of being transformed by the renewal of the mind.

Regeneration gives us new life; renewal continually reshapes that life. God does not just restore us to what we were before sin damaged us. Through the Holy Spirit, He makes us new and continues making us new. Salvation is not simply the removal of guilt. It is the beginning of a new creation.

## Application
Don't settle for a version of Christianity that just manages your old life. The Holy Spirit has not come to help you behave better, but to make you new. Let His work reach your habits, desires, thought patterns, and reactions. Where you've settled and believe, “This is just who I am,” remember that regeneration means your past no longer has the final word, and renewal means God is still at work.

Bring the places in you that feel tired, damaged, or unchanged before the Holy Spirit. His kindness is not only what brought you into salvation; it is what continues leading you into transformation.
