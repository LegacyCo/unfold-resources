## Commentary
This passage shows that God's eternal plan is finished...and still unfolding. There is a past, present, and future reality of what the gospel accomplishes. When we repent: 
our sins are forgiven (past reality), 
times of refreshing come from the presence of the Lord (present reality), and 
Jesus returns (future reality).

## Word Study
-*repent*: metanoia - a change of mind
-*return*: epistrephō - to turn around, change direction
-*wipe away*: exaleiphō - obliterate, cancel or destroy, to remove as if by rubbing (see also Rev 2:14 - Jesus wipes away every tear, Rev 3:5 - names are erased from the book of life, Col 2:14 - our debts are canceled)
-*refreshing*: anapsuxis - recovery of breath, rest, revival
-*presence*: prosōpon - the presence or proximity of someone understood in terms of the face; with the implication of being in front of them

Summary: Repentance is a holistic act, meaning it involves the entire person; mind and body. When we change the way we think about God, to such a degree that it changes the direction we're moving, our sins are removed. The change of direction (returning) also positions us in front of the Lord (face-to-face). And in His presence is fullness of joy, a total restoration back to our original design.
