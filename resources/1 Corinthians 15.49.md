## Commentary
“Just as we have borne the image of the earthy, we will also bear the image of the heavenly.”

Your first birth gave you a family likeness.

You inherited physical characteristics from your parents, but the family line reaches farther back than them. Every human life traces itself to Adam. We bear the image of the earthly man: mortal, corruptible, and wounded by a world in rebellion against God.

Then Jesus came as the last Adam.

He entered Adam’s family without participating in Adam’s sin. He died the death that belonged to Adam’s children, but death could not hold a righteous Man in the grave. The Spirit raised Him, and He became “a life-giving spirit” (==1 Corinthians 15:45==).

Now everyone joined to Christ receives a new family likeness. The Spirit is conforming us to the image of the heavenly Man, from glory to glory (==2 Corinthians 3:18==). What begins through the new birth will be completed in the resurrection, when we bear His image without corruption, weakness, or death.

Your natural family may explain certain patterns in your past, but it no longer has final authority over your future. Addiction may have traveled through generations. Anger may have filled your childhood home. Fear, poverty, divorce, or despair may appear repeatedly in your family’s story. Those patterns can be real without remaining sovereign.

You have been grafted into a new family. The old lineage no longer possesses the authority to tell you what kind of person you must become.

This does not mean every struggle disappears the moment you are born again. But it does mean every struggle has lost the right to define you. You now fight from a new identity, with a new Spirit at work within you, toward a likeness that God Himself has promised to complete (==Romans 8:29==).

The first Adam gave you an image you could not escape. The last Adam gives you an image no enemy can prevent.

## Application
Stop using your family history as a prophecy over your life.

You may have learned anger, fear, manipulation, or self-destruction in the house where you were raised, but you have now been raised with Christ. Acknowledge the reality of what happened without granting it the authority to determine what happens next.

Your inheritance in Christ is stronger than anything passed down through Adam. Bear the likeness of your new family.
