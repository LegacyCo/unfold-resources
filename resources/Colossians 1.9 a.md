## Commentary
The first thing Paul mentions when he prays for the Colossians is how he is ceaseless in praying they be "filled with the knowledge of God's will." This is the same thing Paul prays for the Ephesians (Ephesians 1:9, 17). Praying to be filled with the knowledge of God's will is an apostolic prayer. It's a prayer we must pray for ourselves and for the church. Knowing God's will means we understand His plan, His purpose, and His ways. It means we have our eyes fixed on eternal things (2 Corinthians 4:18) and are not swayed by the circumstances around us that would be opposed to His will.

## Application
Paul’s prayer goes deeper than relief from present circumstances. He is asking that the church would become so filled with the knowledge of God’s will that they can see beyond the moment they are standing in.

Before you ask God to fix your circumstances, ask Him to fill you with the knowledge of His will.
