## Commentary
These cravings of the people caused the anger of the Lord to be aroused, yet He performed an incredible miracle to satisfy their cravings — a miracle so great that Moses thought it would be impossible (see v21-23). The miracle is not an endorsement of the way the mixed multitude was behaving. 

## Application
We can't assume that a miracle is an endorsement of character. The miracle is evidence of a living, powerful, sovereign God who intervenes in the lives of people out of the goodness of who He is.
