## Commentary
The Old Testament sacrificial system was designed in such a way that the people of Israel were naturally induced to corporate meals together which celebrated the sacrifice. The freewill peace offering mentioned in this passage is said to then be "eaten the same day that you offer it, and on the next day." The Lord says the same thing in Leviticus 7:15-16. It was impossible for one family to eat that much meat. Gathering other families to eat the sacrifice would have been common for Israel. It was a meal together with the family of God in celebration of the sacrifice.
