## Commentary
The word we translate "own possession" (or "peculiar people" in some translations) is the Greek word pĕripŏiēsis. Pĕripŏiēsis does not mean peculiar or weird. It means one exclusively belonging to another. And today, that makes you weird. As I heard Damon Thompson say, "It makes you subversive. It makes you counter-cultural because you're supposed to give a little bit of yourself to your job, and a little bit of yourself to your family, and a little bit of yourself to your hobbies, and a little bit of yourself to vacation, and a little bit of yourself to you...and we end up spread very thin." Pĕripŏiēsis is saying what we are really designed for is to belong exclusively to God. And when we live as though we belong exclusively to Him, all that other stuff we try to give ourselves to fades away and the things that are left become part of a life lived in worship of the One we belong to.
