## Commentary
Elijah prayed for rain, sent his servant to look toward the sea, and heard the report, "There is nothing"...six times. Are we able to bear the report, *there is nothing*? Are we able to bear that report six times and still kneel in faith? Or are we of the sort whose faith can't handle reports that contradict what our faith contends for? Do we have kingdom vision that sees what can't be seen (2 Corinthians 4:18)? 

When the verifiable evidence says, *there is nothing*, what do we do? When the facts contradict the truth of who God is, what do we do? When Lazarus is in the grave and the third day is passed, what do we do? When the Prince of Peace is silently sleeping while the storm is closing in on us, what do we do? We hit our knees a seventh time. We are not of the sort who let facts determine truth. Truth has no expiration date. Truth is not circumstantial. 

God has no problem with the clock striking twelve. We use time to tell us if the prayer worked; the medicine arrived just in time, the check showed up just in time, the breakthrough came just in time. God uses time to sift the soul, to determine if faith remains; 
"Lord, if You would've been here in time, my brother would not have died" (John 11:21). 

"He who believes in Me will never die...do you believe this?" 

"Lord, I did believe, and so did Lazarus...and He's now four days in the grave. That's fact." 

"It may be a fact, but it's not truth. *I* am truth. And I am resurrection. Now watch what happens when faith brings facts face to face with Truth."
