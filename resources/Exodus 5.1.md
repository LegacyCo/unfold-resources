## Commentary
God had every intention of giving His people their inheritance in the Promised Land, but He commanded Pharaoh to let Israel go so she could worship, feast, and enjoy God's presence. As Samuel Whitefield writes in "Will You Choose the Wilderness," "Communion was first, then inheritance. Relationship was on God's mind before assignment."
