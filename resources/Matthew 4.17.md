## Commentary
A lot of people confuse repentance with feelings of remorse. They feel bad, cry, promise to do better, and then go right back to the same patterns because nothing actually changed at the level of surrender. But repentance is not regret. It's the moment a man stops trusting his own judgment as final. Repentance is the surrender of the throne.

True repentance means I stop defending the way I’ve been living. I stop negotiating with sin. I stop treating God like an advisor and start receiving Him as Lord.

True repentance will show up in concrete ways. If I've been managing my life apart from God, repentance means I bring my decisions under His authority. If I've believed lies about myself, others, or the world, repentance means I let truth confront and reorder me. If I've been walking in pride, independence, bitterness, lust, greed, or unbelief, repentance means I turn from those things and begin walking another way.

Repentance is the ongoing posture of a disciple. It's how we keep coming out of agreement with lies and back into agreement with God.
