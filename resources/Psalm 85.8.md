## Commentary
The restored life is sustained by the ongoing voice of God.

The psalmist doesn’t say, “I heard what God spoke.” He says, “I will hear what God the LORD will speak.” This is not the memory of a word once spoken. It's the posture of a people who know they must continue listening.

And what does He speak? Peace.

“He will speak peace to His people and to His saints.” The verb “speak” is written in the imperfect tense, meaning it continues with ongoing action that never stops. The Psalmist writes that He longs to turn his ear to what the Lord is saying because he knows the Lord is always speaking unending peace to His people.

God speaks peace again and again to the people who belong to Him. He speaks peace over their fear. He speaks peace over their shame. He speaks peace over the places where captivity left a mark. He speaks peace until the people who were restored learn how to live restored.

But then comes the warning: “Let them not turn back to folly.”

Why? Because folly begins where listening ends. The danger for God’s people is not simply that they once wandered. It's that they stop listening and drift back into the patterns that made them captive in the first place.

God’s peace is a living word we continually receive. His peace keeps reordering us, anchoring us, and bringing our hearts back into alignment with Him.

This is why Psalm 85:8 sits at the center of the psalm. The land has been favored. The captivity of Jacob has been turned. Salvation is near. Glory is ready to dwell in the land. Truth is ready to spring out of the earth. The land is ready to yield its increase.

But the people must keep listening to the voice of the Lord.

As long as they remain attentive to His voice, they remain aligned with His ways. And as they remain aligned with His ways, the land under their possession begins to bear witness to the peace spoken over them.

## Application
The restored life isn’t sustained by memory alone, but by ongoing communion. So keep listening, keep receiving His peace, and don’t turn back to the patterns He already brought you out of.
