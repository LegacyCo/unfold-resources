## Commentary
Word study of Philippians 4:12:

- Content: showing satisfaction with things as they are, the attitude that is satisfied with what is available.

- Abased: to be humbled, humiliated, made low, especially in regard to the basic needs of life.

- Abound: to be in a more prosperous condition, to have left over, exceeding the usual number or size
