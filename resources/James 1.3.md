## Commentary
Faith, like anything else that is intended to grow, grows under tension — like muscles that are torn in exercise and repaired stronger or roots that gain strength as they push against rock. In the same way, if the trials never come, faith has nothing to push against. The trial is the opportunity faith needs to stretch and press and experience time under tension.

Consider the tiny mustard seed. That seed will never grow unless it's buried under the weight of soil. Soil suffocates and presses against the seed. It cuts off oxygen and light. But thankfully those things aren't needed in that season. What's needed is pressure. Single-minded focus on one thing, breaking thru. What's needed is for the seed to know who it is. If the seed thinks its something other than a seed, it will see the soil is an enemy trying to choke it out. But as long as the seed knows it's a seed, it will recognize the dirt as an opportunity for increase. In this regard, identity is essential for faith to grow.

## Application
If we don't know who we are as believers, we will be confused when trials come. That confusion will bring in "two thoughts" that cause our faith to stop growing and become doubt. But when we know that trials are also part of our inheritance, and are for our good, then we will have the mind of the mustard seed that welcomes the pressure, knowing the pressure is what will one day produce the fruit we were designed for.
