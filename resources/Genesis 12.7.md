## Commentary
Depending on how you count them, Scripture records seven covenants (though some only count five):
  1. Creation (by inference and language)
  2. Adamic 
  3. Noahic 
  4. Abrahamic 
  5. Mosaic 
  6. Davidic 
  7. Christ
