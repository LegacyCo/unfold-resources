## Commentary
Jesus has given us the gift of the table to celebrate...a celebration that He Himself is fasting until "that day" when the kingdom of God has come in its fullness. Far be it that we should take lightly the gift that He has given, truly given, meaning He no longer has it. For the sake of honoring this great gift, communion should be celebrated with joy and shared often.
