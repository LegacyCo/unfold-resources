## Commentary
The scribes and Pharisees come to Jesus asking why His disciples "transgress the tradition of the elders" not washing their hands when they eat bread (v2). In Jesus response, He tells them that by honoring their own tradition and not keeping the command of the Lord (to honor your father and mother) they have "made the word (logos) of God of no effect (*ekyrosate*)." Ekyrosate means to invalidate the authority of, to render ineffective. A tradition of man, which refused to give honor where God desires honor to be given, had the ability to invalidate the authority of the word of God and render it void of power.

## Cross Refernce
Other examples of this:
	- Jeroboam (1 Kings 11:38)- God gives Jeroboam a word, "Then it shall be, that if you listen to all that I command you and walk in My ways, and do what is right in My sight by keeping My statutes and My commandments, as My servant David did, then I will be with you and build you an enduring house as I built for David, and I will give Israel to you." But Jeroboam did not take heed to what the word said (see 1 Kings 12:25ff). The enduring house was cut off forever (see 1 Kings 14:7ff).
	- King Saul (1 Samuel 9:16) - God gives King Saul a word; he will save Israel from the Philistines. But Saul did not take heed to the word (see 1 Samuel 13:9). His kingdom was taken away (see 1 Samuel 13:14) and he was killed by the very people the Lord said he would save Israel from (see 1 Samuel 31:1ff).
	- Jesus says in the telling of the parable of the sower that the seed sown among thorns are those who hear the word (*logos*) but the "cares of this world, the deceitfulness of riches, and the desires for other things choke the word (*logos*) and it becomes unfruitful (*akarpos* - useless, barren)" (Mark 4:18-19).
