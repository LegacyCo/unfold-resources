## Commentary
“From Him the whole body, joined and knit together by what every joint supplies… causes growth of the body for the edifying of itself in love.”

Read that carefully, because it’s huge. Growth doesn’t just come *to* the body. Growth happens *within* the body—when every part does its share. Christ is the Head and the source (“from Him”), but the supply lines of His life run through the joints: the touch-points where your life connects to mine.

That means you carry something I need. And I carry something you need. The encouragement, the correction, the prayer, the presence in a hard week—these aren’t extras on top of the spiritual life. They’re the ligaments through which the Head supplies the body.

This is why independence is a lie the body can’t afford. The Spirit may fill you alone, but He forms you in community. Leaders may equip you with truth, but the body gives you the space to practice it (==Ephesians 4:11-12==). A joint that withholds its supply doesn’t just miss out—it starves the parts it was meant to feed (==1 Corinthians 12:21==).

Walking worthy of the calling isn’t a solo assignment. Paul’s language in this chapter is stubbornly plural—one body, one Spirit, one hope, one Lord, one faith, one baptism (==Ephesians 4:4-6==). So the question isn’t only “Am I walking worthy?” It’s “Are *we*?”

## Application
Name what your joint supplies—and where it connects. If you don’t know what you carry for the body, ask the people who know you; they usually do. Then refuse to live a life of unconnected Christianity: show up, supply, receive. A body grows in love when every part does its share—starting with yours.
