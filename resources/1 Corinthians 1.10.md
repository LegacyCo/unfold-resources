## Commentary
Paul's strong plea with the church of Corinth to "speak the same thing" brings to mind the unity of the people of Babel. God says of them in Genesis 11:6, "Behold, they are one people, and they all have the same language. And this is what they have started to do, and now nothing which they plan to do will be impossible for them." Unity breaks through the wall of impossibility.
