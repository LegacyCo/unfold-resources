## Commentary
There's a similar story in the Bible of someone's eyes being opened after they eat: "Then the eyes of both of them were opened, and they knew that they were naked; and they sewed fig leaves together and made themselves coverings" (Genesis 3:7).

There is a type of "eating" that joins us to the source of what we are eating. This is why Paul could say to the Corinthians, "are not those who eat of the sacrifice partakers of the altar?" (1 Corinthians 10:18) Eating from the altar opens our eyes to know spiritual realities empowered by that altar. We are either blessed by of the altar we eat from (like the disciples on the road to Emaus) or we are cursed by the altar we eat from (Adam & Eve). 

In this story, the disciples eyes were opened to "know *Him*." Jesus was the source of the table they were eating from, and Jesus was the revelation they received.

In Genesis 3, Adam and Eve's eyes were opened to know *themselves* (they were naked). Deception was the source of that table, and self centeredness became the revelation they received. Shame and fear was the result.

It was God's design that eating would open our eyes. As long as Adam and Eve at from the Tree of Life, their eyes would be open to what God was teaching them about life and His creation. But when they ate from the Tree of Knowledge, their eyes were opened to things they weren't yet ready to handle. Jesus redeems what we have seen as a result of eating from the wrong tree by becoming living bread for us to feast on.
