## Commentary
David refused to drink the water because he understood what it had cost his men to get it. Three warriors had risked their lives by breaking through enemy lines simply to satisfy his thirst. Since they could have died bringing it to him, drinking it would have felt like satisfying his own desire at the expense of their lives. Instead, David poured the water out before the LORD as an act of worship. By pouring it out, he symbolically gave to God what he considered too precious to consume himself. We may look at that as waste. But by turning the water into worship, David honored the sacrifice the men made by turning their costly gift into an offering to God.
