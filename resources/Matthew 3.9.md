## Commentary
Paul writes in Galatians 3:29, "If you are Christ's, then you are Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise." The issue is do you belong to *Christ*. Not a patriarch, not a particular tribe...the Son of God. 

One of the scandals of the new covenant is family lineage has no bearing, only faith in Jesus. The Pharisee's were confident in their pedigree, among other things. John laid the axe of truth at the root of that tree of deception. 

We can be good stewards of those who come after us and ensure that they have every benefit of being raised in an atmosphere of God's presence and the reality of His kingdom, but we will stand before the Lord as individuals, not tribes.

The same can also be said in reverse. The dysfunction, trauma, lies, and atmosphere of godlessness you may have been raised in do not define you. Faith in Jesus does. Those things may have shaped you, but faith in Christ turns the pain of an unrighteous family lineage into a testimony of His grace.
