## Commentary
Job's perspective was "the Lord gives and the Lord takes away." Abraham's perspective was "the Lord gives, the Lord takes away, and the Lord gives back again." Praise God we are children of Abraham and not Job (Galatians 3:7 - "It is those who are of faith who are sons of Abraham.")
