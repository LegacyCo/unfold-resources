## Commentary
I heard Damon Thompson say this: All along, Jesus told His disciples they had to love each other. So why does he later on call loving one another a new commandment? Because he changes the standard from loving each other the way *you* should love each other, to loving each other the way you've been loved by Jesus. That's a game changer. I don't get to love you the way that I know how to love. And I don't get to love you the way I think you deserve to be loved. I have to love you the way that Jesus loved me. And he said, that's so revolutionary that I'm going to call it a new commandment. It's the same commandment with a brand new standard. And the new standard is I have to feel about you the way I believe Jesus feels about me. And He forgave me over and over again. So I get to forgive you over and over again.
