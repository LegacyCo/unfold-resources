## Commentary
This is a quote by N.T. Wright from His book *How God Became King*: The common assumption is that the ‘coming of the son of man' in the New Testament refers to the "coming" *to* earth by one who is presently in heaven. To think this way ignores the reference Jesus is making here to ==Daniel 7:13-14== which clearly indicates that the direction of travel is, so to speak, upward, not downward. In Daniel, "one like a son of man," in other words, "a human figure," "comes" from earth to heaven to be presented before the "Ancient of Days," to be given dominion, glory, and a kingdom. It is a move from suffering and humiliation to enthronement and sovereignty. The kingdom has come and the King is seated on His throne.
