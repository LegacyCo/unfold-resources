## Commentary
Jesus frees His people from anything that would hold them down, which means we can now set our minds on "things above." This is only possible because Jesus has overcome gravity. And we have the mind of Christ. 

Consider Paul's words in 1 Corinthians 2:9-16 - No one knows the things of God except the Spirit of God. And we have been given the Spirit so that we might know the things that have been freely given to us. The carnal man cannot know the things of the Spirit because they are spiritually discerned. Not only are we "seated in heavenly places" (Ephesians 2:6), but we have the ability to meditate on things the world has no access to. 

Paul tells the Ephesians not to walk as the rest of the Gentiles walk, "in the futility of their mind, having their understanding darkened, being alienated from the life of God, because of the ignorance that is in them" (Ephesians 4:17-18). The world is led by the futility of their mind. Their understanding is darkened. Ignorance is in them. Christ has set us free to now think thoughts the Holy Spirit can say "Amen" to.
