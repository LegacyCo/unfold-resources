## Commentary
In Christ, God’s grace is given as a precious gift. It is not something we attain or earn. It is not something we possess on our own, it is a gift that God lavishes. And outside of God there can be no talk of grace (see also John 1:16-17 - "The Law was given through Moses; grace and truth came to be through Jesus Christ"). Edit check test
