## Commentary
The Old Testament makes multiple mention of God's people praying towards the temple/sanctuary and God hearing their prayers. Consider that Jesus is the temple, the sanctuary...and what was prescribed in the Old Testament is fulfilled in the New. Now our prayers are not aimed at a building in a city but at a Savior on a throne.

See ==John 2:19-21== and ==Hebrews 4:14-16==
