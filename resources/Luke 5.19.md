## Commentary
How tragic it is when spectators block the way to Jesus. What does it mean to be a spectator? The scribes and the Pharisees were present in this moment while Jesus was teach. But they were spectating, reasoning among themselves, "sitting by" but not sitting at His feet (see v17). Their attendance blocked the way of others who needed to get to Jesus. 

Religion will always block the way of those who need God. It presents itself as the true representation of all that is godly, as the way for the sinner to come to God, but in reality all religion does is keep people from actually getting to God.

Matthew Henry comments on this verse, "for they _sat by_ as _persons unconcerned_, as if the word of Christ were nothing to them. They sat by as spectators, censors, and spies, to pick up something on which to ground a reproach or accusation. How many are there in the midst of our assemblies, where the gospel is preached, that do not _sit under_ the word, but _sit by!_ It is to them as a _tale_ that is _told them_, not as a _message_ that is _sent them;_ they are willing that we should preach _before them_, not that we should preach _to them_."

However, the truly hungry *will* find their way to God, even if they have to pull the roof off. 

In this passage, the friends of the crippled man didn't wait, they *had* to get to Jesus. We cannot afford to wait, either. If the Holy Spirit is provoking us, if there is a grace to be reconciled to God, it must be seized upon..."while it is still day"..."wake up O sleeper" (Ephesians 5:14)

## Application
Has your way to Jesus been blocked? By what...religion, church, sickness, busyness, apathy? Bypass the door and tear the roof off. Go after God no matter what. The hungry are fed. Will He find faith in the earth when comes? Will He find faith in you when He comes. 

"What does faith look like?" I know one thing, tearing the roof of to get to Jesus is a pretty good picture.
