## Commentary
This is from Humility by Andrew Murray: Accept with gratitude everything that God allows from within or without, from friend or enemy, in nature or in grace, to remind you of your need of humbling, and to help you to it. Believe humility to indeed be the highest virtue, your very first duty before God, and the one perpetual safeguard of the soul. Set your heart upon it as the source of all blessing. The promise is divine and sure: "He that humbles himself shall be exalted" (==Matthew 23:12==). See that you do the one thing God asks: humble yourself. God will see that He does the one thing He has promised. He will give more grace; He will exalt you in due time. 

## Application
Don't despise the place of brokenness. God is high and holy, yet He chooses to dwell with the contrite and humble in spirit. The heart that admits its need, releases its pride, and turns fully toward Him becomes a place where His presence rests.

So when God exposes pride, weakness, or sin, don't harden yourself or rush to appear strong. Let Him bring you low enough to heal you. Humility is not the end of hope; it is often the place where God begins to revive the heart.
