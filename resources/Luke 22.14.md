## Commentary
This is from "The Day the Revolution Began" by N.T. Wright: When Jesus wanted to explain to his followers what his forthcoming death was all about, he did not give them a theory, a model, a metaphor, or any other such thing; he gave them a meal, a Passover meal, or at least what they seem to have thought was a Passover meal, though it turned out to be significantly different. Instead of looking back fifteen hundred years or so to the great event of the Exodus from Egypt - though that inevitably remained in the forefront of everyone's minds on that day - he turned the meal around so that its primary significance looked forward to what was going to happen the next day.
