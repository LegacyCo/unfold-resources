## Word Study
The Greek wording in this verse ("called saints") is the same as v1, "Paul, called an apostle of Jesus Christ." The Greek word for saint is *hagios*. Hagios means holy, consecrated, sanctified. Just as Paul is given the calling as an apostle, all who believe in Jesus are given the identity of saint — holy.
