## Commentary
Art Katz writes this in "Apostolic Foundations" - "For yet in a very little while, He who is coming will come, and will not delay" (v37). Here comes the punch-line: "Now the just shall live by faith." Having just written about what comes after the inheritance and the reward, the writer brings the subject into the immediate, present now. In other words, "The just shall live by this eschatological faith in the present now." Will the Lord find this kind of faith upon the earth when He returns?
