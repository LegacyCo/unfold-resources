## Commentary
This verse has echoes of ==Genesis 1:2==, but it’s as if you’re looking in one of those warped carnival mirrors; you see the recognizable form but its distorted.

The earth in no longer void, but now it’s filled with corruption and violence. Creation has now slipped into ruin. Humanity isn’t just _not_ bearing God’s image, humanity is actually slipping deeper into ruin. That’s the literal translation of this verse, ”The earth was on its way to ruin.” The only course of action for Yahweh is to destroy what no longer bears His image.
