## Commentary
Israel experienced a mini-revival under king Hezekiah. The people willingly contributed to the sacrifices and offerings for the service of the house of God. The temple was able to restored and "set it in order." This restoration was made possible because of the work God had done in His people. The move of God in Hezekiah's day had prepared them to restore the ultimate house: God's house. 

## Application
When God moves in our day, there will be many benefits. One of those is God's house, His people, will be restored and set in order.
