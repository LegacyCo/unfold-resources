## Commentary
Imagine how the presence of God must have changed everything in Obed Edom's home: how he talked to his wife and his children, how he spent his time, his conversations with his guests, what he thought about — every aspect of his life was touched by the fact that God's presence was dwelling in his home.

## Application
Paul writes in 1 Corinthians 6:19, "Do you not know that your body is the temple of the Holy Spirit who is in you, whom you have from God, and you are not your own?" When the presence of God is truly welcomed into a life, it doesn't stay confined to a prayer closet or Sunday morning service. The reality of God's presence touches everything—how you speak, how you love, how you spend your time, what fills your mind, and the atmosphere you create around you. Obed Edom’s house was blessed because God’s presence was not treated like furniture; it became the defining reality of the home. The real question is not whether we visit God’s presence now and then, but whether His presence is shaping the way we actually live.
