## Commentary
Wisdom rests quietly in the heart of the discerning. It doesn't need to announce itself, prove itself, or force itself into every conversation. The wise carry understanding inwardly and release it when the moment requires it.

Foolishness works differently. It quickly reveals itself because the fool cannot keep from speaking, reacting, and making himself known. What is hidden in the heart eventually becomes visible through the mouth.

Wisdom is often quiet before it is visible. It listens, observes, waits, and considers. Its silence is restraint. The wise understand that having something to say doesn't always mean it needs to be said.

What rests in the heart will eventually reveal the condition of the person. Wisdom produces patience, measured words, and discernment. Folly exposes itself through impulse.
