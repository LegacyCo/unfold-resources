## Commentary
The Hebrew word that we translate "tested" here is nāsâ - to exercise, to train, to conduct a test to ascertain the nature of something. For example, the queen of Sheba *tested* Solomon to verify or disprove what was said about him (1 Kings 10:1)d. David tested Saul's armor and found that it was not for him (1 Samuel 17:38-39). Daniel's diet of vegetable's was tested by the king's attendant for 10 days to determine whether it was good or not (Daniel 1:14).

We are not to test God, but He does test His people. While the wicked despise the testing of the Lord, the righteous welcome it. David writes in Psalm 26:2, "Examine me, LORD, and put me to the test; Refine my mind and my heart." The righteous know that the Lord's testing of us:

-Refines us. In Exodus 20:20 at the Mount Sinai incident, Moses tells a terrified Israel, "Do not be afraid; for God has come in order to test you, and in order that the fear of Him may remain with you, so that you will not sin."
-Exposes areas of weakness or unbelief. In Deuteronomy 8:2, God reminded Israel that they should "remember that the LORD your God led you these forty years in the wilderness, to humble you and test you, to know what was in your heart, whether you would keep His commandments or not." Did God need to know what was in Israel's heart, or did Israel need to know what was in their heart?
-Strengthens us in the same way our muscles are strengthened when they are tested in exercise. 

Here, in Genesis 22, God is refining Abraham, He is exposing any areas of weakness or unbelief, and He is strengthening him.

This is not what the enemy does. The enemy *tempts*. When Jesus was led out into the wilderness by the Spirit, Matthew, Mark, and Luke say that He was "tempted by the devil" (for example, see Matthew 4:1).

The real test for Abraham was not so much the sacrificing of his beloved son, although that test was very real. The bigger test was whether Abraham will sacrifice the one person who can perpetuate the promises of God. Will Abraham sacrifice the promise that God made him?
