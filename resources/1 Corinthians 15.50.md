## Commentary
“Flesh and blood cannot inherit the kingdom of God; nor does the perishable inherit the imperishable.”

The problem is not that God’s kingdom is closed. The problem is that the life we inherited through Adam is incapable of entering it.

The kingdom belongs to the King, and everything within it bears His nature. It is a kingdom of humility because the King is humble. It is a kingdom of love because the King is love. It is free from death because the King has conquered death. Nothing produced by sin, fear, pride, corruption, or self-preservation can survive within the realm of His reign.

This is why Jesus said we must be born *again* to see the kingdom (==John 3:3==). The kingdom requires a kind of life that the first birth can never produce. Adam cannot be trained into inheriting Christ. The perishable must put on the imperishable, and the mortal must put on immortality (==1 Corinthians 15:53==).

Paul is not teaching that physical existence is evil or that God intends to discard the body. The resurrection of Jesus proves the opposite. God’s answer to mortal humanity is transformation. The body sown in weakness will be raised in power; what is sown natural will be raised spiritual (==1 Corinthians 15:43-44==).

That transformation begins when the Spirit joins us to the risen Christ and will be completed when our bodies are raised in His likeness. The kingdom is inherited by people who have received the life of the King.

Everything Jesus defeated stays outside the gates. Death stays outside. Sin stays outside. Fear stays outside. The old life of Adam stays outside. To enter this kingdom, we must receive a life in which those enemies have already been overcome.

He is the kingdom. To inherit His kingdom is to share His life.

## Application
Pay attention to what you are still trying to carry through the gates.

Any identity rooted in fear, pride, resentment, addiction, family history, or self-preservation belongs to the life that cannot inherit the kingdom. Bring it into agreement with the cross. You have been transferred from the domain of darkness into the kingdom of God’s beloved Son (==Colossians 1:13==). Live according to the realm you now inhabit.
