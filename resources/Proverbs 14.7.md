## Commentary
Wisdom knows when to walk away. Proverbs tells us to leave the presence of a foolish person when we recognize that knowledge and understanding are not in them. This is more than avoiding people we disagree with. It's refusing to remain under the influence of someone committed to foolishness.

Association shapes us. The voices we continually listen to, the attitudes we tolerate, and the conversations we repeatedly entertain begin to form our thinking. Foolishness is rarely content to remain private; it spreads through counsel, humor, offense, gossip, and compromise.

The wise can love the foolish without joining them in their foolishness. They can pray for them, speak truth to them, and show kindness toward them, while still establishing distance from their influence. Walking away is not always rejection. Sometimes it is discernment.

There are places wisdom cannot grow because foolishness is continually being entertained. The wise recognize when a conversation, relationship, or environment is pulling them away from truth, and they have the courage to leave.
