## Commentary
It was the bridegroom who received the credit for the wine, not Jesus. If this was the first sign of Jesus manifesting His glory, it was by no means a public demonstration. It was restricted to the waiters and the disciples. 

Only those who follow Him closely know that Jesus is Lord of water and wine, the secret provider of food and drink at every table and at every celebration. He is the true giver of true food and drink.
