## Commentary
We see here the pattern of forerunning, of making ready the way of the Lord. Jesus gives His disciples specific details of what to look for and what to do. They will find a "large upper room, furnished and prepared." So really, who prepared the room, the disciples or Jesus?

## Application
We simply follow His lead as He guides us. As Paul says in 1 Corinthians 15:10, "I have labored more than them all, yet not I but the grace of the Lord which was with me."
