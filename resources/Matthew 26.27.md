## Commentary
This is from "The Table of the Lord" by Arthur Wallis: We bring bread and wine and get back Christ. God uses common things as channels of His revelation. 

The act of eating can make God more real than a stained glass window. The act of drinking can make God more real than a 50-voiced choir. There is nothing too ordinary to be used by Him. All of life is sacred and holy.

The simplicity of Christ comes to its crown in the feast of the Lord's Supper. There is no gorgeous rite or showy ceremony. There is nothing of that many-coloured pagenatry that had once been needed to attract the world. A cup of wine and a piece of broken bread - these are the seals and symbols of the gospel. 

I never feel the simplicity of God so powerfully and so freshly as when I sit at the communion table. 

There are great mysteries in our redemption. There are deep things that even angels cannot fathom. But in the center is a fact so simple that its best ritual is bread and wine.
