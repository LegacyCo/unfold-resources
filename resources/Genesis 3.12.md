## Commentary
Billy Sunday once said, “An excuse is the skin of a reason stuffed with a lie.”

A simple yes from Adam would have been the right answer to God’s question. But Adam became defensive. He points the finger of blame at the woman, “She…gave me the fruit.” And as if that wasn't enough, he also puts blame on God, “The woman whom *You* gave me...”

Just a few verses ago Adam was grateful for this precious gift that God had given him, "Bone of my bone, flesh of my flesh!” But now Adam says the woman the Lord “gave him" turned and "gave *him*" the forbidden fruit. 

The implication is incredible: God is ultimately responsible for Adam's deception because God's gift was a mistake...she was responsible for his downfall. The tree of knowledge has taught Adam how to be the victim, and to make God and the woman the guilty ones.
