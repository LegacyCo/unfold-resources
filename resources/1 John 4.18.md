## Commentary
“There is no fear in love; but perfect love casts out fear, because fear involves torment. But he who fears has not been made perfect in love.”

Fear and love cannot occupy the same house, and John tells us why: fear carries with it the threat of punishment. As long as a believer relates to God braced for punishment, the Father's love cannot be fully received—love must be embraced for fear to be cast out. This is precisely the leaven religion works to keep in us. If the spirit of religion cannot keep you from Jesus, it will settle for keeping you from the Father—keeping just enough of Adam's fear alive that you never quite run into His lap (==Genesis 3:10==).

Jesus told a parable about where that fear leads. A nobleman entrusted his servants with his resources; two traded and gained, but the third buried his mina in a handkerchief and explained why: “I feared you, because you are an austere man” (==Luke 19:20-21==). The servant imagined the master to be something he was not—the master who entrusted his wealth to servants and rewarded faithfulness with cities (==Luke 19:17==) was nothing like the austere man the servant had invented. And the master's response is sobering: “Out of your own mouth I will judge you” (==Luke 19:22==). The servant's fear determined his fate—not the character of the master he served. So it is with every believer who lives braced for punishment: the fear does not describe God; it constructs a false god, and then the believer lives under the false god's economy. The remedy is not trying harder to be unafraid. It is being made perfect in love—letting the actual character of the Father, revealed in Christ, displace the imagined one (==Romans 8:15==).

## Application
Ask the servant's question of yourself: what kind of master do you believe you serve? Listen to your instincts after you fail—do you move toward God or brace for punishment? Bracing is the tell that a false, austere master still lives in your imagination. Bring that image to the light, compare it against the Father Jesus revealed, and receive His love precisely at the point of failure. Perfect love is what evicts the fear; nothing else can.
