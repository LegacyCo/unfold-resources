## Commentary
Peter reminds every spiritual leader that the people they lead belong to God, not to them. Anyone entrusted with authority must remember that they are handling what belongs to Him.

As Watchman Nee writes in *Spiritual Authority*, “How serious will be the judgment upon those who grab God’s authority with their carnal hands. May we fear authority as we fear the fire of hell. To represent God is not an easy thing; it is too great and too marvelous for us to touch.”

This is why spiritual authority should never be grasped with carnal ambition. It must be carried with fear and trembling, not used as a means of control, self-promotion, or personal importance. The path is obedience, not authority. It is to become a servant, not to fight to be the head; to become a slave, not to seek to rule.

Moses and David carried enormous authority, yet neither became great by trying to establish his own position. Their authority was formed through hiddenness, surrender, suffering, and obedience. The safest leaders are those who do not crave authority, but who have learned to tremble before the One whose authority they represent.

The flock has not been entrusted to us so that we can rule over it, but so that we can lay down our lives for it.
