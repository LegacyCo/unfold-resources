## Commentary
Paul understood that Jesus builds His church, but He does not build it apart from people. He builds through apostles, shepherds, teachers, servants, mothers, fathers, and every living stone who gives themselves to His eternal purpose. Jesus People are those who allow Jesus to build His house His way. They know the church does not belong to them, it belongs to God. And God is very particular about His house.

So that leaves us with a very important question: are we building God's house according to God's design? Is what we are building, and calling "church," carrying the shape, substance, and Spirit of Christ Himself?

## Application
It is possible to serve, lead, and labor for the sake of the gospel and still build with the wrong materials. Ambition, comparison, control, self-promotion and a host of other carnal pursuits can never produce the kind of house that can hold the weight of Jesus sitting down in His house. 

In the end, the fire won't test how impressive our churches look, it will test what they are made of.
