## Commentary
Man is the only part of creation that is rebellious. But man is also the only part of creation God gave dominion. Man's rebellion against God had the secondary consequence of creation—which he was assigned to rule and reign over— experiencing decay. Thus, Paul's words here in Romans 8: creation longs for the revealing of the sons of God. Creation will be delivered from decay into the freedom of the children of God. Until then, all of creation groans.
