## Commentary
You have as much of God as you want — and you know as much of God as you want. The Holy Spirit has been given to us as a guide into the depths of the heart of God, and there's no bottom to those depths...He is endless and inexhaustible. 

What does God desire for this generation? 
What songs are hidden in His heart? 
What ideas and inventions have yet to be discovered because no one has opened that door yet? 
What paintings have yet to be painted or stories have yet to be written? 
What problems in our world have yet to be solved because the solution is still tucked behind one of those doors deep in the heart of God?

He doesn't hide these things in Himself because He likes cat and mouse games. He hides these things to protect us from finding them casually. He is found by those who diligently seek Him so that we don't receive revelation without intimacy. To discover the deep things of God casually would be dangerous. He reveals Himself to those who are intimate with Him, and intimacy without covenant is prostitution. Only covenant lovers have permission to say, like Moses said, "Show me Your glory, God". And you'll know you are in covenant with Him when His presence means more to you than His promise. Moses said, "If Your presence doesn't go with me, I'm not going into the land of promise." That's the language of those who have permission to mine the depths of God.

## Cross Reference
David writes in Psalm 25:14, "The secret counsel of the LORD is for those who fear Him, And He will show them His covenant."

If you are in covenant with God, made possible by the shed blood of His Son, then you have unlimited access to diligently seek the deep, hidden, glorious mysteries in the heart of an inexhaustible God. As Solomon writes in Proverbs 25:2, "It's the glory of God to conceal a matter, and the glory of kings to seek it out."
