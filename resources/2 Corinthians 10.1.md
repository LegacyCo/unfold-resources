## Commentary
Paul pleads with the church according to...the meekness and gentleness of Christ. Not the authority. Not the power. Not the dominion. Not the majesty. The *meekness*. When the world would lean on the authority of the king to get their way, Paul pushes against the gentleness of the Lord to move the church.
