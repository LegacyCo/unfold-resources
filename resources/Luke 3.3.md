## Commentary
John's gospel of repentance was to change behavior of his hearers. His gospel of repentance made the "crooked places straight and the rough ways smooth" (v5). When the people asked John "what shall we do?" his response was to point them to the law, to modify their behavior, "He who has two tunics, let him give to him who has none, he who has food let him do likewise...collect no more than what is appointed for you...do not intimidate anyone or accuse falsely" (v11-14).

This is all that the gospel of repentance has to offer, because only the One mightier, who comes *after* John, can baptize in the Holy Spirit, which changes a man from the inside out. Only the One who baptizes in fire can burn away the efforts of the law and reveal the Father. Only the Spirit of Christ can change the way a man thinks about God.
