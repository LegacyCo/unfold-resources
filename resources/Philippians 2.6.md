## Commentary
This is from *Humility* by Andrew Murray: Christ is the humility of God embodied in human nature. He is eternal love humbling itself, clothing itself in the garb of meekness and gentleness, to win and serve and save us. As the love and condescension of God makes Him the benefactor and helper and servant of all, so Jesus is and always will be the incarnate humility. Even in the midst of the throne, He is the meek and lowly Lamb of God (see ==Revelation 5:6==).
