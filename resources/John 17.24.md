## Commentary
“Father… You loved Me before the foundation of the world.”

Answer this question and it will reorder your whole relationship with God: at what point did the Father begin loving the Son? There was no beginning. The Father’s love for the Son precedes creation itself—and one verse earlier, Jesus said the Father loves *you* “as You have loved Me” (==John 17:23==). Same love. Same kind. Which means the same answer applies to a second question: at what point did the Father begin loving you? His love for you has no starting line.

Most of us believe otherwise. We assume God began loving us when we confessed our sin and prayed the prayer—that He was the Judge before we believed and became the loving Father afterward. The spirit of religion loves that perspective, because if His love has a beginning, then it can also have an end—and a love that can end is a love that can be leveraged. But you saying yes to Jesus didn’t *cause* God to love you; it *confirmed* a love that was already there. Big difference. His love comes before everything, including your repentance. You cannot earn a love that predates your existence, and you cannot lose a love that never started.

So yes—we pray, fast, give, repent, and walk in good works. But we do those things because we are loved, never to become loved. The order is the gospel.

## Cross References
The before-the-foundation pattern runs through the whole New Testament: ==Ephesians 1:4== (“He chose us in Him before the foundation of the world”—not after we got our act together), ==Ephesians 2:10== (even our good works were “prepared beforehand”), ==2 Timothy 1:9== (grace “given to us in Christ Jesus before time began”), ==1 Peter 1:20== (the Lamb “foreordained before the foundation of the world”), ==Revelation 13:8== (slain, in God’s heart, from the foundation of the world). Everything decisive about your salvation predates you.
