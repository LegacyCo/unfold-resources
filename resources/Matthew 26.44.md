## Commentary
How many times has the Lord come to us when He needed someone to "watch and pray with Him," only to find us given over to the flesh? He never leaves us in the sense of taking His Spirit from us, but there are moments when what He needs in us is not available. We are either distracted or not capable. 

## Application
Realizing this, a good prayer is, "Lord give me the grace to overcome my flesh and weakness in those moments when you are looking to partner with me to accomplish Your will."
