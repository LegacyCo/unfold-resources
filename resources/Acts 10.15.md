## Commentary
Peter’s vision was about Gentiles being welcomed into the kingdom of God, but the principle reaches deeper. God was teaching him that once He has cleansed something, no one has permission to keep calling it unclean.

That includes us.

We need to be careful with the names we keep giving ourselves after God has cleansed us. If God says you are cleansed, then calling yourself filthy is not humility. If God says you are holy, then calling yourself unholy is disagreeing with what God says about you. 

Holiness is not something we invented about ourselves. It is something God declared over us because of the blood of Jesus and the indwelling of the Spirit. We are not holy because we never stumble. We are holy because God has claimed us, cleansed us, and set us apart as His own.

Peter had to stop calling common what God had cleansed. And we have to stop calling ourselves by names the cross has already corrected.

## Application
Don’t argue with God in the name of humility. Confess sin where there is sin, but don’t confess an identity God has already crucified. If He calls you holy, learn to agree with Him.
