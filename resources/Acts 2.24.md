## Commentary
Peter says that God has "loosed the pains of death." The word we translate as "loosed" is the Greek word *lyo*. Lyo means: to untie, loose, set free, divorce, destroy, demolish. This is the same word John uses in Revelation 1:5, "To Him who loved us and released (lyo) us from our sins in His own blood."

Other instances of lyo: 
-removing Paul's prison bonds (Acts 22:30, 
-unbinding Lazarus from his grave-clothes (John 11:44), 
-the woman who had been bound by satan for 18 years being released from the spirit of infirmity (Luke 13:16), 
-the deaf man whose tongue had been bound by demons was loosed and spoke plainly (Mark 7:35), 
-Paul says that Jesus has torn down the dividing wall of hostility (Ephesians 2:14), -Barabbas was released (Matthew 27:17), the master felt compassion on the slave and released him of his debt (Matthew 18:27).

In the same way that Jesus was released from death, in the same way that Paul was set free from his prison chains, in the same way that Lazarus was divorced from his grave-clothes, in the same way that the spirit of infirmity was demolished and the woman was released—in the same way, the love of God has separated us from our sin. Even under an inferior covenant King David understood this,  
For as the heavens are high above the earth,  
So great is His mercy toward those who fear Him;  
As far as the east is from the west,  
So far has He removed our transgressions from us. (Psalm 103:11-12).
