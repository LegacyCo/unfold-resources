## Commentary
Eve said the tree of knowledge was in the “center of the garden.” The result of eating from that tree is self-centeredness. Adam and Eve began to self-examine and become self-centered. Before eating they were not ashamed of their nakedness, their attention was only on Yahweh and the glory of His creation. Now, they are ashamed at what they saw in themselves. 

In this verse we see the first instance of religion, man's attempt to satisfy God in his own effort. Eve tells the serpent that God told them not to eat, nor touch, the fruit of this tree. God only told Adam not to eat the fruit. When Adam communicated God's command to Eve, he added to God's word. This is the heart of man-made religion; take the purity of God's word and add to it...just to be safe.
