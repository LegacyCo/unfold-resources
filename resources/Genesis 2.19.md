## Commentary
In chapter one we see God naming things. But now we see man naming things. This is the first time Adam exercises his authority over creation.

As Adam looked for his companion among the animals he was naming, he saw that there was no match. In showing Adam all of the animals, God is preparing him to value the woman as unique in all of creation.
