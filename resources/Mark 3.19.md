## Commentary
In this passage, Jesus called the 12 disciples up on the mountain, appoints them to be with Him, and gives them authority. It was what we would call a "mountain-top encounter with God." Then Mark writes something interesting; "And they went into a house." The glorious mountain-top encounter must find its way into the house — the place of family, of mundane, day-to-day life. The "house" is to be transformed by the mountain-top encounter, not just the "streets."
