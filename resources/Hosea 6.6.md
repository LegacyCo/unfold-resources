## Commentary
The people continued bringing sacrifices while their hearts drifted further from the One receiving them. Their religious activity remained, but the inward consecration had disappeared. They gave God what the law required while withholding what He truly desired (==1 Samuel 15:22==).

Religion always prefers an offering it can control. A sacrifice can be presented, measured, completed, and left behind. The knowledge of God demands the entire life (==Jeremiah 9:23–24==). It requires relationship, attention, obedience, and continual surrender.

We can do many things for God while avoiding the thing God Himself actually desires. Activity can become a substitute for intimacy because it allows us to feel faithful without remaining in His presence long enough to be known…and changed. We may offer Him our hands while maintaining control of our hearts (see ==Matthew 9:13==).
