## Commentary
Peter compares the believer's faith to gold which is "tested by fire." And just like gold, faith is purified by the heat of fire. Faith that has not faced pressure and heat is untested. There may be mixture in that faith, which Jesus tells Peter is doubt — the presence of two competing thoughts.

## Application
Testing reveals what kind of faith we actually have. Fire doesn’t create the mixture; it exposes it. When faith is tested, competing thoughts rise to the surface—we trust in God but we're also afraid, we're self-reliant, or we realize we don't really believe He'll come through this time. But that exposure is mercy. God is purifying your faith so it can become steady, single-hearted, and mature. This is the faith-to-faith journey of the born-again believer (see Romans 1:17).
