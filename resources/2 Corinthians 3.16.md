## Commentary
The old covenant places a veil over the minds of all who hear it, a veil that separates them from the glory of the new covenant. That veil is "taken away in Christ" (v14). Paul is speaking about the Jews but the point he is making applies to anyone who still lives under the old covenant. Praise God, that veil is taken away when "one turns to the Lord."

## Application
It is possible to read Scripture, hear truth, and still miss the glory of what God is saying if you are relating to Him through law, performance, and self-effort. The veil is not just over people who reject Christ outright; it remains over anyone trying to approach Him on the basis of the old covenant. But when a person turns to Jesus, the veil comes off. That means the answer to being reconciled to God is not trying harder. It's turning again to Christ. In Him, what was hidden becomes clear, and what once felt condemning becomes alive with glory, freedom, and transformation of repentance.
