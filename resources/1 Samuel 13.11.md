## Commentary
King Saul told Samuel, "When I saw that the people were scattering from me...and that the Philistines gathered together." Compare this horizontal perspective to Daniel in Daniel 9:1-3, "I understood by the books the number of years specified by the word of the Lord...Then I set my face toward the Lord God to make request by prayer and supplications, with fasting." Daniel's response to what was happening around him was to lift his eyes. He shifted his gaze from the horizontal to the vertical.

## Cross Reference
See Exodus 2:12 for another example of a horizontal perspective, "he (Moses) looked this way and that...and he killed the Egyptian."
