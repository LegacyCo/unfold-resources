## Commentary
Wesley Duewel wrote this in *Mighty Prevailing Prayer*: God's overarching eternal plans are unchangeable, but in working out the details, He has ordained to work in cooperation with His praying, obeying children. He adapts His working to your prayer and obedience. While He reserves the sovereign right to work independently, His normal plan is to work in cooperation with and through the prayer and obedience of His own.
  
God therefore delights to adjust the working out of His plans to your cooperating obedience, to your laying hold of His promise, to your preparing the way of the Lord. He has made it possible for you to prevail in prayer, and He is waiting now for you and me to prove the blessed possibilities and the glorious realities of prevailing prayer. 

## Application
Don’t treat prayer like a formality when God treats it like participation. He has chosen to work through the prayers and obedience of His people, which means your agreement matters. So lay hold of what He has promised, obey what He has spoken, and keep praying until Christ's victory finds expression in your situation.
