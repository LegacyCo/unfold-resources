## Commentary
“And all these, having obtained a good testimony through faith, did not receive the promise, God having provided something better for us.”

Read the end of Hebrews 11 carefully and you’ll find two kinds of heroes standing side by side. Some conquered kingdoms, stopped the mouths of lions, and received their dead raised to life (==Hebrews 11:33-35==). Others were mocked, scourged, chained, stoned, sawn in two—wandering in sheepskins, destitute, afflicted, tormented, “of whom the world was not worthy” (==Hebrews 11:36-38==). Same faith. Same obedience. Wildly different outcomes in this life. And then the verdict over all of them together: they obtained a good testimony through faith, but *did not receive the promise*.

This is the part of God’s reward system we have to grow up into. Some, like Noah, receive earthly reward as a kind of down payment on the heavenly one. Others live and die faithful and never see the payout on this side. Heaven doesn’t read that as failure—heaven reads it as treasure still on deposit, waiting for a better resurrection (==Hebrews 11:35==). I once heard it put this way: “When we stand before the Father, our greatest treasures will be the things we did not get rewarded for here on earth… The greatest rewards will come to us in that day for things we didn’t get fulfillment of on earth. We will be thankful for every time someone passed us by when it was time for recognition.”

So if your obedience hasn’t produced what you expected yet—if you’ve been faithful and the promise still hasn’t landed—you’re in the best company Scripture has to offer. Obedience connects you to a reward system established by a sovereign God who is building you for eternity.
