## Commentary
This is from "Apostolic Foundations" by Art Katz — When the Jews of Jesus' time were antagonized and stupefied at this bewildering Man who performed these glorious things on the wrong day, He could say to them in utter simplicity that it was the Father who was doing the works. You will know when you are in the rest of God when there is a deep-seated peace in your inner being. This does not mean there will be an absence of trial or tension, but in the midst of the turmoil of it, you are in the Shekinah place, the holiest place of all, independent of the circumstances that are flurrying everywhere about you.
