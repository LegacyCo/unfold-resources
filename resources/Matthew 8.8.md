## Commentary
There is a beautiful connection between the centurion’s words and ==Psalm 107:20==: “He sent His word and healed them, and delivered them from their destructions.”

The centurion understands something remarkable about Jesus’ authority: Jesus does not need to be physically present for His power to be present. “Only speak a word, and my servant will be healed.”

In ==Psalm 107:20==, God sends His word and healing follows. Here, the centurion believes Jesus can do the same thing. _His word carries His authority._

Jesus does not need to enter the house, touch the servant, or perform some visible act. He only needs to speak.

The centurion may not fully understand everything his confession implies, but Matthew allows us to see it: the healing authority Scripture attributes to God is now being exercised through the word of Jesus.
