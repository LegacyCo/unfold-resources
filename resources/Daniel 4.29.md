## Commentary
The time between King Nebuchadnezzar's dream and the fulfillment of that dream was twelve months. After months of waiting, when it seemed the dream could not possibly be true, it became true in an instant..."while the word was still in the king's mouth" (v31).

Waiting can make us apathetic. It can cause even the most powerful dream to become diluted in our memory. A dream that made the most powerful man on the planet afraid on his bed (v5) became a distant memory because of distance and time.

While our attachment to a word the Lord has spoken to us may slip, we must remain steadfast to what He has spoken. One day is as a thousand to Him. He exists outside of time. The delay has no bearing on the word from His perspective.
