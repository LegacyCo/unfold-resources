## Word Study
The Hebrew word that we translate here as "set apart" is pālâ. It means to be distinguished, to treat with distinction. It is a rare word in the Old Testament, only used seven times. For example: following the golden calf incident, Moses argues with God that if He chooses not to go with His people, this will mean loss of Israel’s distinction among the nations (==Exodus 33:16==). Israel is distinguished as a people by the presence of God with them.

Here in Psalm 4, David anchors his confidence that the Lord will hear his cry in the truth that he has been set apart by the Lord for Himself. He knows he belongs to God. He has covenantal confidence.
