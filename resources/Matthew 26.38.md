## Commentary
Jesus takes Peter, James, and John further into the garden than the other disciples. He reveals to these three that His "soul is exceedingly sorrowful, even to the point of death." He also gives them them an assignment, "Stay here and watch with Me." They were entrusted with something the others were not. Jesus revealed His heart to them ("My soul is exceedingly sorrowful"), and with this vulnerability came responsibility ("watch with Me").

Jesus did not love them more than the others, but there was something in these three that Jesus wanted to reveal by way of intentional discipleship that may not have been in the others just yet.
