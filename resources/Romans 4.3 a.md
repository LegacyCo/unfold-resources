## Commentary
This is from *The Total Superiority of the New Covenant* by Dean Briggs: It is critical to know which covenant you are under! Furthermore, you get to choose, i.e. the manner in which you "achieve” righteousness, you can allow God to impart His righteousness to you, totally unearned and undeserved. Like Abraham, you believe God, and it gets counted to you as righteousness.

You can relate under the terms of an Old Covenant or a New Covenant. Both are ways of relating to God. Either way, you choose! As a Christian, day by day, you still must choose.
