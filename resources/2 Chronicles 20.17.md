## Commentary
While God's people did not need to fight, they still needed to go out to the battlefield. They needed to position themselves in front of their enemies. The battle belonged to the Lord, but there still was a responsibility for them to engage in the fight...even if merely to bring their presence onto the battlefield. The Lord sends His people to the battlefield not to see their enemies, but to see Him — and see Him deal with their enemies.
