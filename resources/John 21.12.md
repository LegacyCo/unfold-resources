## Commentary
Throughout Scripture the King is seen as the One providing a meal for us (Isaiah 25:6-9, Psalm 23:5, Luke 12:37, Matthew 22:1-12. Have you ever prepared a meal for someone? It's a lot of work, and it costs something. 

One day we will eat with Jesus in the age to come. The price He paid for that meal was blood. The work involved was not so much preparing the meal, but in preparing the guests.
