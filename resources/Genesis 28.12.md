## Commentary
“Then he dreamed, and behold, a ladder was set up on the earth, and its top reached to heaven; and there the angels of God were ascending and descending on it.”

The language of this verse is the language of Babel. The builders on the plain of Shinar wanted “a tower whose top is in the heavens” (==Genesis 11:4==); Jacob sees a ladder “whose top reached to heaven.” The difference is the direction of the construction. Babel's tower was raised from below, out of man's bricks, and God came down to stop it. Jacob's ladder is *set up* by God, given to a man asleep on a stone with nothing in his hands, and God stands at the top of it speaking covenant promises (==Genesis 28:13-15==). The connection between heaven and earth that man could not build, God simply provides.

There is a tower in the earth that reaches into heaven, and it is a spiritual reality given flesh in Christ. Jesus told Nathanael, “you shall see heaven open, and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of Man” (==John 1:51==)—claiming Jacob's ladder for Himself. He is the meeting place of God and man, the one Mediator (==1 Timothy 2:5==), the way no one can build and everyone may climb. What the religion of Babel attempts with earthly materials, God has already accomplished by coming down in His Son.
