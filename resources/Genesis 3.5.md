## Commentary
Was God aware that the serpent and Eve were having a conversation about Him? Of course. Why didn't He intervene? 

For God to intervene in that moment of testing would be for Him to cut off the process of Eve learning how to carry His image in the earth.

God has a process that He takes His people through to bring them to maturity...and He is committed to that process because He is committed to His sons and daughters becoming like Jesus. Even Jesus, when He took on flesh, went through the process of growing and learning and maturing. Hebrews 5:8 says,
	"though He (Jesus) was a Son, He LEARNED obedience by the things which He suffered."
Jesus didn't come into this world with obedience, He learned obedience by submitting Himself to the Father's process of suffering. It's no different for us. Some things we receive instantly as a result of faith in Jesus (salvation, adoption into the family of God, righteousness, holiness), while other things we must learn by giving ourselves to the Father’s process of maturity. 

God will not override your freewill and force you into obedience...not when He intends the trial or the temptation to be the chisel that actually shapes you into the image of His Son. Don't waste your difficulty...because God may not have caused it, but He sure isn't going to waste the opportunity of you learning what it means to bear the cross. It’s actually as we bear the cross in this life that we become conformed to the image of Jesus. Relaxing in a garden eating grapes with lions and peacocks isn’t the purpose of salvation…you being transformed into the image of the Son of God is what salvation now affords you.

## Application
It's tempting to look at this story and think, "It seems so easy, Eve. You had everything in the garden available to you…just don't eat the fruit of that one tree.” But we must see ourselves in Adam and Eve. 

Remember, Genesis 3 is our story, too. We must see how much God has freely given to us, and that we, too, are faced with the simple task of obedience to what He says is “not good.” As inconceivable as it is that Adam and Eve would eat the fruit, we, too, have eaten the fruit. We, too, have sinned. The Bible says in Romans 3:23 that all have sinned and fallen short of the glory of God. Outside of Christ, we are all under the power of death. But praise God, through the life, death, and resurrection of Jesus, the greater power has come…and He opens our eyes to see light and save us from our spiritual darkness. As Paul says in Acts 26:18, 
	"we can now turn from the power of Satan to God, and receive forgiveness of sins and the inheritance of those who have been made holy (HOW?) by faith in Jesus."
