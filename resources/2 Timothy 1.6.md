## Commentary
Paul tells Timothy to reactivate/kindle afresh the gift of God that was given to him through the laying on of hands. He tells Timothy something similar in 1 Timothy 4:14, "Do not neglect the gift that is in you, which was given to you by prophecy with the laying on of the hands of the eldership." A gift had been deposited into the life of young Timothy. Something of God had truly come into his life through apostolic ministry.

And yet Paul still has to tell him to stir it up.

Impartation is real, but impartation is not the same thing as maturity. A gift can be given in a moment, but character is formed over time. Grace can be imparted suddenly, but stewardship is learned slowly. Faithfulness, endurance, courage, and depth with God are formed in the process of long obedience in the same direction.

## Application
There are no shortcuts in the kingdom. No matter who prays for you, you still have to walk it out. You still have to submit to the processes by which God makes a man able to carry what he's been entrusted with.
