## Commentary
Faith does not come by hearing the word of the Lord. Israel heard the word of the Lord in the wilderness, but the hearing was not united with faith (Hebrews 4:2). The word of the Lord makes hearing possible, but what is heard must still be united with faith.

The word doesn't come to ignite faith, the word comes to expose the thoughts and intents of the heart (Hebrews 4:12). That word is coming to you like a scalpel and exposing whatever doubt is lodged in your heart.
