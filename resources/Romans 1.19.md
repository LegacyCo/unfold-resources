## Commentary
Paul is continuing his explanation of why humanity is accountable before God. What may be known about God is plain, because God Himself has made it known.

Paul doubles down on his argument here in v19: the knowledge of God is not just an external witness in creation, though Paul will address that in the next verse. There is also an *internal* witness in each of us. God has placed within humanity an awareness of Himself. As image bearers, we were made by God, for God, and with a capacity to recognize the reality of God.

There is something within the human heart that knows life is more than biology, appetite, survival, and circumstance. ==Ecclesiastes 3:11== says God “has put eternity into man’s heart.” There is an ache for the uncreated Creator that points us beyond the material world.

Paul says this inner awareness exists because “God has shown it to them.” In other words, this knowledge does not originate in human wisdom. It is revelation. God has taken the initiative to make Himself known.

That does not mean every person has saving knowledge of God apart from the gospel. Romans 1 is not teaching that creation or conscience can bring a person into the fullness of salvation in Christ. Paul’s point is that God has revealed enough of Himself to make humanity *accountable*. The problem is not a total absence of light. The problem is what fallen humanity does with the light it has been given. The tragedy of sin is that the human heart takes that witness and suppresses it, choosing independence over obedience to God.
