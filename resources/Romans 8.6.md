## Commentary
A literal translation of this verse would be, "For the mindset of the flesh is death, but the mindset of the Spirit is life and peace." Repentance changes the way we think from agreeing with the things of the flesh to agreeing with the things of the spirit. The unrepentant mind thinks thoughts that lead to death...that's what sin does, it leads to death. The repentant mind thinks thoughts that lead to life and peace...that's what life in Christ leads to, and that life begins with repentance.

## Application
Every sin has a thought-pattern behind it.

Before sin shows up in your actions, it has usually already been welcomed in your mind. Pride begins by agreeing with self-exalting thoughts. Lust begins by agreeing with corrupt thoughts. Fear begins by agreeing with thoughts that exclude the faithfulness of God. Bitterness begins by agreeing with accusations and rehearsed offenses. That's why repentance is so vital. Repentance is the breaking of agreement with the mindset of the flesh and the embracing of the mindset of the Spirit.

This means repentance is not only about what you stop doing, but about what you stop agreeing with. You are not just turning from bad habits. You are turning from death-producing ways of seeing, judging, desiring, and interpreting life. And in Christ, you are learning to think thoughts the Holy Spirit can say "Amen!" to.
