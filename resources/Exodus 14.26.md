## Commentary
God parted the sea, but He partnered with Moses. The sea only parted *after* Moses lifted his rod. The sea only closed on the Egyptians after Moses stretched out his hands. God was showing something about Himself to His people; He is the all-powerful God who acts on behalf of His people by demonstrating Himself *through* His people.

"Stand still and see the salvation of the Lord...the Lord will fight for you, and you shall hold your peace (keep still, remain inactive, be silent)" (Exodus 14:13-14)...amen, yes, of course. But God responds to this by looking at His leader and saying, "Move forward." 

There is a tension here between knowing God will do what only God can do, and us doing what only we can do. Our role is waiting in faith (which looks like silent, inactive stillness) and moving forward in obedience when it is time.
