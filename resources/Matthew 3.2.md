## Commentary
The phrase "at hand" means something is within reach. This is a staggering statement John is making, to which Jesus doubles down and says, "The kingdom of God is *within* you" (Luke 17:21). The reality of God's kingdom is not *out there* somewhere. The atmosphere God's world is not a far off, detached reality we are waiting to *go to* after death. The reality of God's kingdom is within reach *now*. It belongs to those who refuse to come under the gravitational pull of this age and settle for status quo Christianity. As I heard it put, "If it's happening *up there*, it ought to be happening *down here*. And if it's not happening *up there*, it shouldn't be happening *down here*."

------

This is from "Will You Choose the Wilderness" by Samuel Whitefield: John's message of repentance was shocking because John first called Israel to repent. Israel expected God to deliver her from gentile oppressors like Rome, but gentile oppressors were not the real problem. John addressed the much bigger problem: Israel was not compatible with God. God could end the rule of Rome in a moment, but Israel was not ready for His rule.
