## Commentary
This is a great verse. But there's truth buried in the challenging translation issues. The translation, "For with God nothing will be impossible," completely misses the "*pan* *rhema*" at the end of the verse - every/all freshly spoken word. The better translation is, "No freshly spoken word of God will be without ability."

The amplified translation would be something along these lines: "No freshly spoken word of God will ever come to you that does not contain its own ability to perform itself." This concept is reinforced in James 1:21, 
	"In humility, receive the word implanted which is able to save your soul." 
The power for transformation is contained within the word you receive.
