## Commentary
The point of the mustard seed is not primarily how slowly the Kingdom grows. Jesus is revealing how unexpectedly small the Kingdom appears when it first arrives.

The Kingdom that will one day fill the earth and overthrow every rival power has already come among men, but it has come in a form no one expected. It does not yet appear with visible majesty, political force, or irresistible power. It looks small, ordinary, and easy to overlook.

That's the mystery. The same Kingdom that will one day be revealed in glory is already present in seed form. Its present appearance does not diminish its reality.

What looks insignificant may still carry the rule and power of God within it. The mustard seed should not be despised because of its size. It is still the Kingdom.
