## Commentary
The mysteries of the kingdom aren't handed out to the curious; they are entrusted to those who are near. The crowd heard the parables, but the disciples came closer. And later Mark tells us, “When they were alone, He explained all things to His disciples” (Mark 4:34).

There is a dimension of prayer that brings us into that same place—alone with Jesus, away from the noise and the crowd. Prayer doesn’t just ask God for answers; it positions us to receive understanding. Some things are not revealed in public because they can only be received through intimacy.

The crowd may hear the story, but the disciple stays long enough to ask what it means. And those who can be alone with Jesus are the ones who begin to see what others only hear.
