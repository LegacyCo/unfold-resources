## Commentary
“The secret counsel of the LORD is for those who fear Him, and He will show them His covenant.”

God has an inner circle. That’s what this verse is saying—there is counsel He shares, plans He discloses, things He tells His confidants that the crowd never hears. And look at the ticket into that circle: not giftedness, not title, not platform. The fear of the Lord. The person who takes God seriously—who honors Him, trembles at His word, and refuses to treat Him casually—that’s the person God pulls close and speaks to.

Jesus put the new covenant version of this in one staggering sentence: “No longer do I call you servants, for a servant does not know what his master is doing; but I have called you friends, for all things that I heard from My Father I have made known to you” (==John 15:15==). Friendship with God comes with information. This is a wonderful verse to hold before the Lord when you need direction—for your relationships, your work, your family, your next step. Don’t outsource His voice to someone else’s feed. Seek Him yourself. 

## Word Study
- *secret counsel* : **sōd** — a circle of intimate friends; confidential conversation; the deliberation shared within a trusted council. This isn’t “secret” as in hidden treasure to hunt for—it’s “secret” as in what close friends discuss around a table.

Watch the word work elsewhere:

- ==Jeremiah 23:18== — the mark of a true prophet: “who has stood in the *counsel* (sōd) of the LORD, and has perceived and heard His word?”
- ==Amos 3:7== — “Surely the Lord GOD does nothing, unless He reveals His *secret* (sōd) to His servants the prophets.”
- ==Proverbs 3:32== — “the perverse person is an abomination to the LORD, but His *secret counsel* (sōd) is with the upright.”
- ==Psalm 55:14== — David and his friend “took sweet *counsel* (sōd) together”—the same word, used of human friendship.

Summary: sōd is table language, not throne-room-protocol language. God’s deepest communications happen in the intimacy of friendship, and Psalm 25:14 tells you who gets a seat—those who fear Him.
