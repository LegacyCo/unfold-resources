## Commentary
The Lord begins this "message" to Israel by saying, "You shall be holy, for I the Lord your God am holy." He then goes on to give them practical laws for living as the people of God (don't glean the edges of the field, don't cheat your neighbor, do no injustice in your judgements, love your neighbor, don't eat the fruit of your trees in the first 4 years, etc.). After each of these statutes, the Lord says, "I am the Lord your God." He is showing them what it means to be holy as He is holy, to do what He does.

The point is not that they follow the rules. The intention is that they live like God lives and treat one another as God treats them.
