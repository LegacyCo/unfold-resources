## Commentary
Humanity’s problem is not ignorance. Paul says that truth is being suppressed. The message of the Bible is not that people are honestly seeking God but unable to find Him.
Here, Paul describes a people who have been given enough light to know that God is real, and yet they push that truth down because they don't want to live under His authority. The issue is not that God is silent. The issue is that fallen humanity resists what God has made known.

This is why Paul says the truth is suppressed “in unrighteousness.” Sin does not only affect behavior. It affects perception. Rebellion distorts the way people respond to reality. The created world is speaking, but the rebellious heart does not want to hear what creation is saying.

Psalm 19:1–4 says, “The heavens declare the glory of God, and the sky above proclaims his handiwork.” Creation is declaring the Creator. The skies, the order of the world, the rhythm of day and night, and the beauty and complexity of what God has made all bear witness to His glory and reality. David says, “Their voice goes out through all the earth, and their words to the end of the world.” Creation does not reveal everything that must be known for salvation, but it does reveal enough to make humanity accountable before God.

Paul makes a similar argument in Acts 14:17 when he says that God “did not leave Himself without witness.” He gave rains from heaven and fruitful seasons, satisfying hearts with food and gladness. In other words, the ordinary gifts of life are not meaningless. Rain, harvest, provision, beauty, and joy all testify that creation is not self-originating or self-sustaining. There is a Creator behind it all, and He has not left Himself without witness.

Romans 1:18 exposes the seriousness of human rebellion. God created humanity to know Him, worship Him, and live in communion with Him. But instead of receiving the truth and responding with gratitude, sinful humanity suppresses the truth in order to live as though God is unnecessary.

Romans 1:18 reminds us that the fallen human condition does not want God to be God. And this is precisely why the gospel is needed.
