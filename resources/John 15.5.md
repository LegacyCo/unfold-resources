## Commentary
The Christian life is not difficult apart from Jesus; it is impossible. We may be able to appear fruitful for a season, but real fruit can only come from union with Christ.

And this is where prayer becomes so much more than asking God for things. Prayer is the practice of abiding. It is the soul turning again and again toward Jesus as the very source of life. The branch doesn’t produce fruit by trying harder. It produces fruit by remaining connected to the vine.

Prayer is the place where we remember our dependence. It is where we dwell in Him and allow His life to dwell in us.

Fruit is not forced; it is formed. And it's formed in those who abide.
