## Commentary
Notice, the issue was not that Jesus had nothing more to give. The issue was that His disciples did not yet have the capacity to carry what He wanted to give.

That means when you ask God for more, the first answer may not be more revelation, more power, or more visible increase. The first answer may be process. God answering your prayer for more will look like God initially dealing with your capacity. He loves you enough not to give you what you're asking for until He has formed in you the strength, structure, and inward maturity to bear it.

## Application
Don't despise the process God takes you through to make you more like Jesus. The process is not the postponement of the answer to your prayers. It's actually the beginning of it. He is building in you the capacity to bear what you asked for.
