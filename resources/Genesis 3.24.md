## Commentary
To keep man from living forever in his new condition of sin, an angelic presence and a flaming sword are sent to guard the way to life. This sword now guards/tends/watches over the way to life. This is the same word Yahweh spoke to Adam in Genesis 2:15, telling Adam he will “guard/tend/watch over” the garden. Man can no longer tend to the Garden, so the garden is tended by a sword. 

Think about that for a moment: There is a *sword* that now guards the way to the tree of eternal life. 

God's word is often seen in Scripture as a sword. For example; Paul writes in Ephesians 6:17 that we are to take up the “sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God.” 

Jesus is, of course, the Word of God made flesh (John 1:1, John 1:14). 

What God placed at the east side of the Garden in the old covenant to keep man from unending life is now revealed in the new covenant as the *way* to life. The Living Word became flesh so that He could die on a tree and restore the way back to the tree of life. The flaming Sword has become the bleeding Lamb.
