## Commentary
In the age to come, the Son, though Master of all, will serve His servants at the table when God spreads the Messianic banquet for His people.

This is from "Come to the Table" by John Mark Hicks: God's intent in redemption is to dwell with His people in a communion of love. God seeks fellowship with His people because He desires to share community with us in loving fellowship. He created for the sake of a table, and He recreates to experience that table once again. God's goal has always been a table.
