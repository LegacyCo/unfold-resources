## Commentary
Shame expresses the feeling of embarrassment. Shame also expresses the feeling of rejection and abandonment, whether by a friend or someone in a position of authority. Shame implies a personal experience of disgrace and rejection. Shame occurs when someone fails in an attempted risk and thereby loses their position of honor. Shame is experienced when perceived codes of conduct or expectations are not fully met. Shame is used by culture to control certain behaviors it deems indecent. The intention is to bring disgrace and dishonor on an individual or group by way of exposing them to public opinion in order to control behavior.

David is here reminding himself that God has revealed that He would not disgrace them or shame those who trust in Him ("Our fathers trusted in You...and were not ashamed - Psalm 22:4-5). 

What a powerful prayer and reminder, "O my God, I trust in You, let me not be ashamed."
