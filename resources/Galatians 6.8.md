## Commentary
The word for "decay" here means destruction by way of decomposition, the separation of something into its core elements or parts until it no longer exists anymore. Living "in the flesh" reaps decay and decomposition, a break down over time even though new sowing is happening. Walking in the Spirit, however, reaps life so abundant it can only be described as eternal.

## Cross Reference
See Romans 8:19-22 - All of creation was subjected to decay when Adam sinned, a decay it will be set free from just as we are released from our own decay.
