## Commentary
Why did God save you? Verse 7 gives an answer few of us would guess: “that in the ages to come He might show the exceeding riches of His grace in His kindness toward us in Christ Jesus.” You were saved to be *shown*. God intends to put His grace on display, and the display case is us.

The apex moment of that grace was the crucifixion of the Son. At the cross, justice and mercy had a head-on collision. Sin was judged once and for all—but rather than the judgment falling on us, it came down on Jesus, who prayed from the very center of the collision, “Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they do” (==Luke 23:34==). Give them what I deserve; give Me what they deserve. That exchange is the crowning moment of God’s grace and love, displayed for all eternity.

In London, the crown jewels of England sit on public display at the Tower, where millions file past them every year. They aren’t locked away in some hidden vault—they’re set out under glass, on purpose, so the whole world can see the wealth and glory of that kingdom. God does the same. His grace is put on display through the church for all creation to see the glory of His kingdom—“that now the manifold wisdom of God might be made known by the church to the principalities and powers in the heavenly places” (==Ephesians 3:10==). The church is the trophy case of God’s love and grace.

That means your rescued life isn’t just for you. Every restored marriage, every broken habit left behind, every act of undeserved kindness flowing out of a person who received undeserved kindness—each one is a jewel under glass, announcing the riches of the King. Grace saves us and grace seats us, but grace has one more job: it shows the world who God is. And it does that, mostly, through you.
