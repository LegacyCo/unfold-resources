## Commentary
George Mueller's foundational belief was that "either we trust in God, and in that case we neither trust in ourselves, nor in our fellowmen, not in circumstances, nor in anything besides; or we DO trust in one or more of these, and in that case do NOT trust in God." 

That's the tension Psalm 37:5 places us in. “Commit your way to the Lord” means we do more than mention our burdens to God. We roll the weight of our life onto Him. We entrust the outcome, the timing, and the process to His hands.

We are so prone to want to answer our own prayers. We pray, but then how difficult it is to wait — and how easy it is to begin looking for the nearest human solution. We may call it stewardship, but often it is fear dressed in religious language.

The promise is that God will act when we truly entrust our way to Him.
