## Commentary
“Then Jacob awoke from his sleep and said, ‘Surely the LORD is in this place, and I did not know it.’”

Jacob is a man in the middle of a journey he did not choose. He has left his home, his family, and everything familiar, traveling alone toward a land where he will look for a wife. Night catches him in the open with a stone for a pillow (==Genesis 28:11==)—and it is there, in the loneliest stretch of the road, that God gives him the dream of the ladder and speaks: “I am the LORD God of Abraham your father… the land on which you lie I will give to you and your descendants… Behold, I am with you and will keep you wherever you go” (==Genesis 28:13-15==). God was in that place all along, and Jacob did not know it. And many on their own journey of faith need this same reminder.

The journey of faith wears people down. Many who start off well end up lying down in a bed of thorns because they get tired of the road—the cares and comforts of life look softer than the next stretch of obedience (==Luke 8:14==). Jacob's revelation is the remedy for that weariness. The presence of God is not waiting at the destination; it is present at the low point, at the stone-pillow stage of the journey, whether or not the traveler feels it. “How awesome is this place! This is none other than the house of God, and this is the gate of heaven!” (==Genesis 28:17==)—said not of a temple but of a patch of open ground where an exhausted man slept. God is there to remind the worn-out sojourner that He is with them, and He gives the strength to keep surrendering, to keep going deeper, and to not give up on Him or on this life of faith (==Isaiah 40:29-31==).

## Application
If you are tired of the journey, do what Jacob did: wake up to where God actually is. Say it over the place you are in right now—the job, the waiting, the hard season: “Surely the LORD is in this place.” His presence there does not depend on your awareness of it, but your strength depends on waking up to it. Before you lie down in the thorns, lie down at Bethel: mark the place, worship there (==Genesis 28:18==), and take the promise with you—“I am with you and will keep you wherever you go.”
