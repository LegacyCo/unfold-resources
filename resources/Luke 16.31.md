## Commentary
Hardness of heart is a terrifying thing. It makes us unreceptive to the life-giving power of God's word. We may think, "If an angel showed up, then I would believe...If I saw the burning bush or heard the voice from heaven, then I would believe." Jesus says in this passage that there are those, who by way of unbelief towards what God has already spoken in His word—"Moses and the prophets"—will neither be persuaded though one rise from the dead.

One has risen from the dead. His name is Jesus. And to those who by faith receive Him, His word, and His kingdom, He gives new life.
