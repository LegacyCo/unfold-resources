## Commentary
“But you, when you pray, go into your room, and when you have shut your door, pray to your Father who is in the secret place; and your Father who sees in secret will reward you openly.” And what’s the best reward the Father could give you? Himself.

The secret place is where the fire is ignited and hunger is cultivated—the sacred place of covenantal intimacy between bride and Bridegroom, where new life is birthed. And here’s the thing about it: no one *stumbles* their way into the secret place. You choose it. It isn’t called the secret place because it’s hard to find. It’s called the secret place because so few choose to go there.

So if your hunger for God has gone quiet, here’s a practical path back.

Establish the secret place. Pick the room, the chair, the hour. Shut the door on purpose, because it will never shut itself.

Ask for hunger. If you can’t muster desire, pray the most lethal prayer against apathy I know: “God, I want to want You. Help me. Give me the grace to seek You with my whole heart.” That’s a prayer the Father loves to answer.

Create space. If your life is stuffed, don’t drown in guilt—just make room. Remove one noisy thing this week: one app, one show, one nightly pattern that’s been owned by distraction. Those things aren’t evil. They’re just loud, and hunger needs space to grow.

Obey what He already told you. Nothing kills hunger like delayed obedience. If God has already told you to forgive, reconcile, confess, make the hard phone call, or break an agreement with sin—stop waiting for more clarity and do it. Obedience clears the weeds, and God responds to it every time.

God comes where He’s wanted. He always responds to the hungry.
