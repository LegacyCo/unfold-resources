## Commentary
Zacharias doubted the message he received. The consequence was he lost his voice. Unbelief causes us to lose our voice of influence. In His mercy, God silences those that are about to destroy their destiny with their own confession. 

When John was born nine months later, Zacharias wrote a name for the boy that was not a family name. It was the name the angel gave him (v13). By coming back to the word of the Lord, his tongue was loosed.

## Application
We must be careful what we say in seasons where God has spoken but nothing has happened yet. Unbelief has a voice, and if left unchecked, it can sabotage what God is trying to do in you. Sometimes the mercy of God looks like silence—restraining your words until your heart comes back into agreement with His word.
