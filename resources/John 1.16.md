## Commentary
“Of His fullness we have all received, and grace upon grace.”

John traces every gift back to the fullness of Christ.

Everything required for obedience, forgiveness, endurance, purity, generosity, love, and hope already exists in Him. His life is an inexhaustible source. The needs of His people will never reach the limits of His supply.

John describes the flow as “grace upon grace.” One gift follows another. Fresh mercy arrives for each new morning. Strength comes for the obedience in front of us. Wisdom meets the decision. Patience meets the difficult relationship. Hope rises inside the disappointment.

Grace keeps coming because Christ remains full.

A lamp shines as power moves through it. Its brightness comes from connection to the source. In the same way, the life of Jesus becomes visible through people who remain joined to Him.

Exhaustion often reveals the places where we have begun drawing from personality, discipline, experience, or natural strength. Those resources carry us for a distance, then reach their limit.

Christ has no limit.

The Christian life flows through continual reception. We turn toward Him, recognize our need, and draw from the life already present within us. His fullness becomes patience in our weakness, courage in our fear, generosity in our scarcity, and love where resentment once lived.

Grace upon grace means there is always more of Jesus available for the life He has called us to live.
