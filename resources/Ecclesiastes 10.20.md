## Commentary
All gossip must be purged. Words are extremely powerful. To say something negative about someone is to have broken covenant with him, and even to have spiritually "murdered" them (see Proverbs 18:21 - "Death and life are in the power of the tongue," 1 John 3:15 - “Whoever hates his brother is a murderer, and you know that no murderer has eternal life abiding in him”)

## Application
Make a commitment to never speak negatively about someone behind their back...a "bird may hear." Our words must have a sovereign integrity that does not depend on circumstance.
