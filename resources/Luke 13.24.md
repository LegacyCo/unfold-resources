## Commentary
The word "strive" here is *agonizomai* — it's where we get the word "agony." It means to fight, to struggle, to exert yourself in a contest. Jesus isn't talking about casual interest or half-hearted curiosity. He's talking about people who are pressing into the kingdom with everything they have. And even then, He says many who seek will not be able to enter. That ought to wake us up.

Here's what I think Jesus is getting at: the narrow door isn't about a complex theological formula you have to figure out. It's about total surrender. The door is narrow because you can't bring anything with you. You cant drag your old identity through it. You cant carry your self-sufficiency, your pride, your insistence on living for yourself. Those things are too big to fit through the door. The door is exactly the size of a person who has died to themselves and is now living for Him who died and rose on their behalf (==2 Corinthians 5:15==). 

The wide door? That's the one where you get to keep calling the shots, where you bring your stuff, where you live for yourself and just add Jesus to the mix. A lot of people prefer that door. But just as the door on Noah's ark sealed him in and protected him from everything going on outside, the narrow door of surrender protects you from the version of yourself that still wants to be in control. 

Jesus isn't saying, "Strive," because He's making salvation hard to obtain. He's saying the life He's calling us into requires us to let go of the life we've been clinging to.
