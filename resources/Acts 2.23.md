## Commentary
The only verb used in Scripture describing what the Father does in relation to crucifixion of the Son, is "delivered" (used here as well as Romans 4:25). 

Why does this matter? Have you ever imagined Jesus being beaten and brutalized and thought it was something of the Father's doing? What about the images of Jesus on the cross, bloodied and torn. Was there anything in you that thought those things stemmed from the Father's demands? 

The Father did not beat the Son. He did not bruise Him, assault Him, and certainly, He did not murder Him. We did. All that was required was blood. The Son was delivered up for the shedding of blood for the sin of the world. And when the Father delivered the Son into the hands of wicked men, wicked men did wicked things to the Christ. 

“This is love, not that we loved God, but that He loved us and sent His Son to be the propitiation/payment/exchange for our sins" (1 John 4:10).

## Application
Jesus came to bring us to the Father, not to save us from Him. On the cross, Jesus didn’t say “Father, beat Me instead of them.” Jesus said, “Father, forgive them for they do not know what they are doing.” 
  
Jesus says in Matthew 16:21 that He must suffer many things at the hands of...*His Father*? NO! 

"He must suffer many things at the hands of the elders and chief priests and scribes.”  
  
In Luke 18:31-33, Jesus tells His disciples that He will be delivered to the Gentiles and be “mocked and insulted and spit upon. *They* will scourge Him and kill Him." 
  
In Matthew 17:12 Jesus says, “Elijah has come and they (the religious leaders of the day) did not know him but did to him whatever they wished. Likewise, the Son of Man is also about to suffer at their hands.”  
  
The spirit of religion wants us to still see God through the lens of Adam’s brokenness…and if religion can keep you afraid of God like Adam was, then you won’t receive His love. You'll be inclined to keep the Father at a safe distance.

Fear and love can't exist together (see 1 John 4:18). If fear keeps perfect love at a distance, then fear will never be cast out. Love must be embraced for fear to be cast out. Do you see why religion wants you to be afraid of God? As long as you’re afraid of Him, you will never walk in the fullness of what Jesus died to give you.  
  
Did God kill His Son? Absolutely. Blood was required and the sinless Lamb of God willingly went to the cross and shed His own blood…but it was only blood that the Father required. Nowhere, in the sacrificial system do we see it necessary for the lamb to suffer and be brutalized. In fact, the sacrifice was made in an atmosphere of celebration and joy…”the lamb is dying on our behalf.”  
  
Jesus knew He would suffer, but He drank the cup anyway. Jesus knew that when He was placed into the hands of wicked men, wicked men were going to do wicked things. Before He was arrested in the garden, Jesus says in Luke 22:53 that it was “the hour of darkness…and the time of power for that darkness.” The powers of darkness finally had their moment when the Father’s hedge of protection was removed so that scripture could be fulfilled. Jesus could’ve asked the Father to send 12 legions of angels to save Him from that hour…but for love He refused: “What shall I say? Father save Me from this hour? No, for this purpose I have come. Father glorify Your name” (John 12:27). And darkness, taking advantage of its opportunity, did dark things to the Light of the world. Jesus suffered. He suffered at the hands of wicked men empowered by the forces of darkness. 
  
The Lord often uses men as the instrument of His judgment. This was the case when Israel turned their back on God and broke their covenant with Him. God raised up Babylon as the instrument of His judgement against Israel. But Zechariah 1:15 says Babylon “went too far (v15 NIV).” They acted wickedly and were too severe in how they treated Israel. Judgment for sin was necessary, but the extent of suffering inflicted on God’s people was evil, depraved, and wicked.  
  
Similarly, the leaders of Israel whom Jesus was delivered to for the judgment of OUR sin went too far. Their assignment was to produce blood from the Lamb. But the Lamb suffered, and the prophets saw this beforehand and declared it. In Acts 3:18 Peter says, “Those things which God foretold by the mouth of all His prophets, that the Christ would suffer, He has thus fulfilled.”  
  
A.W. Tozer writes this in his book *The Radical Cross*, “We have not been redeemed by one person of the Trinity putting Himself against another, but by the three Persons of the Godhead working in glorious harmony.”   
  
Jesus took every bit of Adam’s brokenness to the grave. Jesus came to judge sin and the devil once and for all for lying about who His Father is. And now, you we can see God as He actually is.
