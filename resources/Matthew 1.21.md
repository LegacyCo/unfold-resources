## Commentary
Jesus is the Greek form of the Hebrew name Yeshua or Joshua, which means “Yahweh saves” or “The LORD is salvation.”

The name of Jesus is a revelation. It doesn't come from a family line. The angel told Joseph to name the child *Jesus* because “He will save His people from their sins.” His name announces His mission.

This child is coming to save. 

Many in Israel were longing for deliverance from Rome, oppression, poverty, and exile. But the angel's announcement goes further than political liberation. Israel’s deepest bondage was not Rome. It was sin.

Jesus came to deal with the root, not just the branches.

The connection of Yeshua's name to Joshua is also significant. Joshua led Israel into the promised land, but Jesus brings His people into the greater salvation Joshua could only foreshadow (==Joshua 1:1–9==; ==Hebrews 4:8–10==).
