## Commentary
Jesus had given His disciples power and authority to heal the sick, cast out demons, and preach the kingdom (v1-2). But having power and authority for sign gifts does not translate into the power to be compassionate. Jesus was compassionate on the people who were with Him. He knew their needs and He used His power and authority to creatively meet their needs.
