## Commentary
One thousand years after this psalm was written, the greatest religion the world had ever known, and the finest system of justice the world had ever known, came together to crucify the Son of God. The misunderstanding, betrayal, and denial of close friends added another dimension of mistreatment. The mocking of the soldiers and the crowds deepens both the agony and the irony. The nations raged, as this verse said they would, rising up against the Lord and against His Anointed. And God's response is, "Yet I have set My King, My Son, upon My holy hill."
