## Commentary
The greatest religion the world had ever known, and the finest system of justice the world had ever known, came together to put Jesus on the cross. The misunderstanding, betrayal, and denial of close friends adds another dimension. The mocking of the soldiers and the crowds deepens both the agony and the irony. Finally, the nations rage, as this verse said they would, rising up against the Lord and against his anointed. And God's response is, "Yet I have set my king, my son, upon my holy hill."
