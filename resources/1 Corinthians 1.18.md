## Commentary
The cross looks like foolishness to the wisdom of this age. It appears weak, wasteful, and defeated. But in the mind of God, the cross is wisdom.

God overcomes through surrender, conquers through sacrifice, and brings life through death. His wisdom doesn't preserve the self; it lays the self down. It doesn't grasp for power; it empties itself in love. This is why the cross continually confounds the natural mind.

To live according to this cross-shaped wisdom means our old ways must die. Pride, self-protection, ambition, retaliation, and the need to be understood cannot lead us anymore. Following Jesus will often require choices that make little sense to the world around us.

And those choices will leave scars. Obedience may cost us comfort, approval, opportunities, and relationships. But those scars are not signs that wisdom failed. They are evidence that the cross has marked our lives.

True wisdom will always looks like Jesus.
