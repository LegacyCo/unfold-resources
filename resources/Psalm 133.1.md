## Commentary
“Behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity!” David compares it to the anointing oil poured on Aaron’s head, running down onto his beard and his garments (==Psalm 133:2==). In Israel the oil was poured on the *head*—and from the head it flowed down over the whole body. Christ is the head of His Body (==Colossians 1:18==). When brothers dwell together in unity, the anointing that rests on the Head flows down and touches everyone connected to the Body. Anointing travels through unity.

Then comes the payoff of the psalm: “For *there* the LORD commanded the blessing—life forevermore” (==Psalm 133:3==). Don’t rush past the verb. The blessing isn’t requested there; it’s *commanded* there. Where God’s people dwell together in unity, blessing doesn’t need to be begged for—God orders it to the location. And notice what the commanded blessing is: life. God’s design is that when covenant people dwell together, life is produced—and a community around them encounters a God they’ve never seen, because He reveals Himself through a people who are one (==John 17:21==).

## Word Study
- *together in unity* : **yachad** (Hebrew) — togetherness, union, being joined as one. It’s the same word David uses in worship: “let us exalt His name *together*” (==Psalm 34:3==). Yachad isn’t proximity—people sitting in the same room—it’s lives joined into a shared oneness.

Summary: “there” is the most important word in Psalm 133. The commanded blessing has an address, and the address is *yachad*.
