## Commentary
In Jesus's response to the disciples question, He says the prophecy spoken by Malachi of Elijah coming before the great and dreadful day of the Lord has been fulfilled in the ministry of John the Baptist (see Malachi 4:5). While Israel is waiting for Elijah, God sends John the Baptist — and many missed him. Some missed it so bad, they "did to him whatever they wished."

## Application
We must be careful in our literal interpretations of what God says to us. Like God told Mariam and Aaron about His dealings with Moses,
	"Hear now My words: If there is a prophet among you, I, the LORD, make Myself known to him in a vision; I speak to him in a dream. Not so with My servant Moses; He is faithful in all My house. I speak with him face to face, Even plainly, and not in dark sayings" (Numbers 12:6-8).
The Lord will speak to us, but He is often parabolic in His speaking. "In dark sayings" means riddles, hidden things, allegories, perplexing sayings, difficult questions. We must have the mind of Christ and the interpretation of the Spirit (see 1 Corinthians 2:6-16 for one of the descriptions of the activity of the Holy Spirit).
