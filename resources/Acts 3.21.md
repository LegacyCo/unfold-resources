## Commentary
Jesus is received in heaven until the "restoration of all things." The King will come into an atmosphere that has been made ready and become prepared to receive Him (see cross references below).

## Word Study
The word "restoration" is *apokatastaisis*. It is only used one time in the New Testament. It infers the restoration of creation to the state of existence before the fall. It has the basic meaning of re-establishment of a former state; also, the bringing back of hostages.

Apokatastaisis has a specialized use in astronomical and cosmological communities in the Hellenistic and late classical age. The word became a “technical term for the restitution of the cosmic cycle” of the constellations. Ancient, and particularly Stoic, thought imagined the course of the universe in terms of an infinite series of cyclical cosmic periods, of which the apokatastasis is always the final stage of the old period and the point where the new one begins.

## Cross Reference
See Luke 10:1 for an example of Jesus sending His disciples "before His face into every city and place where He Himself was about to go."

See also Luke 9:52. Jesus sends His disciples ahead of Him "to prepare for Him."

See also Luke 22:8. Jesus sends His disciples ahead of Him to prepare the Passover meal. This is example is interesting because Jesus tells them they will find a "furnished" room. Jesus provides the provision even when He sends us ahead to prepare for His arrival.
