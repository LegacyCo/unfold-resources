## Commentary
It's fascinating that Jude says we are to "keep ourselves" in the love of God. Isn't God the One who does the keeping? Yes, He does. In fact, this is exactly how Jude began his letter, "To those who are...kept (preserved) in Christ." Jude takes the same word from verse one that describes Jesus's action in keeping us, tereo, and applies it to our action of remaining in His love.

As I mentioned in verse 20, the life of the follower of Jesus is not passively waiting for faith to happen to us. We stand on the promise of the gospel and lay hold of those promises. We confidently stand on the victory of the cross and lay hold of everything the sacrifice of Christ purchased for us. The gravitational pull of this age is away from love, away from God, away from righteous living. So Jude sees it necessary to remind us to remain in these things...to keep ourselves positioned in God's love.

## Application
What is one way you can "keep yourself" in the love of God today? 
- Perhaps you recall a promise from Scripture throughout the day like John 17:23, "You (Father) love them them just as You have loved Me." 
- Perhaps you take extra time to sit with the Lord in prayer and meditate on His love for you. 
- You can rewrite a lie you have believed about God's love for you with the truth of Scripture (Rom 5:8, John 3:16, 1 John 3:1, Eph 2:4-5, 1 John 4:19, Ps 86:15 to name a few). 
- Or you could demonstrate His love to someone around you, making it a tangible and visible reminder for yourself in the process. 
There are many ways we can keep ourselves in the love of God. Ask Him to show you how.
