## Commentary
Paul begins his second letter to the Corinthians with a discussion on suffering. After the initial greeting (v1-2) he uses the word comfort in v3. This is the first of ten times he uses the word in the next five verses. The Greek word that we translate "comfort" is *paraklēsis*, which means encouragement, exhortation, appeal, comfort, consolation. This word is a derivative of the same original root word as paraklētos; the word Jesus uses in [[John 14.26]] referring to the Holy Spirit...the *Comforter*. 

God is the God of all paraklēsis and, indeed, is Himself the paraklētos.
