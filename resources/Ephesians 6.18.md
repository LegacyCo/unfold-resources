## Commentary
Praying without ceasing doesn't mean nonstop prayer, we are not heard for our "much speaking" (==Matthew 6:7==). It means to always be in communion with the Lord. Warren Wiersbe writes that we should "never have to say when we pray, 'Lord, we come into Your presence,' because we never left His presence."
