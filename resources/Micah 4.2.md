## Commentary
Compare this end times prophetic vision ("Come, let us go up to the mountain of the Lord") to a similar end times vision in Joel, "Behold, in those days and at that time, when I bring back the captives of Judah and Jerusalem, I will gather all nations and *bring them down* to the Valley of Jehoshaphat; and I will enter into judgment with them there on account of My people, My heritage Israel, whom they have scattered among the nations; they have also divided up My land...multitudes, multitudes in the valley of decision! For the day of the Lord is near in the valley of decision" (Joel 3:1-2).

Everyone ends up in Jerusalem. But how they get there determines the Jerusalem they experience. If they say, "Come let us go up to the mountain of the Lord..." then they experience the glory of Zion. However, if they are gathered there as a result of rebellion, then they experience the judgment of Jehoshaphat. It's the difference between coming to the house of the Lord or being gathered to the Valley of Decision. It's the difference in swords and plowshares, spears and hooks (Micah 4:3, Joel 3:10). It's the difference in going *up* and being brought *down*.
