## Commentary
There are some amazing similarities between this passage and 2 Chronicles 5, when the glory filled the temple Solomon had just built. We know that there were 120 disciples present in the upper room on the day of Pentecost (Acts 1:15). That's also how many priests were present at the inauguration of Solomon's temple. There was also a special measure of unity among the believers in the upper room; they were "with one accord." This is the same language used in 2 Chronicles 5:13; when the singers and musicians "were as one" the glory of the Lord filled the "house."

Just as God's glory had filled the temple in Solomon's day, His glory filled the "new temple" here in Acts 2. 

"Don't you know that you are God's temple" (1 Corinthians 3:16)
