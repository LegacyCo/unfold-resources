## Commentary
The high priest was called and commissioned to stand before the Lord. He was to be clothed in specific garments. These garments were incredibly important. Without them the priest was not set apart (sanctified). But it was the responsibility of someone *else* other than the priest to make these holy garments. The priest was dependent on the gifted artisans, filled with the spirit of wisdom, for the creation of the clothing that would set them apart for ministry unto the Lord.

In this we see the necessity of community, of Spirit-filled artisans within the community, and the role community plays in clothing itself in glory and beauty which permits it to stand before the Lord and minister to Him.
