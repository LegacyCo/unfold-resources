## Commentary
His mysteries and secrets are hidden, but they are not hidden in the dark. They are hidden in the light. He says in Deuteronomy 30:11:
	"This commandment which I command you today is not too mysterious for you, nor is it far off."
It is actually very near...so near that it is "in your mouth and in your heart, so that you may do it" (Deuteronomy 30:14).

## Application
God’s will is often not hidden from us because He is trying to keep us in the dark, but because we keep looking for it in places He never put it. We search for something distant, dramatic, and mysterious, while He points us back to what is near—His word, His voice, and the light He has already given. Many times the next step of obedience is not far off; it is already pressing on your heart and sitting in your mouth. The issue is usually not that God has concealed it, but that we have hesitated to do what He has already made clear.
