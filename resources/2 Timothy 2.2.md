## Commentary
This is from "Covenant Relationships" by Asher Intrater: The first step of discipleship is to discern who has the potential to be faithful. The second step is to choose them to be trained. There are four generations of disciples that Paul mentions in 2 Timothy 2:2: Paul, then Timothy, then the faithful men, then the other faithful men. If discipleship is not seen as developing faithful friendship, it will become a production-line mechanism devoid of intimacy. Paul had an intimate, trusting friendship with Timothy. He was calling Timothy to reproduce that same kind of companionship with the future disciples.
