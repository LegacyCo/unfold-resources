## Commentary
At the center of the Lord's prayer we find Jesus teaching His followers to pray that God's kingdom might come and His will be done "on earth as in heaven." The "kingdom of heaven" is not about people going to heaven. It is about the rule of heaven coming to earth. When Jesus talks about heaven's kingdom, He means that the God of heaven is establishing His sovereign rule not just in heaven, but on earth as well.
