## Commentary
Paul is not saying that Jesus is simply present among His people. He is saying that the church has been chosen as the place where the fullness of Christ is displayed, embodied, and extended to the world.

Bob Gladstone writes this in "A Time To Build": It is not enough that the train of the Lord's robe fills the temple. It is Paul's dream — as an apostle who understands what God's eternal plan is — that the Messiah's FULLNESS suffuse every section of the temple's spacial dimensions. Paul prays that the church would expand its capacity to host the Messiah as the all-consuming Inhabitant and Owner of the house.

Jesus does not intend to occupy a corner of HIs church. He means to fill the whole house with Himself: each room, activity, decision, relationship, gathering, mission...all of it.

The church is the dwelling place of the fullness of the One who fills all things, the house He has purchased for Himself. He is not a guest visiting our houses of worship. We are His house.

## Application
Whether Christ is present when we gather as a church is the wrong question. The right question is whether we have made room for Jesus to fill what rightly belongs to Him.
