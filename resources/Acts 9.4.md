## Commentary
What Saul was doing to believers He was doing to Jesus. They are one and the same...they are His body.

Jesus says the same to us when we mistreat, speak against, divide, and gossip about other believers. We are not just doing those things to another person, we are doing them to Jesus.

## Application
See also Jesus's words in Matthew 25:40 - "And the King will answer and say to them, ‘Truly I say to you, to the extent that you did it for one of the least of these brothers or sisters of Mine, you did it for Me.’"
