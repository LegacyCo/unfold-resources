## Commentary
Paul moves from outward identity (Jew/Gentile) to inward reality. Circumcision was the covenant sign given to Abraham and his descendants, but Paul says the outward *sign* was never meant to replace inward *surrender*. Even under the old covenant, God was never satisfied with external ritual detached from the inner life of the worshiper. He desired truth “in the inward parts” (==Psalm 51:6==). The sacrifice is important, but the heart of the one bringing the sacrifice is what God is after. As Art Katz wrote in Apostolic Foundations, "The things that are a sweet savor to God are the inwards, the inner parts—not the outward things. God is not interested in the outer hide and the flesh; He counts that along with the dung. The inside, in the inner man, worked by Him in the hidden places, born of inward wrestling are the offerings of a sweet and pleasing savor before God." The mark in the flesh was always meant to point to a heart set apart for God.

Paul is showing that true covenant identity has always required more than outward association. God is after the heart. The Spirit does what external religion can never do. 

## Application
God has never been after the appearance of devotion. He wants the inner life. 

So ask yourself: where have I settled for the outward signs of the cultural Christianity while resisting the inward work of the Spirit?

Don't just offer God the visible parts of your life. Bring to Him who you really are.
