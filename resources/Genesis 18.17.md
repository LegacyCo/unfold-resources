## Commentary
As a covenant partner, Abraham was allowed to be part of the deliberations on the coming judgment on Sodom and Gomorrah. Similarly, Moses, as a covenant partner with God, was able to intercede with God for the preservation of the Israelites in the wilderness (see Exodus 32:30-32).

All of God's actions on the earth are done through the relationship of covenant. God is looking for men to prophetically come into agreement with His purposes. Through this joint authority, the intervention of God takes place in the earth. Without such joint authority, there will be no divine interference.
