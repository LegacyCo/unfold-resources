## Commentary
The way we live is ultimately determined by the way that we think. Consider Jesus' rebuke of Peter in ==Mark 8:33== when Jesus speaks to His disciples about the cross for the first time. Peter took Jesus aside and rebuked Him. Jesus responds, "Get behind Me Satan! For you are not setting your *mind* on the things of God, but the things of men." Peter was not living as a carnal man — but he was thinking like one. His thoughts didn't reveal an identity so much as they revealed a trajectory of his life that Jesus was able to correct.
