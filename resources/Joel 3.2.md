## Commentary
In this end times, prophetic vision, Joel prophesies "I will gather all nations and *bring them down* to the valley of Jehoshaphat." If you've been to Jerusalem, you know that this valley, also known as the "valley of decision" borders the city of Jerusalem. This will be an awful moment of judgment for how the nations have treated Israel.

Compare this to those who willingly say, "Come *let us go up* to the mountain of the Lord, to the house of the God of Jacob; He will teach us His ways, and we shall walk in His paths.' For out of Zion the law shall go forth, and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem" (Micah 4:2).

Everyone ends up in Jerusalem. But how they get there determines the Jerusalem they experience. If they say, "Come let us go up to the mountain of the Lord..." then they experience the glory of Zion. However, if they are gathered there as a result of rebellion, then they experience the judgment of Jehoshaphat.

It's the difference between coming to the house of the Lord or being gathered to the Valley of Decision. It's the difference in swords and plowshares, spears and hooks (Micah 4:3, Joel 3:10).

It's the difference in going *up* and being brought *down*.
