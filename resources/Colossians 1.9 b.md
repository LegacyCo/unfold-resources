## Commentary
Paul uses Temple language language here. The Temple was the place where heaven and earth met, where God chose to dwell among His people. But Paul says that the fullness of God is no longer located in a building. It dwells bodily in Christ.

John says the same thing when he writes that the Word became flesh and “dwelt among us” (==John 1:14==). The language is that of God pitching His tent—making His dwelling, in the midst of humanity. Jesus is the true Temple, the place where God and man finally dwell together.

He is also the image of the invisible God, the truly human One standing at the center of creation. In Him, heaven and earth are held together. Through His blood shed on the cross, everything fractured by sin is being reconciled to its Creator.

In Jesus, God has come near, humanity has been made whole, and heaven and earth are being brought together at last.
