## Commentary
When God created man, He gave Him everything, even the authority to rule over His creation as an ambassador. Privilege always carries with it responsibility. The privilege and the responsibility of bearing the image of God was an incredible expression of God's love for man.

Love demands boundaries. Love says I will do *this* and not *that*...even when I want to do *that*, for love I will choose not to. If there are no boundaries, if there is no right or wrong—if there are not two trees in the garden—then there is no love. 

One of the ways I express love for my kids is by showing them what the boundaries are. The boundaries don’t exist because I’m mean or don’t want them to have fun, the boundaries exist because of love; "don't go play in the park by yourself at two in the morning, don't light a camp fire in your bedroom, don't drink bleach." A child doesn’t understand why they can't play in the park by themselves. They don't understand the evil that is waiting for them...because we do everything we can to shield them from that evil…because of love...and to preserve innocence. To a child, the park is just a place for good. But to an adult, we can see the good *and* the evil.

There was a tree in the garden that when man looked at it, he only saw the good. But God could see what man couldn't. And God said that tree will actually hurt you...hurt you so bad it will bring death into your garden. That tree had knowledge in it—knowledge of things that were too grown up for Adam. God was going to teach Adam everything, even the mysteries of what happened before he was created. But for the time being, those things were too big for Adam. So God said, “Let's walk together and I'll show you everything when it's time...I'll be your teacher, I'll be your source of knowledge. That tree is a shortcut. Shortcuts will do nothing but hurt you. So you can eat of any other tree, just don't eat from that one."

Love placed a boundary around the tree of knowledge.

We hear that, and just like a child, there is something in us that can't help but ask “But why?" There's something in us that just has to know. If you ever need proof that God is real and this story is true, just look at man's insatiable desire to know why. That’s evidence that there was once a man in a garden who ate of a tree he shouldn't have.

God alone determines what is right and what is wrong, and when man exalts himself to that position, he creates a god in the image of his own understanding, a god who makes sense to him. The most terrifying thing I can imagine is a god who is like me. 

God chose a tree to be a tree of life, and He chose another tree to be forbidden. God could have chosen a rock and said “Adam, don't move it." He could have shown Adam a stream and said, "don't jump across it." The point is this; God alone determines right and wrong. God alone determines what gives life and what causes death. We don’t get to ask why and then push the boundary to see what happens. “Why” is proof that we’re being tempted by the tree of knowledge. How we respond to what He says is right and wrong determines whether we are actually walking in life or death, whether we are receiving life from His Spirit or physically alive, yet spiritually dead.

## Application
This passage shows us that God is not just the Creator, He is Lord. 

Most people don’t have a problem worshipping the Creator. But tell someone that there is a King who is Lord of all, and that He demands obedience to what He says is right and wrong, and you’ll see real quick whether they are spiritually alive or dead. 

Is He Creator? Yes. 
Is He Savior? Yes. 
Is He Healer? Yes. 
Is He merciful and kind and better than you could ever imagine? Yes. 

And He is Lord. 

As Lord, He alone determines what is right and, yes, what is wrong. Is He Lord of your life? Is He the King of your kingdom? If God is not Lord of your life then nothing else that I or anyone else can ever say about God matters until you submit to His rule and reign as sovereign Lord over your life.

As God tells Israel in Deuteronomy 30:19-20, 

"I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing; therefore choose life, that both you and your descendants may live; that you may love the LORD your God, that you may obey His voice, and that you may cling to Him, for He is your life and the length of your days."
