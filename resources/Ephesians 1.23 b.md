## Commentary
Paul calls the church Christ’s Body, “the fullness of Him who fills all in all.”

An individual believer can experience extraordinary things with God. You can pray alone, worship alone, study Scripture alone, and encounter Jesus in the secret place. But there are dimensions of Christ you cannot receive alone because Jesus has chosen to distribute His life through His Body.

Eventually, individual Christianity reaches a ceiling.

You cannot see all your own blind spots. You cannot supply every gift you need. You cannot always encourage yourself in the storm, correct your own deception, confirm your own calling, or carry every burden without becoming crushed beneath it.

The eye possesses sight but cannot walk. The hand can grasp but cannot hear. Each member carries something real, but no member carries everything (==1 Corinthians 12:14-21==).

This is not a flaw in God’s design. It *is* the design.

Jesus has arranged His Body so that receiving from Him requires humility toward others. The strength you need may have been placed in a brother. The correction that protects you may come through a sister. The answer to your prayer may be carried by a member whose gift looks nothing like yours.

Isolation promises protection, but it limits our experience of Christ. When we detach from the Body, we don't just distance ourselves from difficult people. We distance ourselves from resources of heaven that Jesus has chosen to place within them.

## Application
Where have you reached the limit of what you can do alone?

Instead of treating that limit as failure, recognize it as an invitation into the Body. Allow someone to know you, encourage you, correct you, pray for you, and supply what Christ has placed within them.

You were never expected to be the whole Body. You were expected to belong to it.
