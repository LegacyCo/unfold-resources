## Commentary
“You crown the year with Your goodness, and Your paths drip with abundance.”

Abundance is attached to *His* paths. The verse does not say every path drips with abundance; it says God's do. That is both the freedom and the restriction of walking with Him. The freedom is to walk wherever His path may lead—and His paths go to places no one could reach on their own. The restriction is to stay within the boundaries of that path. Someone may ask, “What if I don't like His path? What if I want to walk on that one over there?” That path will not drip with abundance. The abundance is not portable; it belongs to the way He has laid out.

The same structure runs through Psalm 91: “He who dwells in the secret place of the Most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty” (==Psalm 91:1==). The freedom is to fly with Him. The restriction is to fly underneath the shadow of His wing. Step out from under the shadow and He is no longer functioning as your refuge and fortress—not because His care has failed, but because you have left the place where it operates. God's restrictions are never withholding; they mark the boundaries inside which His goodness flows (==Psalm 23:3==; ==Proverbs 3:5-6==).
