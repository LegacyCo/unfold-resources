## Commentary
In all of Scripture Abel never says a word, yet his blood continues to speak. In what way does Abel speak? 

Hebrews 11 says that “by faith Abel offered to God a more excellent sacrifice than Cain,” and that “through it he being dead still speaks” (==Hebrews 11:4==). That means Abel’s testimony did not end with his murder. His faith continued to bear witness long after his life. Cain may have taken Abel’s life, but he could not take Abel’s witness.

## Application
A life lived by faith can preach to generations we will never meet. Our surrender, obedience, suffering, and trust in God can testify long after our words are forgotten. This is how valuable faith is in the economy of the kingdom.

We owe it to future generations to live surrendered lives of faith that testify, even after we are gone, that He is worthy of our faith.
