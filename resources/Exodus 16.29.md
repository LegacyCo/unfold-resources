## Commentary
God gave Israel the gift of the Sabbath to bring them into His rhythm of rest. If Israel did not discern the season they were in then they would squander the blessing of the 6th and not be able to enter into the rest of the 7th. As long as God's people remained aware of Yahweh's seasonal rhythm then they could respond accordingly.

The mature saint does not squander the season of blessing. Likewise, they are able to rest in the season of the 7th day/year Sabbath (see Leviticus 25:20-21).

For example, Paul understood that there are seasonal rhythms in the kingdom, that there would be seasons of abounding (the 6th) and seasons of resting (the 7th). Because He understood God's ways, He could recognize a season of "suffering need" for what it was — seasonal, "Not that I speak in regard to need, for I have learned in whatever state I am, to be content: I know how to be abased, and I know how to abound. Everywhere and in all things I have learned both to be full and to be hungry, both to abound and to suffer need. I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me" (Philippians 4:11-13).

Another example of the importance of God's people to understand His rhythmic seasons is the parable of the wise and foolish virgins (Matthew 25:1-13). 

While the bridegroom was delayed, the wise virgins were able to rest because they had enough oil to sustain them. They had taken advantage of the season prior to the delay to store up for themselves what was needed to allow them to rest. 

The foolish virgins, on the other hand, squandered the season of the 6th day by not storing for the 7th. When they needed to access more oil on a day when they should have had it in reserve there was none to access. The wedding began, the door closed, the seasons changed, and they were exposed as unprepared squanderers who did not discern the season.

Understanding God's seasons and being confident that our Heavenly Father will provide for us makes us content with what we have and keeps us from being anxious about what we do not have. Since we are secure in His love and who He is as Father, we are free from the anxiety about the future, for the future, just like the present is provided for by God (see Matthew 6:34).
