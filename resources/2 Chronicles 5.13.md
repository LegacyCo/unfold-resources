## Commentary
There are some amazing similarities between this passage and Acts 2, when the glory of God filled the disciples on the Day of Pentecost. We know that there were 120 disciples present in the upper room (Acts 1:15). That's also how many priests were present here at the inauguration of Solomon's temple. There was also a special measure of unity among the believers in the upper room; they were "with one accord." This is the same language used here regarding the singers and musicians; they "were as one" and the glory of the Lord filled the "house of God."
