## Commentary
Adam and Eve's eyes were opened to know they were naked. Flesh had been given a place in the garden. Flesh produced shame, something they were unfamiliar with. Shame quickly gave way to fear. Deception was the table they ate from, shame and fear was the result.

## Cross Reference
See Romans 8 for Paul's discussion on the difference between flesh and Spirit.
