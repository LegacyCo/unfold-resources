## Commentary
Paul says the Body of Christ grows and builds itself up in love “when each part is working properly.”

Many people carry real wounds from the church. They trusted leaders who misused authority. They reached for help and were ignored. They exposed weakness and were met with judgment. Some of their deepest pain came through people who claimed to represent Jesus.

Jesus does not dismiss those wounds. He weeps with those who weep, draws near to the brokenhearted, and brings hidden things into the light (==Psalm 34:18==; ==Luke 12:2==). The failures of people who used His name do not reveal indifference in His heart.

But we must also refuse to allow those failures to separate us permanently from the Body He loves.

The answer to broken church is not pretending nothing happened. It is not protecting institutions at the expense of wounded people. But neither is the answer permanent isolation, suspicion, and noncommitment. Darkness is not defeated by removing more light.

Paul says the Body is built when every part supplies what it carries. That means the church you long to see requires your participation. You may be carrying the compassion someone else needs, the courage to confront what others ignored, the hospitality you once searched for, or the kindness you prayed someone would show you.

Become for someone else what you needed someone to be for you.

Church hurt tells you to protect yourself by remaining outside the family. Jesus invites you to heal without surrendering your place within it. Your wound does not disqualify you from helping build something healthier. In the hands of Christ, it may become the place from which you recognize and care for wounds others cannot see.

The church remains God’s plan—not because she has always represented Him well, but because Jesus has not withdrawn His love, presence, or purpose from her.

## Application
Take your seat at the table.

Ask what Christ has placed within you to supply to the Body. Love, serve, tell the truth, protect the vulnerable, welcome the overlooked, and become an answer to your own prayers for His people.

The Body grows when each part stops waiting for someone else to become the church.
