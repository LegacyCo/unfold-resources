## Commentary
Chistotham, the bishop of Constantinople in the late 300's had this charge to his church, "Make for yourselves a guestchamber in your own home. Set up a bed there and a table and a candlestick. Have a room to which Christ may come. Say 'this is Christ's room...set apart for Him.'" This came to be called the "Christ room" and was common practice for any Christian of the middleclass and up for 100's of years. All guests were to be welcomed as Christ..."for I was a stranger and you welcomed Me."
