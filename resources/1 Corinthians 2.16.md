## Commentary
Jesus frees His people from anything that would hold them down. Paul writes in Colossians 3:2 that we are to set our minds on "things above." This is only possible because Jesus has overcome the gravitational pull of this age. Not only are we "seated in heavenly places" (Ephesians 2:6), but we have the ability to meditate on things that the world has no access to. We have the mind of Christ.
