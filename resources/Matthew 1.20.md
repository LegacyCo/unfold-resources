## Commentary
Dreams are one of the ways that God speaks to His people. That was true during Bible times, and it's true today. 

Between this verse and Matthew 2:22, Matthew records six instances of God speaking through a dream. Matthew is beginning his story of the Messiah by pointing out the supernatural ways God intervened in ordinary people's lives to shape history. 

Scripture is full of stories just like this where God communicates to people through a dream. For example: 

-Jacob: God confirms His covenant promises (Gen 28:10-22)

-Joseph: God shows him the trajectory of his life (Gen 37:5-11)

-Pharaoh: God warns him about the coming famine (Gen 41)

-Solomon: God gives him the gift of wisdom (1 Kings 3:5-15)

-Nebuchadnezzar: God warns him about pride (Dan 4)

-Paul: God tells him to remain in Corinth (Acts 18:9-10)

These stories are not in the Bible simply to serve as historical record. They show us one of the ways God speaks into the human situation. 

Our family has been greatly impacted by dreams. The trajectory of our story has been shaped by God speaking to us in dreams. Each major move that we have made was initiated by a dream. Of course, we don't receive a dream without carefully considering it, praying into it, submitting it to others we trust and are in relationship with, and often waiting for a confirmation. But with childlike faith, when we have a dream we believe is from the Lord, we do our best to steward that dream well.

## Application
If you're not sure if you've ever had a dream from the Lord, ask Him to speak to you in a dream tonight. James says, "You have not because you ask not" (James 4:2). You may wonder, "Well, how do I know if it's my own thoughts or the Lord?" With faith like a child, trust that the Good Shepherd knows how to speak to His sheep, His sheep know His voice, and will not follow the voice of another (John 10:27-28). 

I find that dreams from the Lord tend to have very specific details: colors, numbers, objects, places. Those details have deeper meaning: the number seven can mean complete wholeness or rest, the color red can mean covenant, a car can mean a ministry assignment, a person can represent a season you were in when you knew them or a personality trait they are known for, etc. When you have a dream that you believe could be from the Lord, take it to Him in prayer. He will give you understanding. Jesus says in John 16:13 that one of the roles of the Holy Spirit is to, "Guide you into all truth." He will guide you into the truth of what that dream means. If you have people around you who are gifted at interpreting dreams, ask them what they think it could mean.

God is still speaking to His people. And dreams are one of the ways He speaks.
