## Commentary
==Acts 7:22== tells us Moses was “mighty in words and deeds” when he was forty years old. In other words, he was ready to set Israel free. He had strength, education, influence, and zeal. From the outside, it would have seemed like the perfect time for Moses to make his mark.

But God waited another forty years.

That may seem wasteful. We tend to measure readiness by gifting and ability. But God measures readiness differently. Before Moses could be sent, he first had to be emptied. Before he could stand before Pharaoh, he had to lose confidence in himself.

So when God finally calls him here in Exodus 3, Moses does not say, “I’ve been waiting for this.” He says, “Who am I?”

God had not wasted the wilderness years. He had used it to disqualify Moses from self-reliance so He could qualify him according to His standards: humility, obedience, dependence, surrender.
