## Commentary
The exiles who returned from Babylon sang this song to the Lord when the foundation of the temple was laid. It is the same song that was sung in 2 Chronicles 5:13 when Solomon was blessing the temple after it was completed.

Jeremiah had prophesied that this song will be sung again in Jerusalem after the exile of seventy years (Jeremiah 33:11). In this prophecy, Jeremiah points to the day when the Branch of righteousness will execute judgment and righteousness in the earth...and Judah will be saved. It is a Messianic prophecy.

This song is a song of the house of God. A song to be sung when the Lord's house is established. It's a song that the Lord longs to hear in our generation.
