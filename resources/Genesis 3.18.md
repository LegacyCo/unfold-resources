## Commentary
It is important for us to know the role of eating in the kingdom of God...or to put it another way, it is vital that we eat well. I don't mean making sure we get more vegetables in our diet, I mean rightly discerning spiritual things. There is a thread of eating woven through Scripture. As we follow that thread, we see the redemption story clearly.

For Adam, eating well was a matter of life and death. As long as he ate from the proper tree, he received life from what he ate. But if he ate from the wrong source, he would receive death. 

And when Adam chose to eat of the wrong tree, everything changed.

Many of us think God kicked Adam out of the garden as a punishment. But in reality, God had to remove Adam from the garden lest he still have access to the tree of life and eat from it. Even though he had eaten from the wrong tree, life is always greater than death. Even in his sin, if Adam were to continue to eat from the tree of life, he would live forever,
	"Then the LORD God said, “Behold, the man has become like one of Us, knowing good and evil; and now, he might reach out with his hand, and take fruit also from the tree of life, and eat, and live forever”—therefore the LORD God sent him out of the Garden of Eden, to cultivate the ground from which he was taken." (Genesis 3:22-23)
Even the curse that Adam experienced as a result of sin was tied to eating,
	"Cursed is the ground because of you; 
      With hard labor you shall eat from it 
      All the days of your life...By the sweat of your face 
      You shall eat bread." (Genesis 3:17-19)
From now on, Adam would eat by toiling. The grace to receive life from what he ate was removed.

Jesus says in John 6 that He is living bread, and whoever eats of His flesh receives His life. He is for us what the tree of life was for Adam. Coming to the table of the Lord is coming to the source of life and receiving the grace that Adam lost.

What makes this meal life giving? What makes this table different from every other table? Is there something special about this bread and this cup? Think of it this way; what made Peter's shadow unique? What made Paul's handkerchief special? What makes God's word powerful? When the spiritual reality of something is joined to the faith of an individual, what God says about that something becomes realized. It's just a shadow, until someone believes what Psalm 91 says, that we take refuge under the shadow of His wings, or as Malachi 4:2 says, "there is healing in his wings." It's just a Bible verse, until someone reads it and believes that it's true. 

And the loaf, it's just bread, until someone receives it as the life-giving bread of God that stepped down from heaven to restore us back to the garden.

## Application
When we receive by faith what Jesus says is His flesh and blood, we receive His grace. We become partakers of His divine life. Access to the tree of life has been restored in Christ. Come all who are thirsty, drink. "You who have no money, come buy and eat...Why do you spend money for what is not bread...Listen carefully to Me, and eat what is good, and delight yourself in abundance" (Isaiah 55:1-3).
