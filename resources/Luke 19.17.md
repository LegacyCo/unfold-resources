## Commentary
This passage speaks to the early stages of discipleship. Part of the process of discipleship is discerning who can be trusted with the resources of heaven. The training that follows is a series of experiments in which a disciple is given ever-increasing areas of trust in which to respond. If one is faithful in a certain sphere, he can be given the next stage. If one is faithful with little, he is likely to be faithful with much. As we can see from this parable, one proceeds from smaller to larger. Each task represents a test for the next stage. 

Commenting on this passage in Luke, Asher Intrater writes this in his book "Covenant Relationships," "A change in type of responsibility is given. The first area is one of service in objects, money and practicalities. The later stage is one dealing more with authority, people, governance and teaching. One is first a servant with objects. Then he may be granted governance with people. Here the people are described as a city, which could be parallel to a home group or a pastorate in the ministries today."
