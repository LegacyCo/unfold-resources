## Commentary
“Good and upright is the Lord; therefore He teaches sinners in the way.”

David connects two things we frequently separate: the goodness of God and His response to sinners. Because the Lord is good, He shows sinners the way.

Religion often presents God as standing at the end of the road with His arms folded, waiting to see whether a sinner can find the right path, correct their behavior, and make themselves presentable enough to approach Him. David gives us a different picture. God show the way to Him to those who have lost it.

That's what Jesus did throughout the Gospels. He found people in their blindness, shame, confusion, and rebellion and revealed the Father to them. He ate with tax collectors and sinners—not because their sin was insignificant, but because they would never find freedom from it without first encountering the goodness of God (==Romans 2:4==).

God’s holiness doesn't make Him reluctant to approach sinners. His holiness means that everything He does is completely free from corruption—including the way He loves, confronts, restores, and forgives them. He can tell a woman to leave her life of sin without condemning her (==John 8:11==). He can expose what is buried in the heart while offering living water to the person carrying it (==John 4:14-18==).

## Application
Where have you allowed your failure to convince you that God no longer wants to lead you?

Your sin may explain how you became lost, but it does not change the nature of the One who comes looking for you. Turn toward Him. The God who confronts your wandering is the same God who will show you the way home.
