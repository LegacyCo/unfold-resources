## Commentary
The gospel leaves no room for boasting. If righteousness came through law-keeping, religious status, ethnic privilege, morality, knowledge, discipline, or human effort, then someone somewhere would have reason to boast. But the gospel leaves no room for that sort of boasting.

Justification is by faith apart from works of the law. Faith receives what grace gives. Faith does not come to God holding a résumé. Faith comes empty-handed, trusting the sufficiency of Christ. This destroys pride at the root.

The gospel humbles everyone equally. The moral person is not saved by morality. The religious person is not saved by religion. The broken person is not too far gone. The outsider is not too far away. All must come the same way: through Jesus Christ, by grace, through faith.
