## Commentary
Paul gathers together a chain of Old Testament quotations to make one devastating point: sin is not just a Gentile problem. It is not just a Jewish problem. It is a *human* problem. Every person stands guilty before God.

This is one of the great leveling passages in Scripture. Paul is not saying every person is as evil as they could possibly be. But he is saying sin has touched the whole of each of us. The mind, the will, the desires, the tongue, the path of life, and the posture before God have all been affected by sin. There is no part of humanity that remains untouched by the fall.

This confronts the modern assumption that people are basically good and only need better information, better systems, or better opportunities. We don't need improvement. We need rescue. We don't need instruction. We need a transfer of righteousness from outside ourselves.
