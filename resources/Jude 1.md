## Commentary
Jude uses a specific word for those he is addressing this letter to: egapemenois. It's a verb that means "to be loved, passionately loved." It comes from the root word agape. This letter is addressed to those who are called, passionately loved by the Father, and kept (preserved, watched over) in Jesus. That's you.

## Application
Do you see yourself as beloved by God...passionately loved by Him? Take a moment and ask the Holy Spirit to reveal the love of God to your heart. Ask Him to show you times in your life when He has preserved you and watched over you.
