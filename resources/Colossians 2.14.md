## Commentary
Paul says God has “canceled the certificate of debt consisting of decrees against us, which was hostile to us; and He has taken it out of the way, having nailed it to the cross.”

Understand what that certificate was, and you’ll understand why the enemy is so desperate to keep you from believing it’s gone. Authority is everything in the spiritual realm. Satan’s weapon against you was never raw power—it was legal standing. The certificate of debt recorded every sin, every accusation, every agreement you made with lies and death, every foothold the enemy had the right to use against you. As long as that record stood, the accuser had a case (==Revelation 12:10==).

Then Jesus took the document and nailed it to the cross. Everything you and I did, or ever will do, that was contrary to God’s design was placed on Jesus as though it belonged to Him (==2 Corinthians 5:21==). When He paid the debt we owed—and when we repented and surrendered our lives to Him—the enemy lost every bit of legal ground he had in us.

The bank can’t come take your house once the mortgage is paid off. It can send threatening letters. It can call and insinuate. But it has no claim, and both parties know it. That’s the position of every believer whose debt is nailed to the cross: the enemy still talks, but he no longer holds the paperwork. The only fight left in him is deception—working to make you live as if a canceled debt were still on the books.
