## Commentary
Paul says that God’s purpose is for His manifold wisdom to be made known “by the church to the principalities and powers in the heavenly places.”

The church is preaching a sermon to the spiritual realm.

God did not choose to display His wisdom through an argument, institution, building, or impressive individual. He chose a people. Every time people who were once divided become one in Christ, the church announces that Jesus has accomplished what no earthly power could accomplish.

Jews and Gentiles had been separated by generations of hostility, yet the cross created “one new man” from both (==Ephesians 2:14-16==). Slaves and masters, rich and poor, men and women, educated and uneducated were gathered around one table and given one Spirit. Their unity declared that the powers which organize the world through fear, hierarchy, hostility, and division had met a stronger kingdom.

This is why the enemy works so aggressively to divide the church. Our unity is not just relational harmony. It is spiritual warfare.

When believers forgive offenses, bear with weakness, refuse gossip, cross cultural divisions, and remain at the table when walking away would be easier, the church displays the wisdom of the cross. The powers see a community held together by something stronger than preference.

A divided church may still preach accurate sermons, but it contradicts the sermon God intends the church herself to preach.

The wisdom of God is not merely spoken from the pulpit. It is displayed by a people who could not remain together without Jesus.

## Application
Consider what your relationships within the church are announcing to the spiritual realm.

Every act of humility, forgiveness, patience, and covenant love declares that the cross is stronger than the forces attempting to divide us. Do not reduce reconciliation to relational maintenance. Your refusal to divide is part of God’s public demonstration of victory.

## Word Study

- _manifold_ : **polypoikilos** — many-sided, richly varied, or composed of many different forms. The word describes something like a many-faceted jewel whose beauty becomes visible as each side catches the light.

The church displays God’s _polypoikilos_ wisdom because no single person, culture, gift, or expression can reveal it all. His wisdom becomes visible through the beautiful difference of a people made one in Christ.

Summary: the diversity of the church is the canvas upon which He displays the many-sided wisdom of the cross.
