## Commentary
The creation story is a story of God *speaking*. As ==Hebrews 11:3== says, "By faith we understand that the worlds were framed by the word of God, so that the things which are seen were not made of things which are visible."

Psalm 33:6, 9 says, "By the word of the Lord the heavens were made...for He spoke and it was."

God's Word contains within itself the power to create something from nothing—to bring chaos into order. 


## Application
Is there an area of your life that seems "formless and void"? Is there chaos around you that needs to come into order? Stand on the Word of the Lord and release life into your situation. We are, of course, not the Creator, but as image bearers of the Creator, filled with the same Spirit that hovered over the chaos and brought it into order, we are entrusted with the high calling of bringing His *world* into order with His *design*. ==Proverbs 18:21== says death and life are in the power of the tongue. Choose to speak life over your home, your family, your community. Declare the promises of God over the areas around you that are disagreeing with the victory of the cross.
