## Commentary
All beginnings must begin with God. When we acknowledge Him as the source of all, and the beginning that gives meaning to everything that comes after it, then our lives are ordered according to His design. No chaos outside of us will have the power to influence the peaceful order within us. When our days begin with Him, our days are ordered according to His wisdom. When our blessings and resources are first given back to Him, He multiplies and gives increase as only He can. When our lives are built on Him, they have a solid foundation.

While we can't see God in physical form (for now), we can know Him by His actions...we can see the ripple effects of His character. As Paul writes in ==Romans 1:19-20==, "What may be known of God is evident...For since the creation of the world His invisible attributes, that is, His eternal power and divine nature, have been clearly perceived, being understood by what has been made."

And as David writes in ==Psalm 19:1-2==, "The heavens tell of the glory of God; and their expanse declares the work of His hands. Day to day pours forth speech, and night to night reveals knowledge."

In the book of Ephesians, Paul points to this first verse of the Bible and concludes *that's actually not the beginning of the story...*

"He chose us in Him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blame before Him in love, having predestined us to adoption as sons by Jesus Christ to Himself, according to the good pleasure of His will" (==Ephesians 1:4-5==).

For people on this side of the cross, the creation story isn’t just that God created…it’s that God knew us and loved us and intended for us to bear the image of Jesus in a world that wasn't even formed yet. God's plan for you is that you would now reflect His image into the world around you…that you would show the world what God looks like…and then multiply that image in the earth (what Jesus calls "make disciples").


## Word Study
In this verse, "God" is translated from the Hebrew word Elohim. The form of Elohim used in this verse is *plural*. In the very beginning of God’s story we see the mystery of the Trinity. Before there was a universe, there was a complete communion God enjoyed within Himself. In creating this universe, God expressed the love that He always enjoyed within Himself. He didn’t need to create, but that’s what love does. Love will always produce life. Creation is the outflow of the love of God that existed before the foundation of the world. Because of love, God invited us into the circle of life that He has always known within Himself. This is almost too marvelous to consider.


## Application
Where will you find God today? In a song, a stranger's laugh, the tears of a loved one, a poem, a soft breeze, an embrace? God is all around us...waiting to be discovered.

Where will you *reflect* God today? To a hurting friend, a stranger in need, a challenging relationship in need of forgiveness, to the person in the mirror? God lives in you...waiting to be reflected to the world around you.
