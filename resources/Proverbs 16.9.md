## Commentary
Planning is good, right, and necessary. Wisdom does not reject preparation. It simply refuses to confuse preparation with control.

Proverbs 16:9 tells us that we make plans in our hearts, but the Lord directs our steps. Our plans are not the final word; they are a platform for the desires of God to land on and be accomplished. We prepare faithfully, move intentionally, and think carefully, but we remain surrendered enough for God to interrupt, redirect, delay, or completely reshape what we intended.

The problem begins when we hold our plans too tightly. What was meant to serve the will of God can become an idol that competes with it. We may begin asking God to bless our agenda rather than allowing Him to establish His own.

A surrendered plan says, “This is what I believe is wise, but You are free to lead me another way.” That kind of posture does not make planning pointless. It makes planning usable. God can guide what is open in our hands, but what we refuse to release may have to be dismantled before He can lead us forward.
