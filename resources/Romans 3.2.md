## Commentary
By “oracles”, he means the spoken words, declarations, promises, commands, covenants, and revelations of God that had been entrusted to Israel.

The word _oracle_ can sound strange in English. But in Scripture, “oracles of God” simply means the words God has spoken.

So Paul’s point is this: Israel had a massive privilege. God had entrusted them with His revealed word.

They had the Law.  

They had the covenants.  

They had the prophets.  

They had the temple worship.  

They had the Scriptures.  

They had the story of redemption unfolding through them.

Paul is not saying the Jews were better than everyone else in themselves. In Romans 2, he has already shown that possessing the law does not make someone righteous if they disobey it. But he is saying they had a real advantage: God had spoken to them in a way He had not spoken to the nations.

That is why the privilege also carried responsibility.

To be entrusted with the oracles of God means Israel was meant to receive, preserve, obey, and bear witness to God’s word before the nations.
