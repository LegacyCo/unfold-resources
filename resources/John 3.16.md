## Commentary
Jesus's use of the word cosmos in John 3:16, 17, and 19 doesn't *just* refer to people, He means all of creation; the universe...everything "not God." 

The most well-known verse in the Bible is not just an expression of God's love for humanity, but for all of His creation which has suffered decay since sin entered.

God sent His Son into the cosmos so that the cosmos might be saved and restored through Him.
