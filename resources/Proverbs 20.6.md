## Commentary
This is from "Covenant Relationships" by Asher Intrater - The purpose of covenant is to ensure faithfulness and commitment to the very relationships that are so precious in God's sight. We live in a world where many people talk about how they love one another in insincere ways. Introducing the concept of covenant is a refreshing challenge to back up the claims of love.
  
In this verse, faithfulness does not mean confidence or boldness so much as the persevering integrity to hold to a covenant without breaking it. What a widespread meaning this verse has for us today! Many people claim to have all love, but those who are really true to their covenants are harder to find.
