## Commentary
The supernatural manifestation of the Holy Spirit was incomplete to bring the masses to repentance. The moment required an image bearer, filled with the Spirit of God, to take his stand a bring understanding for those who were asking, "Whatever could this mean?" (Acts 2:12). The goal of Peter taking his stand was v37, the crowds responding, "What shall we do?"

## Application
As amazing as it is when God manifests His power in signs and wonders, He still looks for men and women who can declare truth to the crowds, who can explain "whatever could this mean?" Pray for the signs that grab the attention of the world around you, and take your stand when God moves in power. Don't worry about what you will say in that moment. As Jesus tells His disciples in Matthew 10:19-20, "It will be given to you in that hour what you should speak; for it is not you who speak, but the Spirit of your Father who speaks in you."
