## Commentary
This is a quote by Dr. Robert Gladstone from his book *A Time to Build*: Time is God's weapon. He uses it His way, not ours...If God gives a promise and then decides to wait a few years or centuries before He fulfills it, why should we then come to the conclusion that He will never fulfill His promise? As long as there is time on the clock, God's unfulfilled promises can still be fulfilled. Let the Master of time do His work.

## Application
Don’t confuse delay with denial. If God has spoken, your responsibility is not to force the promise into your timeline, but to remain faithful while the appointed time unfolds. Waiting doesn't mean nothing is happening. It means God is working in ways you cannot yet measure.
