## Commentary
Ezekiel 36:23 is spoken as a rebuke. God’s people had given the nations around them the wrong image of who He is—His great name had been profaned, polluted, among the peoples. And listen to how God says He’ll fix it: “I will sanctify My great name… and the nations shall know that I am the LORD… when I am hallowed in you before their eyes.” When I demonstrate My holiness—*through you*.

That little phrase carries God’s entire strategy. The primary way God interacts with the world is through His people. When something is not right in the earth, His go-to method of intervention is the people who know Him. Look at the pattern in Exodus 3: God saw Israel’s oppression, heard their cry, and came down to deliver them—and then said, “Come now, Moses, and *I will send you*” (==Exodus 3:10==). If I were Moses I might have said, “God, wouldn’t this be easier if *You* went to Pharaoh?” But that’s not His plan. When it was time for deliverance, God found a man, revealed Himself to that man, and sent him.

It’s the same today. The world is desperate for a people who can show them who God really is—which is why it’s essential that we actually know Him, live according to His way, and lead like Jesus rather than like religion, culture, or preference. The world needs to see God, not us.

So yes—pray for the salvation of your community. That’s good and right. But pray with the awareness that God is looking at *you* to become the answer to your own prayers. We don’t save anyone; there’s no other name under heaven by which men are saved (==Acts 4:12==). But the saved have a commission: show the world what salvation looks like. We don’t just pray and wait for God to show up. We pray, we believe Him for impossible things—and we live lives that rightly represent Him while we do.
