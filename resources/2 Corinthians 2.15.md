## Commentary
“For we are to God the fragrance of Christ among those who are being saved.” The verse before says God “diffuses the fragrance of His knowledge through us in every place” (==2 Corinthians 2:14==). Paul’s picture is a Roman triumphal procession—incense filling the streets as the victorious general parades through the city. The aroma went everywhere the procession went. Nobody in the city had to attend a meeting to smell it.

That’s how the knowledge of God spreads through your life. A fragrance doesn’t argue. It diffuses—it fills the atmosphere of whatever room it enters. And whether you realize it or not, your life is diffusing something in every place God carries you.

Your generosity when a stranger is in need emits a fragrance. Your kindness when someone is sad emits a fragrance. Your humility when everyone around you is selfishly grabbing at resources emits a fragrance. Your hospitality toward the lonely emits a fragrance. The way you treat your spouse is emitting a fragrance. The way you raise your children is emitting a fragrance. None of those are sermons. All of them are diffusing the knowledge of God in your workplace, your neighborhood, your kids’ school—*in every place*.

This is why the everyday believer leading an ordinary, faithful life is not a spectator to the Great Commission. The world is coming to conclusions about Jesus based on what it picks up from the people who carry Him. The fragrance you release with your life can transform the world as much as any sermon ever preached—because it reaches the rooms no preacher will ever be invited into.

## Application
Ask the Holy Spirit two questions this week. First: “What does my life smell like to the people closest to me?” Not your theology—your aroma. Ask Him to show you what your spouse, your kids, your coworkers actually pick up when you walk in. Second: “Where have You placed me to diffuse You?” Make a short list of the places you already go every week—job, gym, school pickup line, the neighbor’s driveway. Those aren’t interruptions to your mission. According to Paul, those are the “every place” God intends to fill with the knowledge of Himself through you.
