## Commentary
Whatever God touches becomes holy. We understand that about the dirt underneath Moses' feet, but God wants to show the world what He does with dirty people, not just pieces of dirt. And if you believe that Jesus is who He says He is, then God has not just touched you, He has filled you...just like He filled the temple in the days of Solomon. It wasn't the assembling together of stones that made the temple holy, it was God filling that space with Himself. But God doesn't dwell in houses made with human hands...He dwells inside of people. *You* are the house not made with human hands.
