## Commentary
Their is a renewing of strength that takes place as we wait on God. The strength required for a previous season isn't enough for where He is bringing you. *New* strength is needed for that season. Or to say it another way, the same strength that got you here is not sufficient to take you were you're going. Waiting gives God the time necessary to re-*new* your strength. He is a good Father, He is not going to allow you to be put in a situation where you would run and grow weary. The waiting is His goodness that builds up a strength that will keep you from fainting.
