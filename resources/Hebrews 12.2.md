## Commentary
This is from "Evangelical, Sacramental, and Pentecostal" by Gordon T. Smith: Jesus, to whom we look, is the pioneer and perfecter of faith. He is a pioneer in that he goes before us, leading the way, establishing the path and the example. We follow Jesus. But he is also perfecter in that through his work, he makes possible our following.
