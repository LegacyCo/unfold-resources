## Commentary
Moses was faithful over the Father's house as a servant, Jesus as a Son. We are of the same house of the Son if we hold fast our confidence and celebration of hope. Our confidence and hope is in who He is; kind. We have to know Him as such. Just as important is knowing who we are: sons and daughters; or else we act like servants (Moses).

The writer cautions against this a few verses later in v12-13, "Beware, brethren, lest there be in any of you an evil heart of unbelief in departing from the living God; but exhort one another daily, while it is called 'Today,' lest any of you be hardened through the deceitfulness of sin." Sin - being out of alignment with our intended design. If He says we are sons, but we think and act like servants, we are not in alignment with who He says we are. When we don't believe we are sons and daughters, we will live as Old Covenant servants of the law. There is no rest appointed for servants of the law; the promise remains of rest for New Covenant sons, not Old Covenant servants.
