## Commentary
It could be said that Genesis 3 is one of the most important chapters in the Bible. What happens in this chapter is the reason why the world is the way it is, why bad things happen, why people do things they know will hurt them and others. Genesis 3 is why we feel things like anger and jealousy and sadness and pain. Genesis 3 is one of the most important chapters in the Bible because Genesis 3 is the story of everyone who has ever lived. 

Paul writes in Romans ==5:12== that through one man, Adam, sin entered the world and death spread to all, because all have sinned. Translation: you and I are here in Genesis 3.

Genesis 3 also shows us that there is a world beyond this world — a spiritual dimension where darkness is waging war against light, where an angelic rebellion in heaven led to an army of powers and principalities from that world becoming violently opposed to those who bear God's image in this world.

If this is true, if there are, as Paul calls them in Ephesians 6, spiritual powers and principalities that we war against, then the only remedy against these dark spiritual forces is a greater spiritual force of light. Until we come under the shadow of a greater power, we will continue to experience the effects of Genesis 3 — when the mother and father of us all surrendered their authority to the enemy of us all.
