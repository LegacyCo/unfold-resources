## Commentary
Jesus intercedes for us in heaven. The Spirit intercedes for us in our hearts (==Romans 8:27==). We are being prayed for both in heaven and on earth by the Godhead. Or to put it another way: The One who died for us is now praying for us.

## Application
Your confidence is not in the strength of your own prayers, but in the faithfulness of His. Even when you are weak, accused, tired, confused, or unable to find language, Jesus is not silent concerning you.

He is at the right hand of God, and He is interceding for you.
