## Commentary
“I will bless those who bless you, and I will curse him who curses you; and in you all the families of the earth shall be blessed.”

Paul calls this verse the gospel preached in advance: “the Scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the Gentiles by faith, preached the gospel to Abraham beforehand, saying, ‘In you all the nations shall be blessed’” (==Galatians 3:8==). The blessing promised here reaches every family of the earth, and it reaches them through Abraham's seed—ultimately through Christ (==Galatians 3:16==). That is why Genesis 12 cannot just be read as a Sunday-school story about a man named Abraham. Abraham's story is our story.

The New Testament says so directly. “Only those who are of faith are sons of Abraham” (==Galatians 3:7==). “If you are Christ's, then you are Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise” (==Galatians 3:29==)—the promise given right here. Believers are “children of promise,” as Isaac was (==Galatians 4:28==). Everyone who follows Jesus is counted among the families of the earth blessed in Abraham, and more than that, is brought into Abraham's family as a child of faith. To be a follower of Jesus is, in many ways, to walk the journey Abraham walked: called out of one life, believing a word without proof, living as a sojourner toward a land God will show, and becoming a blessing to the families of the earth. His call is our call; his faith is the pattern of ours; his promise is our inheritance.
