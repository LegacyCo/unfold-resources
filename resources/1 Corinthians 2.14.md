## Commentary
Paul mentions here how the "natural man" does not receive the things of the Spirit. The Greek word used here for natural is *psychikos*. It means that which is pertaining to the soul or physical life. It is in contrast to the spiritual life. This individual is ruled by what they can see, feel, and verify. Tangible evidence is this man's god.

Because God is so good, He still makes His graces like joy and peace and wisdom available to the world...as Jesus says in Matthew 5:45, "He causes the sun to rise on the evil and the good, and sends rain on the just and the unjust." But those things the world has access to are mere shadows of the treasures found in Him. The challenge for us as believers is to not settle for the shadow when our inheritance is the authentic. The authentic is only found in Him. Flesh and blood can't inherit those things, they are spiritual realities hidden in Christ for those who love Him, "the natural man can't receive the things of the Spirit." 

## Cross Reference
Jesus says in John 3:3, "Unless you're born again you can not see the kingdom of God."
