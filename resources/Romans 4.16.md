## Commentary
The use of the word *guaranteed* in this passage is deeply pastoral. Paul wants us to know that the promises of God are secure because their foundation is not our ability to keep ourselves qualified. The foundation is God's grace. God does not anchor His promises in human strength or ability. He anchors them in His own faithfulness.

This is why faith and grace are entwined together. Faith receives. Grace gives. Faith does not turn the promise into a wage. Faith keeps the promise in the realm of *gift*.

Paul is also widening the family of Abraham. The promise is guaranteed not only to those of the law, but also to those who share the faith of Abraham. Abraham is the father of all who believe because the family is formed by faith, not bloodline.
