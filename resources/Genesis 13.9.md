## Commentary
Abram was God's man, not Lot. He had every right to choose for himself where he and his family would settle. But instead, Abram give *Lot* the choice, "If you take the left, then I will go to the right..." This was an act of incredible humility and meekness. He preferred Lot in the moment, giving him the choice of the land, *and* he demonstrated trust in what the Lord had told him: "To your descendants I will give *this* land" (Genesis 12:7). He refused to take the situation into his own hands, even though he had every right to.
