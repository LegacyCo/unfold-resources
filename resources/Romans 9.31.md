## Commentary
Religion will always be offended at the idea of righteousness through faith. Righteousness that is obtained through faith destroys works. If righteousness through works is exposed as a dead end pursuit, then the influence and power of religion is taken away. So also is the satisfaction the flesh derives from attaining righteousness through it's own striving.
