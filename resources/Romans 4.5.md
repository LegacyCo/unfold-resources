## Commentary
This is one of the most shocking statements in Romans. Paul says God justifies the ungodly. He doesn't wait for the ungodly to become less ungodly. Justification is not the reward for personal transformation. It is the free gift of His that He gives to those who stop trying to save themselves and trust the righteousness of Christ.

## Word Study - Justification
The Greek word that we translate "justification" is *dikaiōsis*. It is directly connected to the word "righteous" (*dikaios*). Justification is God’s declaration that the guilty person is now righteous because their sin has been placed on Christ, and Christ’s righteousness has been counted to them.

Justification answers the question: How can a guilty person stand before a holy God and not be condemned?

The answer is: through Jesus.

Our sin is given to Him.  
His righteousness is given to us.

Justification is different from sanctification.

Justification is about your standing before God.  
Sanctification is about your ongoing transformation by God.

Justification happens by grace through faith. Sanctification unfolds as the Spirit forms the life of Christ in you.

So when God justifies you, He is not saying, “You are now morally perfect in every way.”

He is saying, “You are no longer condemned. You belong to Me. You stand before Me accepted, forgiven, and at peace."
