## Commentary
Jesus is the fulfillment of over 300 Old Testament prophecies. Here, we see one of them. Isaiah writes this in Isaiah 11:10 about the Messiah,

"In that day there shall be a Root of Jesse, who shall stand as a banner to the people; for the Gentiles shall seek Him..."

The wise men (or Magi) were most likely Zoroastrian priests from Persia who studied the stars. They were Gentiles. And something in their study of the heavens told them to make the journey to a distant land so they could worship a king. Jesus came to be the Savior of the world (see John 4:42).
