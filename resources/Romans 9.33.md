## Commentary
Jesus is both the foundation stone of the church (Luke 20:17) and the stone over which many in Israel will fall.

The word Paul uses here that we translate "stumbling stone" means offense, obstacle, an occasion for taking offense, and an occasion for making a false step. Paul references Isaiah, "whoever believes in Him will not be put to shame." This is similar language to David's word in Psalm 25:3, "Let no one who waits on you be ashamed."
