## Commentary
In v16, Eve’s pain in the labor of childbirth is greatly increased, and here we see in v17 that Adam will also experience pain in his labor. Yahweh addresses their specific area of labor and increases their toil in it. 

For her, the pain is in producing the family. 
For him, the pain is in providing for the family.

The result of Adam’s sin is that now thorns and thistles grow among the fruit bearing trees.

Jesus mentions thorns in His parable of the sower in Matthew 13. He says that if a seed is sown among the thorns, even if the seed is good, the thorns will choke it out and it will never produce fruit (Matthew 13:7). When Jesus later interprets this parable for His disciples, He says that the seed is His word, the soil is the hearts of men, and the *thorns* are 
	the anxieties of the world, 
	the deceitfulness of wealth (Matthew 13:22), 
	and the desire for other things (Mark 4:19) or the pleasures of this life
	(Luke 8:14). 
Translation: Adam’s thorns prevent us from receiving what the seed of God’s word wants to produce in us. 

Recall what was forced onto Jesus’s head as He hung on the cross.

“And the soldiers twisted a crown of thorns and put it on His head…” (John 19:2).

Jesus wore Adam’s thorns as a crown. Jesus took upon Himself your anxieties, the lies about wealth, and the desire for inferior things of this life…so that now, the soil of your heart can be made new. You can now receive the good seed of His word and watch fruit producing trees come to life where there was once thorns and thistles.

That crown of thorns on Jesus’s head wasn’t just something the Roman soldiers did to mock our King; it represents something powerful that Jesus did for you and me.
