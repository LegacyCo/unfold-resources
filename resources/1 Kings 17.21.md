## Commentary
Where are the people of God willing to stretch themselves out three times in prayer? On Carmel, he knelt and prayed for rain seven times (1 Kings 18:41-44). Where are the ones willing to kneel in the dust seven times before the cloud forms? We are so quick to pray once, maybe twice. Yes, Elijah was a man like us (James 5:17), but he prayed fervently...and *until*. The praying *until* brings the dead to life. The praying *until* breaks the back of drought and famine. The praying *until* exposes Ahab and Jezebel. The praying *until* defeats the prophets of Baal.
