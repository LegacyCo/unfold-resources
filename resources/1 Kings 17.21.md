## Commentary
Where are the people of God who are willing to stretch themselves out three times in prayer? Where are the ones willing to kneel in the dust seven times before the cloud forms (1 Kings 18:41-44)? We are so quick to pray once, maybe twice. Yes, Elijah was a man like us (James 5:17), but he prayed fervently...and *until*. The praying *until* brings the dead to life. The praying *until* breaks the back of drought and famine. The praying *until* exposes Ahab and Jezebel. The praying *until* defeats the prophets of Baal.
