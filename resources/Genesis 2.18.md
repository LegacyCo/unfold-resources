## Commentary
For the first time God says that something is "not good." It was not good for man to be alone. It would be easy to say that man was not alone, he was with God. But we see here that God has placed in man something that needs to be in relationship with others like him. To put it plainly, and in new covenant language, living a life of isolation from the body of Christ is not how God designed you. "It's just me and Jesus...that's all I need" is not ok. Even in the perfect unbroken fellowship of the garden it was not ok for man to be alone...even when he was alone with God. Isolation is not God's normal for human beings. Community is the creation of God.

-------

The woman was to be the man’s helper. She was going to help man fill the earth with God’s image, subdue it, rule over creation. This word “helper” is interesting because if there is any suggestion that the woman was designed to be inferior because she helps the man, consider that this word “helper" is the same word Scripture uses to describe Yahweh’s relationship with Israel.

"Israel, trust in the Lord, He is your helper" (Psalm 115:9).
"Our soul waits for the Lord, He is our helper and our shield" (Psalm 33:20).

This word “helper” carries with it a sense of strength and might.

So we can’t conclude that man’s helper would be inferior or less than man in any way. While her role would be different, she would be an indispensable companion for the man. Without her, Adam would not be able to do what God made him to do.

The last part of v18 literally translated would say,

"I will make for him a helper according to what is in front of him.” 

Adam’s counterpart would be in front of Adam…on his level. She was going to be the perfect compliment to the man. She fills in where he is lacking. She will complete him just as the north pole completes the south, just as the moon completes the sun, just as the land completes the sea, just as the earth completes the heavens. God created everything in creation to have a counterpart.

Their same-ness does not mean exact-ness. Man and woman were created to be different, and it’s in their differences coming together as one that they are able to demonstrate who God is to creation. For man or woman to throw off their identity as man or woman would be to tarnish the image of God that only they can carry.

## Application
Adam didn't know that it wasn’t good for him to be alone. Adam was probably just happy be invited to the party. But God saw something that Adam couldn’t see. This seems pretty obvious, but God knows what's good for us and what's not good for us. When we forget that, and we disagree with what God says is good or bad, it's an announcement that we are eating from the tree of knowledge of good and evil. 

Everything God does is love. Even His "no" is love. If you ever need to be reminded that even His “no, that’s not good” is an expression of His love, then just consider that Eve...the mother of creation, came as a result of "God's no.”

Let’s personalize this; What is God looking at in your life right now and saying, “No, this is not good." He is not saying that to condemn you…He is saying that because He loves you, He knows what’s best for you, and He wants to make that “not good” thing in your life “good”…and He will, if you’ll let Him. 

He's not going to put you to sleep and perform surgery on you to make it good. He's waiting for you to come into agreement with Him that this habit or this relationship or this mindset is not good. That’s called repentance; changing your mind so that what your thoughts comes into agreement with what God says. When you do that, and you ask Him to make your situation good, then He'll do what only He can do…He’ll cause all things to work together for good in your life. That’s a promise He makes. But listen, that promise is given in Romans 8:28 to those who love Him and are called according to HIS purpose…HIS plan. We have the opportunity in this life to demonstrate our love for God by walking in obedience to *His* plan for our life, not our plan. Take some time today and ask the Lord if there are any areas of your life that are not good that He wants to turn around for good. Then be brave enough to ask Him to do what needs to be done.
