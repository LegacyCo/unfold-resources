## Commentary
Daniel had been praying for twenty-one days. From his perspective, nothing was happening. There was no visible evidence suggesting that heaven had responded.

But the reality was a different story.

The angel says, “from the first day.” Not the twenty-first day. Not after he earned the response through fasting. From the first day he humbled himself before God, his words were heard, and the answer was released.

The delay was not because Daniel’s prayer failed to break through. The delay was because there was a conflict happening in the unseen realm that Daniel knew nothing about. While Daniel was waiting on earth, spiritual battles were being fought in the heavens.

Sometimes the answer to our prayer has already happened; we’re just not there yet. There are moments when heaven has already said yes, the response has already been sent, and the breakthrough is already in motion, but we are still living in the gap between answered prayer and breakthrough. And the only way we lose is if we quit.

## Application
Don’t measure God’s response by what you can see today. Your prayer may have been heard the moment it left your lips. Stay humble, stay faithful, and keep praying in the gap.
