## Commentary
This is from Apostolic Foundations by Art Katz: Egypt is the antithesis of the desert. Egypt is a picture of the world in its lush carnality and fleshly gratification. God is found at Mt. Horeb, the place of dryness, seeming disappointment and nothing that tantalizes the flesh. God is the God of humility, and it is only the revelation of God's humility that can save men out of Egypt. The rejected and visibly inferior place is alone calculated to destroy the thing that has impressive outward credentials. 

## Application
Don't despise the dry places God leads you into. The wilderness may feel like loss, delay, or obscurity, but it is often where God strips away the things that made us impressive in Egypt and teaches us to depend on Him alone.

We naturally want God to use our outward qualifications. But God often does His deepest work by removing the things we trusted in. He leads us away from what feeds the flesh so that He can form His humility within us.

The place that feels inferior may be the very place where God is preparing your deliverance. Horeb looked empty, but God was there. Moses looked rejected, but God had chosen him. The staff in his hand looked insignificant, but surrendered to God, it became an instrument of freedom.

Stop measuring your life by how impressive it appears. Humble yourself under the hand of God. Let Him lead you into the hidden places, remove your dependence on outward credentials, and teach you to carry His presence. What God forms in humility will eventually confront what the world built through pride.
