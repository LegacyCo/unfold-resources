## Commentary
Again, Luke purposely uses language from the Old Testament to draw our attention to God's intentionality and purpose in how He has dealt with His people. This is Deuteronomy 4:12, when Moses recalled for Israel how the Lord spoke to them from Mount Sinai (also called Horeb),

"And the LORD spoke to you out of the midst of the fire. You heard the sound of the words, but saw no form; you only heard a voice...Out of heaven He let you hear His voice, that He might instruct you; on earth He showed you His great fire, and you heard His words out of the midst of the fire" (Deuteronomy 4:12, 36).

## Cross Reference
See also Psalm 104:4, "He makes His angels spirits, His ministers a flame of fire." A literal translation for "spirits" is also *wind*.
