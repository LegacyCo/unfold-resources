## Commentary
Luke purposely uses similar language to 2 Chronicles 7:1 to draw our attention to the temple being filled with glory,

"When Solomon had finished praying, fire came down from heaven and consumed the burnt offering and the sacrifices; and the glory of the LORD filled the house."

Prayer...fire...came down...from heaven...filled the house.
