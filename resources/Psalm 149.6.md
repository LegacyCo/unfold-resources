## Commentary
Hebrews 4:12 tells us the word of God is sharper than any two-edged sword. Ephesians 6:17 tells us the word of God is the sword of the Spirit. Taken together, we can see the image the Psalmist is painting here: praise on our lips and God's word in our hands.
