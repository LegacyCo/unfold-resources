## Commentary
The covenant is always before God. What He does He does through the lens of covenant. He doesn't act impulsively. He remembers His covenant. That doesn't mean God forgets and then suddenly recalls it, but that His covenant is continually before Him as the settled basis of how He relates to His people. What He does, He does through the lens of His pledged faithfulness, His sworn commitment, and His unchanging word. His provision, His mercy, His discipline, His patience, and His promises all flow from covenant. 

## Application
This means that when our circumstances are confusing, we're not dealing with a distant or unpredictable God. We are dealing with a God who has bound Himself by promise. He sees us through what He has spoken, what He has established, and what He has sworn. Covenant stands behind His actions. Covenant shapes His dealings. Covenant is the fixed frame through which His faithfulness is revealed.
