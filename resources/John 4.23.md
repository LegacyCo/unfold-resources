## Commentary
Jesus says that the Father is seeking those who will worship Him in spirit and truth. The word translated “seeking” carries the thought of looking for, desiring, and pursuing. Consider that the eternal God, who lacks nothing within Himself, has chosen to look for something among men: hearts that will turn toward Him in worship.

This tells us something profound about the priority of God. We often approach Him preoccupied with what we need from Him, while He is seeking what He desires from us (==Hosea 6:6==). We want answers, direction, healing, provision, and usefulness—all important things. And the Father is seeking fellowship with worshipers.

Worship is more than music or a scheduled portion of a gathering. It is the offering of the whole person to God (==Romans 12:1==). To worship in spirit and truth is to come before Him without performance. It is authentic. True. It’s the offering of an internal reality of a life that truly belongs to Him.

The first question of the priestly life is therefore not, “What can I accomplish for God?” It is, “What is the Father seeking that only I can give Him?” The one who understands His desire will not be satisfied performing religious duties. He will become an answer to the longing of God by living before Him in continual worship (see ==Psalm 27:4==).
