## Commentary
"God takes into account the source of things, not just whether they are good or evil. Men ask 'is this good or bad.' But God asks 'where does it come from?' Men ask 'do you have love or zeal?' But God asks 'what is the origin of your love/zeal? No matter how good something is it will not achieve God's eternal purpose it it issues from ourselves." -Watchman Nee

Herod had heavily invested in the temple and turned it into a structure more visibly impressive than the temple Solomon had built. The temple was the crown of Jerusalem and filled with activity, but the fire that fell on Solomon's temple never fell on Herod's temple. The source of what made the temple holy in Solomon's day, God's presence, was absent in Herod's day. 

## Application
It is possible to do the right thing from the wrong source. A person can have zeal, love, generosity, boldness, even ministry fruit—and yet still be moving out of self rather than out of God. God is after more than good behavior; He is after a life that flows from union with Him. What comes from the flesh may appear right, but it cannot carry the life, weight, or enduring fruit of the Spirit.
