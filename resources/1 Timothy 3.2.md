## Word Study
The Greek word that we translate "blameless" or "above reproach" is anepílēmptos. It means unable to seize or grasp. It gives the picture of something that is impossible to trap or grab hold of. In the same way that Jesus said satan has no power, claim, or foothold in him (John 14:30), so must the overseers of God's house.
