## Commentary
“Submit therefore to God. Resist the devil and he will flee from you.”

Look at the sequence, because the order is vital. What comes first? Not resisting the devil. *Submitting to God.* The Greek word is *hupotasso*—to come under, to obey. In the kingdom, step one is always Jesus: seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things will be added to you (==Matthew 6:33==).

That order is where spiritual warfare is won or lost. Resistance without submission is just noise—the sons of Sceva tried it and ran out of the house bleeding (==Acts 19:15-16==). But come under the covering of the conquering King, and the power to resist is drawn from alignment with the One who already won. When we live in obedience to God’s will and way, we resist the enemy from a position of victory, not toward one. Submitting your life to God allows God to be God in your situation.

Then comes step three: “Draw near to God and He will draw near to you” (==James 4:8==). That’s presence language. My kids are safe when they’re at my side—it’s when they wander off on their own that danger lurks.

## Application
How do you draw near? The fundamentals. Worship. Prayer. Get His word in you. Take communion. Pull away from the things of the world and pull into Jesus—turn off the trashy movie and turn on worship, close your mouth when you want to gossip and speak life instead. If you’ve made agreements with death—the “I’ll never be free,” the “this is just how it always is”—break them off out loud in Jesus’ name. Live above reproach, and give the enemy no legal ground to accuse you.

None of that earns the victory. It positions you to receive the victory Jesus already won. Submit. Resist. Draw near. In that order.
