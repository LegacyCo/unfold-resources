## Commentary
The psalmist understands that God’s presence shining on His people is never meant to end with His people. It's also intended to be a public witness.

When God’s presence rests on a people, His ways are supposed to become visible through them. His mercy should make them merciful. His righteousness becomes their righteousness. His peace should mark them. His salvation is intended to become an announcement to the nations.

In short: God blesses His people so the world can see what He is like.
