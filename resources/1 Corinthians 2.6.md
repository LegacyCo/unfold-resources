## Commentary
Paul makes the connection here between maturity and being a people of the Spirit. The Holy Spirit is the One who brings us into maturity. It is not gathering more of the wisdom of this age (which is coming to nothing - v6), it's not even life experiences...because experience without the Spirit is still the wisdom of this age. The Holy Spirit has been given to us, as children of God, to lead us into "all truth" (John 16:13). Among those who have been brought into maturity by the Spirit, Paul can confidently say that he can "speak wisdom among them."
