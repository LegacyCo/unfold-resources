## Commentary
“Now I saw heaven opened, and behold, a white horse. And He who sat on him was called Faithful and True, and in righteousness He judges and makes war.” His eyes are like a flame of fire, on His head are many crowns, and out of His mouth goes a sharp sword (==Revelation 19:12==; ==Revelation 19:15==).

Living under the rule of this King changes how His people talk about trouble.

“Well, how am I supposed to feel when I’m under attack?” Are you kidding? Have you seen our King? He’s got a sword in His mouth and fire in His eyes. He speaks and mountains shake. He’s undefeated.

“What about when there’s famine and lack?” Have you seen what our King can do with five loaves of bread? (==Matthew 14:19-21==)

“What about when sickness invades our home?” He Himself bore our sicknesses and carried our pains (==Matthew 8:17==).

“What about when loved ones die?” Our King stood at a tomb and called a dead man out by name—He *is* the resurrection and the life (==John 11:25==).

“What about when bad things happen to good people?” He causes all things to work together for good to those who love Him (==Romans 8:28==). Even the bad things, He turns.

This isn’t denial—it’s allegiance. Fear does its math using only what it can see. Faith does its math with the King in the equation. So before you rehearse the size of what’s coming against you, rehearse the Rider on the white horse. Every “what about” has the same answer: have you seen our King?
