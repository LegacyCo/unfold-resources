## Commentary
Moses knew he had found favor with God. But instead of using that favor to ask for comfort, ease, influence, or an easier assignment, he asked for something more eternal: “Show me Your ways, that I may know You.”

That is a monumental prayer.

Moses understood that experiencing the acts of God was not the same as knowing the ways of God. Israel had seen miracles and still misunderstood Him. Moses wanted more than outcomes. He wanted understanding. 

“Show me Your ways” is the prayer of someone who wants to know God, not use God.
