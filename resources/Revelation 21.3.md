## Commentary
The whole story of Scripture is moving toward this moment.

God has always desired a sanctuary on earth for Himself. Eden was the beginning of that purpose—a place where God and humanity could dwell together, where man would bear His image and live in His presence. The tabernacle and Temple were not separate ideas. They were signs pointing back to Eden and forward to the day when God’s dwelling would fill all creation.

This is why the Bible does not end with humanity escaping earth and going somewhere else. It ends with God coming to dwell with humanity. Heaven and earth are brought together. The distance is removed. The sanctuary is no longer confined to a garden, a tent, or a building. God makes His home with His people forever.

This has always been His desire, and because we were made for Him, it is also our deepest destiny. Creation was made by Him, through Him, and for Him (==Colossians 1:16==). The final glory of redemption is not simply that we are rescued, but that God finally has the home He has always desired among the people He created for His presence.
