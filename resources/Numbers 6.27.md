## Commentary
This final line explains what the priestly blessing actually accomplished. The priests were not just offering encouragement to God's people. They were placing the name of Yahweh upon Israel. In Scripture, a name represents identity, ownership, character, and covenant relationship. For God to place His name upon His people means they belong to Him and are marked by Him.

This also means blessing is not mainly about receiving benefits _from_ God. Blessing begins with belonging _to_ God. Israel’s security was not found in the strength of their army, the fertility of their land, or the wisdom of their leaders. Their security was that the Lord had claimed them as His own.

For us who now live on the other side of the cross, we may recognize this language in the context of baptism. Believers are baptized into the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Redemption means God has marked us as His own forever.
