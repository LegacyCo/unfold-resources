## Commentary
The most direct temptation for Jesus was to serve the Father out of His own ability to turn the stone into bread. He was hungry, and everyone knows that the first law of life is self-preservation — survival. The reason that the powers of darkness have such sway over us is because this temptation has not been recognized or met. Both bodily survival and personal security (such as financial security) are deeply rooted in human nature. Are we still enslaved to it and tempted to make our bread out of our own sufficiency? Have we experienced the power of this thing broken, and can therefore take the spoiling of our goods with joy? If we are defeated right here, the devil can go home, and there is no need for the further temptations of the pride of life and the lust of the eyes. It is only after we graduate from this temptation that we can be candidates for the next! There are three temptations, but the first is absolutely fundamental. How many of us are incapacitated from serving the Lord in true worship because our first and predominant issue is our own survival and the perpetuation of our own life and bodily well-being? 

-taken from *The Temptations of Christ*, by Art Katz
