## Commentary
Nowhere on this side of the cross do we find license to engage the culture, politics, government leaders, or those who do not see our viewpoint with a combative spirit. In fact, we are taught the opposite; "Pray for all men, for kings, and all who are in authority." Why? "So that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life." So that the gospel may go forth. This is good in the sight of God who desires all to be saved. Our prayers for peace result in the salvation of the lost, not our cries for rebellion against Babylon or Nero or Hitler or the other political party, for that matter.

## Cross Reference
Paul uses similar language to the Thessalonians; "Earnestly aspire to lead a quiet life, mind your own business, work with your hands that you may live properly with those who are outside" (1 Thessalonians 4:11-12).
