## Commentary
“And see to it that you make them according to the pattern which was shown you on the mountain.”

The pattern was *shown* to Moses—he did not draw it up. That distinction runs through everything God builds: just because you have an idea does not mean you have vision. An idea can be discovered; vision must be revealed. An idea can be shaken by circumstances, closed doors, and lack of resources; vision overcomes, because heaven endorses it. The lifespan of an idea depends on the lifespan of the one who birthed it; God-breathed vision is eternal. An idea makes logical sense; vision is often impossible. Vision is marching around Jericho for six days in silence (==Joshua 6:3-4==). Vision is reaching into a fish's mouth for a coin (==Matthew 17:27==). Vision is building an ark far from any water (==Genesis 6:14==). Vision is living in such a way that if God is not who He says He is, you fall flat on your face.

What is missing in the harvest today is not an abundance of eager laborers, good ideas, or resources. It is men and women who carry God-breathed vision received in the place of encounter—who refuse to build anything that did not originate on the mountain. The best the wisdom of this world produced while Moses was on the mountain was a golden calf (==Exodus 32:4==). Without revealed vision, a compassionate believer sees a need, adopts it as a calling, and asks God to bless it—but what we do must originate from Him, not from the circumstances around us. The world's greatest need is not more laborers moving forward with good ideas but lovers who are pregnant with vision, able to build according to what was shown to them on the mountain.

## Word Study
**Pattern** is the Hebrew *tabnit* (תַּבְנִית)—model, plan, structure; the form of a thing given to be copied. David used the same word for the temple: he handed Solomon “the plans (*tabnit*) for the vestibule” and said, “All this… the LORD made me understand in writing, by His hand upon me” (==1 Chronicles 28:11==; ==1 Chronicles 28:19==). Summary: in Scripture the *tabnit* for God's house is never invented, brainstormed, or borrowed—it is received from His hand, and the builder's job is fidelity to what was shown.
