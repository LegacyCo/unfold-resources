## Commentary
This was a pivotal encounter for Jacob. The Lord tells him, "I am with you...and I will not leave you" — that's presence language. Jacob wakes up and realizes the Lord was in that special location and he didn't even realize it. He makes a vow; if the God of THIS place will go with me and complete my journey to my father's house with His shalom, then He shall be my God. Jacob realizes the condition of shalom, peace, is the presence of God.
