## Commentary
This is from *Humility* by Andrew Murray: Only by the indwelling of Christ in His divine humility do we become truly humble. We have our pride from another, from Adam; we must have our humility from Another, too. Pride is ours, and it rules in us with such terrible power, because it is our self, our very nature. Humility must be ours in the same way; it must be our very self, our very nature. As natural and easy as it has been to be proud, it must be-it will be-to be humble. The promise is, "Where," even in the heart, "sin abounded, grace did much more abound" (==Romans 5:20==). All Christ's teaching to His disciples, and all their futile efforts, were the necessary preparation for His entering into them in divine power-to give and be in them what He had taught them to desire. 

## Application
Pride belongs to the old nature inherited from Adam, but humility belongs to the new life we have received in Christ. James says that God gives more grace because humility is not something we manufacture; it is something grace produces in us. The answer to pride is not trying harder to think less of yourself. It is allowing Christ to live more fully within you

So when pride is exposed, don't attempt to defeat it through willpower. Submit that place to God. Ask Christ to form His own humility in you. Where pride has ruled in your life, in Christ, grace can rule all the more.
