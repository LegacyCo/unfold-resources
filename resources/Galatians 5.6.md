## Commentary
The Greek word for "working" means: to effect something, to create, to produce. The point is this: Love creates faith.

We already know from James 2:26 that faith cannot stand on its own, that "faith without works is dead." So we see the nature of faith being dependent on something outside of itself to be alive. Could it be that faith fails to bring about its intended result because its missing the connection to love which is designed by God to be the catalyst that activates faith.

In chemistry a catalyst is an ingredient thats added to turn something that is currently passive into something that is explosive. We understand that faith without works is dead, but do we understand that faith without love is dead, too?
