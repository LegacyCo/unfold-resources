## Voice

The authoritative source is `ABOUT ME/anti-ai-writing-style.md` in the Cowork
folder. If that file is reachable, it wins over anything written here.

Until it is wired in, the working rules are:

- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Concrete nouns over abstractions.
- No hype adjectives, no "unlock", "elevate", "transform your".
- No three-item lists used as rhythm.
- No "It's not X. It's Y." as a closer.
- No em-dash as a default connector.
- No opening with a rhetorical question.
- Write like a person who has done the work, to a person who is in it.
